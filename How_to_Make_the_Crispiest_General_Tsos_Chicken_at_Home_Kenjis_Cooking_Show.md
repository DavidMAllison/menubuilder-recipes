# How to Make the Crispiest General Tso's Chicken at Home | Kenji's Cooking Show

## Ingredients

- 2 lbs boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 1 egg white
- 2 tablespoons shaoxing wine
- 2 tablespoons dark soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons vodka
- 3 tablespoons cornstarch
- 1/4 teaspoon baking soda
- 1.5 tablespoons cornstarch (additional)

## Instructions

1. Cut boneless, skinless chicken thighs into bite-sized chunks
2. Whisk egg white until homogeneous and slightly frothy
3. Add shaoxing wine to egg white and whisk
4. Add dark soy sauce to mixture and whisk
5. Add vodka to mixture and whisk
6. Add 3 tablespoons cornstarch and 1/4 teaspoon baking soda to mixture and whisk until combined
7. Divide marinade in half and set half aside
8. Add an additional 1.5 tablespoons cornstarch to the reserved half of marinade to create a thin batter consistency similar to crepe batter
