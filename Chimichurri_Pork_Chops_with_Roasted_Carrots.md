# Chimichurri Pork Chops with Roasted Carrots

## Notes

Claude-original protein + veg (pork chops, roasted carrots); chimichurri swapped Oct 7 2026 to the authentic Uruguayan/Argentine version adapted from Cafe Delites, in place of the original Claude-devised sauce.

## Ingredients

**Pork Chops:**

- 4 (6 oz each) boneless pork chops
- to taste kosher salt and black pepper
- 1 tbsp olive oil

**Roasted Carrots:**

- 1 lb carrots, peeled and halved lengthwise
- 1 tbsp olive oil

**Chimichurri:**

- 1/2 cup fresh parsley, finely chopped
- 3-4 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 2 small red chilies (or 1 chili, deseeded), finely chopped
- 3/4 tsp dried oregano
- 1/2 cup olive oil
- 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 tsp coarse salt
- 1/2 tsp pepper

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 425 degrees F. Toss carrots with 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, and pepper on a sheet pan. Roast 20-25 minutes, tossing halfway, until tender and lightly caramelized.
2. Meanwhile, make the chimichurri: finely chop the parsley, garlic, and chilies. Combine with the dried oregano, 1/2 cup olive oil, red wine vinegar, coarse salt, and pepper in a bowl. Let sit at least 10 minutes (longer if time allows) to let the flavors meld -- this is a loose, oil-based condiment, not a puree.
3. Season pork chops with salt and pepper. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Sear chops 4-5 minutes per side until golden and internal temp reaches 145 degrees F. Let rest 5 minutes.
4. Serve pork chops plain for the kids; top adult portions with chimichurri. Serve with roasted carrots.
