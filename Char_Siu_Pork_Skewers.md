# Char Siu Pork Skewers

## Ingredients

- 1 1/4 pounds boneless pork shoulder/butt ((see Note))
- Fine sea salt
- Scant 1/4 tsp Chinese five-spice powder
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 2 teaspoons soy sauce
- 1 1/2 tablespoons honey ((amber colored preferred))
- 2 teaspoons ketchup
- 1 to 1 1/2 tablespoons hoisin sauce ((use maximum for bold flavor; see Note))
- White or black pepper
- 1 tablespoon neutral oil ((such as canola or peanut; optional, plus more as needed for brushing))

## Instructions

1. Before beginning, if bamboo skewers are your choice, allow 6 skewers measuring 10 inches in length to hydrate in water for no less than 30 minutes.

2. As you prepare, slice your pork into strips with dimensions of approximately 1 inch in width, 2 to 3 inches in length, and 1/4 inch in thickness. Transfer the strips to a medium-sized bowl.

3. Whisk together 1/4 teaspoon salt, five spice, garlic, soy sauce, honey, ketchup, and hoisin sauce in a small bowl. Sample the mixture to verify the flavors are well-balanced among savory, spicy, and sweet elements. Fine-tune the seasoning as needed by incorporating additional soy sauce, honey, ketchup, or hoisin sauce in 1/2 teaspoon increments. Consider whether to include an extra pinch of five spice or garlic powder. Should a tangy element be desired, incorporate 2 or 3 grinds of pepper. To enhance richness, incorporate the 1 tablespoon of oil into your seasoning blend.

4. Distribute the seasoning mixture over the pork. Using either stirring or preferably hand massage, ensure each pork strip receives an even coating. Load the pork onto the skewers and apply a light brush of oil across the surface.

5. Bring your grill to medium-high heat or ignite charcoal to the same temperature. (Alternatively, position a cast-iron grill pan on your stovetop over medium-high heat.) Apply a light coat of oil to the cooking surface. Once adequately heated, position the pork on the grill or pan and cook for 8 to 12 minutes, rotating regularly, until the surface develops light charring and the meat reaches complete doneness. (Create a small cut in a piece to confirm it has cooked through.) Move the skewers onto a serving plate and present while hot.
