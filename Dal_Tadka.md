# Dal Tadka

**Time:** 40 minutes (10 min prep, 30 min cook)
**Servings:** 4

## Ingredients

### Lentils
- ¾ cup toor dal
- ¼ cup moong dal
- 2 tablespoons chana dal
- 3 cups water

### Masala Base
- 1½ tablespoons oil
- ½ teaspoon cumin seeds
- 1 teaspoon garlic, finely chopped
- 1 teaspoon ginger, peeled and chopped
- ⅓ cup onion, finely chopped
- 1 green chili, chopped (optional — skip for kids)
- 1 cup tomatoes, deseeded and chopped
- ¼ teaspoon turmeric
- ½ teaspoon red chili powder
- ½ teaspoon garam masala
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 tablespoon kasuri methi (dried fenugreek)
- 1–2 tablespoons fresh coriander, chopped
- 1 lemon, for serving

### Tadka (Tempering)
- 2 tablespoons ghee
- ½ teaspoon cumin seeds
- 2 dried red chilies
- 1 tablespoon garlic, finely chopped
- ⅛ teaspoon hing (asafoetida)
- ⅓ teaspoon red chili powder

## Instructions

1. Rinse lentils thoroughly until water runs clear. Pressure cook with 3 cups water for 3–4 whistles (or 10 minutes high pressure in Instant Pot). Allow pressure to drop naturally. Lightly mash, keeping some texture.
2. Heat oil in a pan. Add cumin seeds until they splutter. Add garlic and ginger; fry until fragrant. Add onion (and green chili if using); fry until translucent.
3. Reduce heat. Add turmeric, chili powder, and garam masala; stir quickly. Add tomatoes and salt; sauté until softened.
4. Transfer masala to lentils. Simmer together 6–7 minutes. Crush kasuri methi between palms and stir in. Add coriander and turn off heat.
5. Make tadka: melt ghee in a small pan over medium heat. Add cumin seeds until they crackle. Add dried chilies and garlic; fry until light golden. Add hing and chili powder. Immediately remove from heat and pour over dal.
6. Garnish with fresh coriander. Serve with rice, naan, or roti with lemon on the side.

## Notes

- Kids: set aside a plain portion of dal before the tadka — it's mild without the tempering.
- Dal thickens as it cools; add a splash of water when reheating.
- Can use toor dal only if you don't have all three lentils.

## Ingredient Substitutions

- **Toor/moong/chana dal** — red lentils work fine as a single substitute; available at most grocery stores. Texture will be softer and slightly less complex.
- **Kasuri methi (dried fenugreek)** — skip it or add a small pinch of fresh thyme for a faint herbal note. Not a perfect sub but won't ruin the dish.
- **Hing (asafoetida)** — optional, just leave it out. Adds a faint onion-garlic depth but the dish works without it.
- **Ghee** — substitute butter for the tadka. Not traditional but close enough.
- **Find the real thing**: Indian grocery stores carry all of these. Toor dal, kasuri methi, and hing are also available on Amazon.
