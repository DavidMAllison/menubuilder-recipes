# Yucatecan Black Bean Dinner

## Ingredients

- 1 pound (about 2 1/2 cups) dry black beans, picked over to remove any stones or debris
- 1 1/2 to 2 pounds boneless pork shoulder, trimmed of extraneous fat and cut into 2-inch cubes
- Salt
- About 3 tablespoons fresh pork lard or vegetable oil
- 2 medium white onions, thinly sliced (divided use)
- 2 14.5-ounce cans of diced tomatoes in juice (preferably fire-roasted), undrained
- 1 to 2 habanero chiles, stemmed
- 1 1/2 cups rice, preferably medium-grain
- 6 garlic cloves, peeled and finely chopped
- 1 large avocado, peeled, pitted and sliced for garnish
- sprigs of cilantro, for garnish
- 3 limes, quartered, for garnish
- About 1 cup Xnipec salsa (optional) (recipe below)

## Instructions

1. When ready to serve, reheat the tomato sauce and remove the habanero chiles. Distribute the beans evenly among six small individual bowls by ladling. Spread the rice across 6 large warmed dinner plates and set the pork in the center of each one. Drizzle a modest portion of the warmed sauce to one side of every plate. Arrange several thin avocado slices on the opposite side. Top with fresh cilantro sprigs. Serve at once, with lime wedges and chopped xnipec salsa available on the side if preferred.

2. For approximately one cup of xnipec salsa, gather:

3. 1/2 small red onion, minced; 1 tablespoon fresh lime or sour orange juice; 6 radishes, cut into small pieces or thin strips; 1/2 fresh habanero chile, removed of stem and seeds, then minced; approximately a dozen large cilantro sprigs, chopped; and salt to taste

4. Place the onion in a colander and rinse under cold running water. Drain thoroughly by shaking well, then transfer to a small mixing bowl and stir in the juice. Add all other ingredients together and season to taste with salt, typically about 1/2 teaspoon, and the salsa is prepared to eat.
