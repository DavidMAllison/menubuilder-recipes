# Honey Garlic Chicken

Time: 35 minutes
Yield: 4 servings

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs boneless, skinless chicken thighs or breasts, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/3 cup honey
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon rice vinegar or apple cider vinegar
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 2 tablespoons water
- 1/2 teaspoon sesame oil (optional)
- Sesame seeds and sliced green onions for garnish (optional)

## Instructions

1. Pat chicken dry with paper towels. Season with salt and pepper.
2. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add chicken and cook 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until golden brown and cooked through (165°F). For kid-friendly layered cooking, remove 1/3 of the plain cooked chicken to a separate plate and set aside.
3. Make sauce: In a small bowl, whisk together honey, soy sauce, and vinegar. In another small bowl, mix cornstarch with water to make a slurry.
4. Reduce heat to medium. Add garlic to the pan with remaining chicken; cook 30 seconds until fragrant.
5. Pour honey-soy mixture into the pan. Bring to a simmer. Add cornstarch slurry and stir constantly 1-2 minutes until sauce thickens and becomes glossy.
6. Remove from heat. Drizzle with sesame oil if using. Toss to coat. Garnish with sesame seeds and green onions if desired.
7. Plate glazed chicken for adults; serve reserved plain chicken to younger children. Serve with rice and steamed vegetables.
