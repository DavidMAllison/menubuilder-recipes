# Homemade Pizza Rolls  Cook Time: 18 minutes 

## Ingredients

- For the Dough:
- 2 1/2 cups (315g) all-purpose flour
- 1 packet (2 1/4 teaspoons) instant yeast
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 3/4 cup (180ml) warm water (110 degrees F / 43 degrees C)
- 2 tablespoons olive oil For the Filling:
- 1 cup pizza sauce or marinara sauce
- 2 cups (8 oz) shredded mozzarella cheese
- 1/2 cup mini pepperoni slices (or diced regular pepperoni)
- 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning Optional: diced bell peppers, cooked sausage, mushrooms, olives For Assembly:
- 1 egg, beaten (for egg wash)
- 2 tablespoons butter, melted
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder Extra marinara sauce for dipping

2. Let Dough Rise: Place dough in a greased bowl, cover with a damp towel, and let
3. Prepare Filling: In a bowl, combine mozzarella, Parmesan, pepperoni, and Italian seasoning. Mix well and set aside. Have pizza sauce ready.
4. Roll Out Dough: Punch down dough and roll on a floured surface into a large rectangle, approximately 12x18 inches and about 1/4 inch thick.
5. Cut Squares: Cut the dough into 24 squares (4 rows x 6 columns), each about 3x3 inches. Re-roll scraps if needed to get 24 squares.
6. Fill Pizza Rolls: Place each square on your work surface. Add 1 teaspoon sauce to the center (don't overfill!). Top with 1-2 tablespoons cheese mixture. Be careful not to get filling near the edges.
7. Seal Pizza Rolls: Brush edges with egg wash. Fold dough over filling to create a rectangle or triangle. Press edges firmly with a fork to seal completely. Make sure there are no gaps or filling will leak out.
8. Prepare for Baking: Place sealed pizza rolls on a parchment-lined baking sheet, seam side down. Brush tops with egg wash. Cut 2-3 small slits in each roll to allow steam to escape.
9. Bake: Preheat oven to 400 degrees F (200 degrees C). Bake for 15-18 10. Finish: Mix melted butter with garlic powder. Brush over hot pizza rolls. Tips - Don't overfill or they will burst during baking - Seal edges very well to prevent leaking - The steam vents are important - don't skip them - These freeze well - freeze before baking, then bake from frozen - Let cool slightly before eating - filling is extremely hot! Serving Suggestions Serve with warm marinara sauce for dipping. Perfect for game day, kids' parties, or as an after-school snack. Also great with ranch dressing or garlic butter for dipping.
