# Slow-Cooker Moroccan-Spiced Chicken and Chickpea Tagine

## Ingredients

- 2 lbs boneless, skinless chicken thighs, trimmed
- 1 (15-ounce) can chickpeas, rinsed and drained
- 3 carrots, peeled and sliced into coins
- 1 small yellow onion, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/2 cup dried apricots, quartered
- 1 1/2 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1 1/2 teaspoons ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon ground ginger
- 1/2 teaspoon ground coriander
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- Salt, to taste
- 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro or parsley, for serving
- Cooked couscous, for serving
- Harissa, for serving (adults)

## Instructions

1. In the slow cooker, whisk together broth, tomato paste, cumin, cinnamon, ginger, coriander, and pepper.
2. Add chicken thighs, chickpeas, carrots, onion, garlic, and dried apricots. Stir gently to coat everything in the spiced broth. Season lightly with salt.
3. Cover and cook on low for 6 hours, or until chicken is very tender and carrots are soft.
4. Taste and adjust salt if needed. For kids: serve plain chicken and carrots over plain couscous, no sauce. For adults: spoon the full tagine with sauce over couscous, topped with cilantro and a spoonful of harissa on the side.
