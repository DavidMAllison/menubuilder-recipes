# Enfrijoladas

## Ingredients

- 1 cup uncooked pinto, Peruvian, or black beans, cleaned, OR 3 cups precooked beans
- 1/4 white onion
- 1 garlic clove
- 4 New Mexico peppers
- 2 morita peppers or chipotle peppers in adobo sauce
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
- 3 tablespoons vegetable oil, divided
- 25 corn tortillas
- 2 pounds (907 g) queso fresco, crumbled
- 1/2 red onion, chopped
- For serving: crema Mexicana, crumbled queso fresco, Pico de Gallo, avocado slices

## Before You Begin

- These are bean sauce enchiladas. With leftover precooked beans, done in under 15 minutes.
- A pressure cooker is used for dried beans; skip step 1 if using precooked.

## Instructions

1. In a pressure cooker, combine beans, white onion, and garlic. Close lid, seal, and use bean setting for 45 minutes on high. Let sit 20 minutes, release steam, and open.
2. In a medium skillet over medium heat, toast New Mexico and morita (or chipotle) peppers 30 seconds until they puff. Stir peppers into beans; let rest 5 minutes.
3. In a blender, combine beans, peppers, salt, black pepper, and oregano. Blend on high until smooth. (If using precooked beans, add white onion, garlic, beans, and toasted peppers to blender.)
4. In a large frying pan over medium heat, heat 1 tablespoon oil. Add bean sauce, bring to a simmer, adjust salt, and simmer 5 minutes until thickened.
5. On a griddle over medium heat, heat remaining 2 tablespoons oil. Lightly fry each tortilla about 10 seconds per side.
6. In a small bowl, combine queso fresco and red onion.
7. Dip each fried tortilla in bean sauce, stuff with 2 tablespoons cheese mixture, and roll into a flute. Repeat with remaining tortillas.
8. Spoon bean sauce onto each plate, place three enfrijoladas on top, and cover with more sauce. Top with crema Mexicana, queso fresco, Pico de Gallo, and avocado slices. Serve.
