# Spaghetti with Lemon and Olive Oil (al Limone)

## Ingredients

- Table salt
- 1 pound spaghetti
- 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil, plus more for serving
- 1 medium shallot, minced (about 3 tablespoons)
- 1/4 cup heavy cream
- 2 teaspoons finely grated lemon zest plus 1/4 cup juice (from 3 lemons)
- 1 ounce finely grated Parmesan cheese (about 1/2 cup), plus more for serving
- Ground black pepper
- 2 tablespoons shredded fresh basil leaves

## Before You Begin

- Let the dish rest briefly before serving so the flavors develop and the sauce thickens.

## Instructions

1. Bring 4 quarts water to boil in a large Dutch oven. Add 1 tablespoon salt and pasta; cook, stirring frequently, until al dente. Reserve 1 3/4 cups cooking water, then drain pasta into a colander and set aside.
2. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in the now-empty Dutch oven over medium heat until shimmering. Add shallot and 1/2 teaspoon salt; cook until softened, about 2 minutes. Whisk in 1 1/2 cups reserved pasta water and cream; bring to a simmer and cook 2 minutes. Remove from heat, return pasta, and stir until coated. Stir in remaining 3 tablespoons oil, lemon zest, lemon juice, cheese, and 1/2 teaspoon pepper.
3. Cover and let pasta stand 2 minutes, tossing frequently and adjusting consistency with remaining 1/4 cup pasta water if needed. Stir in basil; season with salt and pepper to taste. Serve drizzled with olive oil and sprinkled with additional cheese.
