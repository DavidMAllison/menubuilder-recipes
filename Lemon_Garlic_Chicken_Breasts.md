# Lemon Garlic Chicken Breasts

## Ingredients

- 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (about 6-8 oz each)
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/2 cup low-sodium chicken broth
- 3 tbsp fresh lemon juice (about 1 lemon)
- 1 tsp lemon zest
- 1 tbsp butter
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped

## Instructions

1. Season chicken: Pat chicken breasts dry with paper towels. In a small bowl, combine salt, pepper, garlic powder, and smoked paprika. Season both sides of each breast evenly.

2. Sear the chicken: Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add chicken and cook without moving for 5-6 minutes until golden brown on the bottom.

3. Flip and finish: Flip chicken and reduce heat to medium. Cook another 5-7 minutes until internal temperature reaches 165F. Transfer to a plate and tent loosely with foil to rest.

4. Make the pan sauce: In the same skillet, add minced garlic and cook 30 seconds until fragrant. Add chicken broth and lemon juice, scraping up browned bits. Simmer 2-3 minutes until slightly reduced.

5. Finish the sauce: Remove from heat and stir in butter and lemon zest until butter is melted and sauce is glossy.

6. Serve: Return chicken to the skillet and spoon sauce over it. Garnish with fresh parsley and serve immediately.

## Notes

- For kids: Set aside plain seared chicken before making the lemon-garlic sauce
- Pound thicker breasts to even thickness for more consistent cooking
- Let chicken rest 5 minutes before slicing to keep it juicy
- Can substitute 1 tbsp dried parsley if fresh is unavailable
