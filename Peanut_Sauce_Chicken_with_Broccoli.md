# Peanut Sauce Chicken with Broccoli

## Notes

Claude-original flavor combo (David's protein + sauce + veg formula), not yet cooked by the family. Add feedback here after the first try.

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs boneless skinless chicken breast
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- to taste kosher salt and black pepper
- 1/3 cup creamy peanut butter
- 3 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 2 tbsp lime juice
- 1 tbsp honey
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
- 1 lb broccoli florets

**Optional / For Serving:**

- 1 tsp sriracha
- 2 tbsp scallions, chopped

## Instructions

1. Whisk together peanut butter, soy sauce, lime juice, honey, garlic, and ginger in a small bowl. Add warm water 1 tbsp at a time until pourable. Set aside.
2. Season chicken with salt and pepper. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook chicken in a single layer, 3-4 minutes per side, until golden and cooked through (165 degrees F). Transfer to a plate.
3. Meanwhile, add broccoli florets and 1 tbsp water to a covered microwave-safe bowl; microwave 3-4 minutes until bright green and crisp-tender (or steam on the stovetop).
4. Pull out plain chicken and broccoli for the kids before saucing.
5. Return chicken to the skillet, pour in the peanut sauce, and toss to coat, 1-2 minutes until warmed through. Serve over broccoli, with sriracha on the side for adults.
