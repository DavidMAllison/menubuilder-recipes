# Homemade Pizza from Scratch  Cook Time: 15 minutes per pizza 

**Adapted from**: [Serious Eats](https://www.seriouseats.com/foolproof-pan-pizza-recipe)

## Ingredients

- 4 cups (500g) all-purpose flour or bread flour
- 1 packet (2 1/4 teaspoons) active dry yeast
- 1 1/2 cups (360ml) warm water (110 degrees F / 43 degrees C)
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 1 teaspoon sugar For the Pizza Sauce:
- 1 can (15 oz) tomato sauce or crushed tomatoes
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon dried basil
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- Pinch of sugar For Toppings (customize as desired):
- 2 cups (8 oz) shredded mozzarella cheese
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- Pepperoni, mushrooms, bell peppers, onions, olives, etc.
- Fresh basil leaves (optional)

## Instructions

1. Make the Dough: In a small bowl, dissolve yeast and sugar in warm water. Let stand salt. Add yeast mixture and olive oil. Mix until a rough dough forms.
2. Knead the Dough: smooth and elastic. Add more flour if dough is too sticky.
3. First Rise: Place dough in a greased bowl, turning to coat. Cover with a damp towel or plastic wrap. Let rise in a warm place for 1-1.5 hours until doubled in size.

6. Shape the Dough: Punch down dough and divide in half. On a floured surface, stretch or roll each piece into a 12-14 inch circle. Create a slightly thicker edge for the crust.
7. Assemble Pizza: Transfer dough to a piece of parchment paper. Spread sauce evenly, leaving a 1/2-inch border. Sprinkle with mozzarella, Parmesan, and your chosen toppings.
8. Bake: Carefully transfer pizza (with parchment) to the hot stone or sheet.
