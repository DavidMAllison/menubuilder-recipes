# Gochujang-Glazed Ribs

**Adapted from**: [America's Test Kitchen](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/recipes/17655-gochujang-glazed-ribs-for-gas-grill)  

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 tablespoons kosher salt
- 1 tablespoon gochugaru
- 1 tablespoon plus 2 teaspoons sesame seeds, toasted, divided
- 1 tablespoon packed brown sugar
- 2 teaspoons pepper
- 1 1/2 teaspoons granulated garlic
- 2 (2 1/2- to 3-pound) racks St. Louis-style spareribs, trimmed
- 2 cups oak, hickory, or pecan wood chips
- 1 (9-inch) disposable aluminum pie plate
- 2 scallions, sliced thin
- 1/4 cup gochujang
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 1/4 cup packed brown sugar
- 2 tablespoons ginger ale
- 2 tablespoons toasted sesame oil

## Instructions

1. Combine kosher salt, gochugaru, 1 tablespoon toasted sesame seeds, 1 tablespoon packed brown sugar, pepper, and granulated garlic in bowl. Place racks on rimmed baking sheet and pat dry with paper towels. Flip ribs meaty side down. Sprinkle bone side with about one-third of spice mixture. Flip and sprinkle meaty side with remaining two-thirds.
2. Using large piece of heavy-duty aluminum foil, wrap wood chips in 8 by 4-inch foil packet. Cut 2 evenly spaced 2-inch slits in top of packet.
3. Remove cooking grate and place wood chip packet directly on primary burner. Set grate in place; turn all burners to high; cover; and heat grill until hot, about 15 minutes. Leave primary burner on high and turn off other burner(s). Adjust primary burner as needed to maintain grill temperature around 350 degrees.
4. Clean and oil cooking grate. Place disposable pie plate over primary burner and add 2 cups water. Place ribs side by side on grill, meaty side up and arranged lengthwise so 1 side of each rack is about 5 inches from heat source.
5. Cover grill and cook ribs for 1 hour. Turn ribs 180 degrees so sides farthest from heat source are now closest (keeping ribs meaty side up). Add more water (up to 2 cups) to disposable pan if dry. Continue to cook, turning ribs and adding water as needed every hour, until ribs are tender and paring knife inserted into meat meets little resistance, 3 to 4 hours longer.
6. Meanwhile, whisk gochujang, soy sauce, brown sugar, ginger ale, and sesame oil in bowl until sugar is dissolved; set aside.
7. Transfer ribs to rimmed baking sheet, meaty side up. Brush meaty side of each rack with 3 tablespoons glaze. Tent with foil and let rest for 20 minutes. Sprinkle ribs with scallions and remaining 2 teaspoons toasted sesame seeds. Cut ribs between bones. Serve, passing remaining glaze.
