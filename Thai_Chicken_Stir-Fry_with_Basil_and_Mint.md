# Thai Chicken Stir-Fry with Basil and Mint

## Notes

- **Kids:** Omit chili entirely. Pull kids' chicken before adding sauce in step 4.
- Serve with steamed jasmine rice.

## Ingredients

**Proteins**
- 12 oz chicken breast or thighs, cut into thin strips

**Produce**
- 4 cloves garlic
- 1/2 medium onion
- 1 Thai chili (optional)
- 1 cup fresh basil
- 1/4 cup fresh mint

**Pantry/Asian**
- 1 tsp cornstarch
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1/2 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tsp fish sauce
- 1 tsp sugar

---

## Instructions

1. Combine chicken with cornstarch and 1 tsp oil. Toss to coat.
2. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wok over high heat. Stir-fry chicken until opaque. Remove and set aside.
3. Add remaining 1 tbsp oil. Stir-fry garlic, onion, and chili for 1 minute.
4. Return chicken to wok. Add light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, oyster sauce, fish sauce, and sugar. Toss to combine.
5. Add basil and mint. Stir-fry 30 seconds until just wilted.
6. Serve immediately with steamed rice.
