# Italian Wedding Soup

Time: 1.5 hours
Yield: 6 to 8 servings

## Ingredients

**Meatballs:**
- 1/2 pound fresh Tuscan bread, torn into bite-sized cubes
- 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
- 1/2 cup loosely packed fresh parsley
- 4 cloves garlic
- 1/4 cup heavy cream
- 2 eggs
- 1 pound lean ground beef
- 1 pound lean ground pork (or Italian sausage)
- Salt and pepper to taste

**Soup:**
- 1 tablespoon neutral oil
- 4 carrots, peeled and diced
- 4 ribs celery, trimmed and diced
- 1 onion, peeled and diced
- 6 cloves garlic, peeled and minced
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 1 teaspoon dry thyme
- 1 teaspoon dry oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon ground nutmeg
- 10 cups chicken stock
- 1 cup small pasta (such as ditalini)
- 5 ounces spinach
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Minced parsley, for serving
- Grated Parmesan cheese, for serving

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 400°F. Pulse bread in a food processor to fine crumbs. Transfer to a large bowl and combine with olive oil, parsley, 4 garlic cloves (minced), cream, and eggs. Season with salt and pepper. Add beef and pork and mix gently until just combined. Form into 1-inch meatballs and arrange on a rimmed baking sheet. Bake until cooked through and lightly browned, about 15 minutes.

2. While meatballs bake, heat neutral oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add carrots, celery, and onion and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, about 8 minutes. Add remaining 6 garlic cloves and cook 1 minute until fragrant.

3. Add butter, thyme, oregano, and nutmeg; stir to combine. Pour in chicken stock and bring to a simmer.

4. Add pasta and meatballs. Cook until pasta is tender, about 10 minutes.

5. Stir in spinach and cook until wilted, about 2 minutes. Season with salt and pepper. Serve topped with minced parsley and grated Parmesan.
