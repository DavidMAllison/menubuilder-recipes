# Lemon Garlic Pork Chops

Source: Inspired Taste / Delish (adapted)
Time: 25 minutes
Yield: 4 servings

## Ingredients

- 4 boneless pork loin chops (about 1 inch thick, 6 oz each)
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/2 cup low-sodium chicken broth
- 3 tbsp fresh lemon juice (about 1 lemon)
- 1 tsp lemon zest
- 1 tbsp butter
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped

## Notes

- For kids: Set aside plain seared pork chops before making the lemon-garlic sauce.
- Don't overcook: 145°F is the safe internal temperature for pork.
- Let chops rest 5 minutes before cutting to keep them juicy.

## Instructions

1. Pat pork chops dry with paper towels. In a small bowl, combine salt, pepper, garlic powder, and smoked paprika. Season both sides of each chop evenly with the spice mixture.
2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add pork chops and cook without moving for 3-4 minutes until golden brown on the bottom.
3. Flip chops and reduce heat to medium. Cook for another 4-5 minutes until internal temperature reaches 145°F. Transfer to a plate and tent loosely with foil to rest.
4. In the same skillet, add minced garlic and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant. Add chicken broth and lemon juice, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom. Simmer for 2-3 minutes until slightly reduced.
5. Remove from heat and stir in butter and lemon zest until butter is melted and sauce is glossy.
6. Return pork chops to the skillet and spoon sauce over them. Garnish with fresh parsley and serve immediately.
