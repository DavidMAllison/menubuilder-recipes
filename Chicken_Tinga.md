# Chicken Tinga

*Tinga de Pollo (Puebla-Style)*

## Ingredients

**For the Tinga Sauce**
- 8 roma tomatoes (about 2 lbs), rinsed
- 2 tomatillos (about 1/4 lb), husked and rinsed
- 3 tbsp safflower or vegetable oil
- 1/2 white onion, slivered
- 2 garlic cloves, chopped
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano, crumbled
- 1/4 tsp dried marjoram
- 1/4 tsp dried thyme
- 1 1/2 tsp sea or kosher salt, or to taste
- 1/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 2 tbsp chipotle sauce from adobo
- 1 whole chipotle chile in adobo, minced (optional, for extra heat)
- 5 cups cooked shredded chicken (rotisserie chicken works great)

**For Serving (Tostadas)**
- Corn tostadas
- Refried beans
- Shredded iceberg lettuce
- Avocado slices
- Queso fresco or cotija cheese, crumbled
- Mexican crema (optional)
- Salsa verde (optional)

## Notes

- For kids: Reserve some plain shredded chicken before adding to the sauce.
- Make ahead: Tinga keeps well refrigerated for 3-4 days.
- Other uses: Also delicious in tacos, burritos, tortas, or over rice.
- Spice level: Reduce or omit chipotle for milder version.

## Instructions

1. Place roma tomatoes and tomatillos in a medium saucepan. Cover with water and bring to a boil over medium-high heat. Reduce heat and simmer for about 10 minutes until soft and mushy. Drain, reserving 1/2 cup cooking liquid.
2. Transfer cooked tomatoes and tomatillos to a blender. Puree until completely smooth, adding a splash of reserved cooking liquid if needed. Set aside.
3. Heat oil in a large skillet or wide saucepan over medium heat. Add slivered onion and cook for 5-6 minutes until soft and translucent. Add garlic and cook 1 minute more until fragrant and lightly golden.
4. Pour the tomato-tomatillo sauce over the onion mixture. Add oregano, marjoram, thyme, salt, black pepper, chipotle sauce, and minced chipotle chile if using. Stir well to combine.
5. Let the sauce simmer for 10-12 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it has deepened in color and thickened slightly.
6. Fold in the shredded chicken, stirring to coat evenly with sauce. Continue cooking for another 5-8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the chicken has absorbed most of the juices and the mixture is moist but not soupy.

## Assembly (Tostadas)

1. Spread a layer of warm refried beans on each tostada.
2. Top generously with chicken tinga.
3. Add shredded lettuce, avocado slices, and crumbled cheese.
4. Drizzle with Mexican crema if desired.
5. Serve with salsa verde on the side.
