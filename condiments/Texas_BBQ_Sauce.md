# Texas BBQ Sauce

**Time**: 10 minutes  
**Yield**: ~1 cup

## Ingredients

- 1 cup ketchup
- 3 tablespoons butter
- 1½ tablespoons brown sugar
- 2 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tablespoon liquid smoke
- 1½ teaspoons mustard powder
- ½ teaspoon celery salt
- ¼ cup water
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Combine all ingredients in a medium saucepan over low heat.
2. Stir occasionally until fully combined and warmed through, about 5–8 minutes.
3. Taste and adjust salt, pepper, or sugar as needed.

## Notes

- Works well as a finishing sauce for ribs, brisket, or pulled pork.
- Can be made ahead and refrigerated for up to 2 weeks.
