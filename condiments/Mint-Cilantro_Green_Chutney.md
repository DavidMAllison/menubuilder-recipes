# Mint-Cilantro Green Chutney

**Time**: 10 minutes  
**Yield**: ~4 servings  
**Adapted from**: [Indian Healthy Recipes](https://www.indianhealthyrecipes.com/green-chutney/)

## Ingredients

- 2 cups cilantro leaves
- 1 cup mint leaves
- 1 teaspoon roasted cumin seeds (or ¾ tsp roasted cumin powder)
- 2–4 green chilies (adjust to taste)
- 2 garlic cloves (optional)
- ¼–½ inch ginger, peeled
- 1–1½ tablespoons lemon or lime juice
- ¼–⅓ teaspoon salt
- 1½–2 tablespoons roasted peanuts (optional)
- 1–2 tablespoons water

## Instructions

1. Remove roots and mature stalks from cilantro; rinse thoroughly. Pluck mint leaves and rinse.
2. Drain herbs completely in a colander.
3. Add all ingredients to a blender jar.
4. Blend into a thick paste, adding water 1 tablespoon at a time as needed.
5. Taste and adjust salt, lemon juice, or chilies.
6. Transfer to a clean jar and refrigerate. Use within 2–3 days or freeze up to 1 month.

## Notes

- The classic all-purpose Indian green chutney. More rounded flavor than cilantro-only versions.
- Pairs with chicken tikka, chaat, samosas, and grilled meats.
