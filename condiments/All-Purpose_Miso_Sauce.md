# All-Purpose Miso Sauce

**Time**: 30 minutes  
**Yield**: about 1¼ cups (20 Tbsp)

## Ingredients

- 1 cup red miso
- 1 cup mirin
- 1/2 cup sake
- 2 tbsp sugar

## Instructions

1. Whisk the miso, mirin, sake, and sugar together in a small saucepan until smooth.
2. Bring to a boil over medium heat, stirring now and then so it doesn't scorch.
3. Turn the heat to low and simmer 20-30 minutes, stirring and scraping the bottom often, until thick enough to coat a spatula.
4. Take off the heat and let cool completely.
5. Store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 2 months.

## Notes

- Makes enough for two batches of Miso Chicken (each uses 10 Tbsp).
