# Tzatziki

**Time**: 40 minutes  
**Yield**: 12

## Ingredients

- 1 pound cucumbers, ends removed and sliced lengthwise (about 6 baby cucumbers)
- 2 cup strained yogurt (Greek or otherwise)
- 4 clove garlic, minced fine
- 1 large handful dill, minced
- 1 lemon, juiced

## Instructions

1. Scoop the seeds out of the cucumbers with a teaspoon to leave neat half-moons, then slice them thin but not paper-thin so they keep some crunch.
2. Combine the cucumbers with the yogurt, garlic, dill, and lemon juice in a mixing bowl, then taste and adjust the acid and seasoning.
3. Serve right away, or preferably cover and refrigerate for a few hours to let the flavors meld.
