# Red Enchilada Sauce

**Yield**: ~3 cups (about 14 servings)

## Ingredients

- 6 dried ancho chiles
- 3 cups beef broth
- 6 oz tomato paste
- ¼ cup corn oil
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1½ teaspoons salt
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- ¼ teaspoon ground cumin

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
2. Arrange ancho chiles on a baking sheet and bake 3–4 minutes until toasted.
3. Remove and discard stems and seeds. Place chiles in a bowl, cover with hot water, and soak 1 hour.
4. Combine soaked chiles, 1 cup broth, tomato paste, oil, garlic, salt, oregano, and cumin in a blender. Blend until smooth.
5. Pour mixture into a saucepan with remaining 2 cups broth. Simmer over medium heat for 10 minutes.

## Notes

- Use for enchiladas, burritos, or as a braising liquid for chicken or pork.
- Keeps refrigerated for up to 1 week, or freeze for up to 3 months.
