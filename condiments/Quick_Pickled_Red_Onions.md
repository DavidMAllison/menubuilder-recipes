# Quick Pickled Red Onions

**Time**: 5 minutes + 20 minutes resting  
**Yield**: ~1 cup (about 8 servings)

## Ingredients

- 1 medium red onion, thinly sliced into half-moons
- ½ cup apple cider vinegar
- ½ cup water
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- ½ teaspoon kosher salt

## Instructions

1. Thinly slice red onion into half-moons.
2. Combine vinegar, water, sugar, and salt in a small saucepan. Bring to a simmer and stir until sugar and salt dissolve.
3. Pour brine over onions in a jar or heatproof bowl.
4. Let sit at room temperature at least 20 minutes before serving. Onions will turn pink as they pickle.
5. Refrigerate after cooling. Keeps up to 2 weeks.

## Notes

- Pairs well with tacos, burrito bowls, sandwiches, grain bowls, and burgers.
- Add a pinch of red pepper flakes or a garlic clove to the brine for variation.
