# Yogurt-Herb Sauce

## Ingredients

- 1 cup whole milk or low-fat plain yogurt
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 2 tablespoons minced fresh cilantro
- 2 tablespoons minced fresh mint
- Salt and pepper

## Before You Begin

- This recipe can be halved to make 1/2 cup.
- Replace cilantro and mint with dill, tarragon, or another herb you prefer.

## Instructions

1. Combine yogurt, garlic, cilantro, and mint in a small bowl; season to taste with salt and pepper.
2. Let stand at least 30 minutes to blend flavors. (Sauce can be refrigerated for up to 2 days.)

## Notes

- Serve with Turmeric Rice and Chicken Salad with Herbs, grilled meats or fish, grain bowls, or as a dip for vegetables or pita.
