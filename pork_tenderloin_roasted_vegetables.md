# Pork Tenderloin with Roasted Vegetables

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 lbs pork tenderloin
- 1 lb baby potatoes, halved
- 3 cups broccoli florets
- 2 medium carrots, cut into 2-inch pieces
- 1 red bell pepper, cut into chunks
- 3 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 tsp dried rosemary
- 1 tsp salt, divided
- 1/2 tsp black pepper, divided
- 1 tsp paprika
- 2 garlic cloves, minced

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 425F. Line a large sheet pan with parchment paper or lightly grease it.

2. Prep vegetables: Toss potatoes, carrots, and bell pepper with 2 tbsp olive oil, 1/2 tsp salt, 1/4 tsp pepper, and minced garlic. Spread on one side of the sheet pan.

3. Season pork: Pat tenderloin dry. Rub with remaining 1 tbsp olive oil. Mix garlic powder, thyme, rosemary, paprika, remaining 1/2 tsp salt, and 1/4 tsp pepper in a small bowl. Rub all over the pork.

4. Place pork on the sheet pan next to vegetables. Roast 15 minutes.

5. Remove pan from oven. Add broccoli florets, tossing with the other vegetables. Return to oven and roast another 15-20 minutes until pork reaches 145F and vegetables are tender and lightly browned.

6. Transfer pork to a cutting board, tent with foil, and rest 5-10 minutes. Slice into 1/2-inch medallions and serve alongside vegetables. Optionally drizzle pan juices over the pork.
