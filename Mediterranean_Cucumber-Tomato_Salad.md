# Mediterranean Cucumber-Tomato Salad

## Ingredients

- 2 large cucumbers, halved lengthwise and sliced
- 3 medium tomatoes, cut into wedges or chopped
- 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar or lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon table salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped (optional)
- 2 ounces feta cheese, crumbled (optional)

## Before You Begin

- Can be made 30 minutes ahead and refrigerated; flavors improve as it sits.
- For kids who prefer plain vegetables, serve cucumbers and tomatoes separately without dressing.

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl, combine sliced cucumbers, tomato wedges, and thinly sliced red onion.
2. In a small bowl, whisk together olive oil, red wine vinegar, dried oregano, salt, and pepper.
3. Pour dressing over vegetables and toss gently. Let sit 5-10 minutes to allow flavors to blend.
4. Just before serving, add fresh parsley and feta cheese if using. Toss gently and serve at room temperature.
