# Teriyaki Salmon with Bok Choy

## Notes

Claude-original flavor combo (David's protein + sauce + veg formula), not yet cooked by the family. Counts toward the max-1-salmon-per-week guideline. Add feedback here after the first try.

## Ingredients

- 4 (6 oz each) salmon fillets, skin removed
- to taste kosher salt and black pepper
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 3 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 2 tbsp mirin
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
- 1 clove garlic
- 3 baby bok choy, halved lengthwise
- 1 tsp sesame oil

**Optional / For Serving:**

- 1 tsp sesame seeds

## Instructions

1. Whisk soy sauce, mirin, honey, ginger, and garlic in a small bowl.
2. Season salmon with salt and pepper. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Cook salmon 4-5 minutes, flip, and cook 2-3 minutes more until just cooked through. Transfer to a plate.
3. Pour teriyaki sauce into the skillet and simmer 1-2 minutes until slightly thickened. Return salmon to the pan and spoon sauce over to glaze.
4. Meanwhile, heat sesame oil in a separate skillet over medium-high heat. Add bok choy cut-side down and sear 2 minutes, then add 2 tbsp water, cover, and steam 2-3 minutes until crisp-tender.
5. Serve salmon over bok choy, garnished with sesame seeds if using.
