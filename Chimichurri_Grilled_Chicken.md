# Chimichurri Grilled Chicken

## Ingredients

**Chimichurri Sauce:**
- 1 cup Italian parsley, packed
- 1/2 cup fresh cilantro, packed
- 1/2 cup olive oil
- 1/4 cup red wine vinegar
- 3 garlic cloves
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/4 teaspoon crushed red pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon salt

**Chicken and Marinade:**
- 4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
- 1/2 cup Greek yogurt
- 2 tablespoons honey
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon freshly cracked black pepper

## Instructions

1. Pick parsley and cilantro leaves from the stems and mince finely with the garlic.
2. Combine the minced herbs and garlic with olive oil, red wine vinegar, oregano, cumin, red pepper, and salt in a bowl to make the chimichurri; set aside.
3. Pat chicken thighs dry with paper towels. Season with salt and pepper on all sides.
4. In a separate small bowl, blend 1/4 cup of the prepared chimichurri with the Greek yogurt and honey.
5. Tuck the yogurt marinade under the skin of each thigh, then place thighs in a zip-top bag with any remaining marinade. Seal and refrigerate at least 2 hours.
6. Preheat grill to 375°F.
7. Grill thighs skin-side down for 15 minutes, then flip and cook 15 minutes more until internal temperature reaches 160°F.
8. Rest, covered, until temperature reaches 165°F.
9. Top with the reserved fresh chimichurri and serve.
