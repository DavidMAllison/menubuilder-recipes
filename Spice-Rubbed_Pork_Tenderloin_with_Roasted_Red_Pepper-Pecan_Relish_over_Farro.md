# Spice-Rubbed Pork Tenderloin with Roasted Red Pepper-Pecan Relish over Farro

## Notes

Weeknight riff on Monday's Grilled Spice-Rubbed Pork Tenderloin with Charred Fingerling Potato Salad -- no grill required (sear + oven-finish instead), potatoes swapped for quick farro, and the roasted red pepper-pecan mix moves from a tossed salad to a relish spooned over the protein. Works with chicken in place of pork. For kids: pull plain pork/chicken slices and plain farro before topping with the relish.

## Ingredients

- 2 (1-pound) pork tenderloins, trimmed (or sub 2 lbs boneless, skinless chicken thighs or breasts)
- 1 tablespoon packed brown sugar
- 2 1/2 teaspoons smoked paprika
- 1/8 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/8 teaspoon ground allspice
- 2 1/2 teaspoons kosher salt, divided
- 1 1/4 teaspoon pepper, divided
- 3 tablespoons olive oil, divided
- 1 cup quick-cooking farro
- 2 cups low-sodium chicken broth or water
- 2 tablespoons white balsamic vinegar
- 3/4 cup jarred roasted red peppers, sliced thin
- 1/2 cup pecans, toasted and chopped
- 2 scallions, sliced thin

**Optional / For Serving:**

- chopped fresh parsley, for the farro

## Instructions

1. Bring the broth (or water) to a boil in a medium saucepan. Stir in the farro and 1/2 teaspoon salt, reduce heat, cover, and simmer per package directions (about 10-15 minutes for quick-cooking farro) until tender. Drain any excess liquid, fluff, and toss with a drizzle of olive oil and parsley if using; keep warm.
2. Mix brown sugar, smoked paprika, cinnamon, allspice, remaining 2 teaspoons salt, and remaining 1 teaspoon pepper together in a bowl. Measure out 2 teaspoons of the spice mixture and set aside for the relish. Pat the pork dry with paper towels and rub evenly with the remaining spice mixture.
3. Heat 1 tablespoon olive oil in a large oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the pork on all sides until well browned, 6-8 minutes total. Transfer the skillet to a 400F oven and roast until the pork registers 145 degrees, 10-15 minutes (chicken breasts/thighs: roast to 165 degrees). Transfer to a cutting board, tent with foil, and rest 5 minutes.
4. While the pork rests, whisk the white balsamic vinegar, remaining 2 tablespoons olive oil, and the reserved spice mixture together in a bowl. Stir in the roasted red peppers, pecans, and scallions.
5. Slice the pork, plate over the farro, and spoon the roasted red pepper-pecan relish generously on top. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
