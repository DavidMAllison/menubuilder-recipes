# Pork Tenderloin Medallions with Lemon-Parsley Pan Sauce

Source: America's Test Kitchen
Time: 30 minutes
Yield: 4 servings

## Ingredients

- 3/4 cup chicken broth
- 1 tbsp grated lemon zest plus 1/4 cup juice (2 lemons)
- 2 tsp all-purpose flour
- 2 (1-pound) pork tenderloins, trimmed and cut crosswise into 2-inch pieces
- 1 tsp table salt
- 1/2 tsp pepper
- 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, divided
- 4 garlic cloves, sliced thin
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
- 3 tbsp minced fresh parsley

## Instructions

1. Whisk broth, lemon juice, and flour: Whisk 3/4 cup chicken broth, 1/4 cup lemon juice, and 2 tsp all-purpose flour together in liquid measuring cup; set aside. Cut 2 trimmed pork tenderloins crosswise into 2-inch pieces and sprinkle all over with 1 tsp table salt and 1/2 tsp pepper.
2. Sear the medallions: Heat 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil in 12-inch skillet over medium-high heat until just smoking. Arrange pork medallions in skillet cut side down and cook without moving until first side is well browned, 3 to 5 minutes. Transfer pork to plate, browned side up.
3. Bloom aromatics: Off heat, stir 4 thinly sliced garlic cloves and 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes into fat remaining in skillet and cook in residual heat until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Stir broth mixture to recombine, then add to skillet, scraping up any browned bits.
4. Simmer the pork: Return pork to skillet browned side up, along with any accumulated juices. Bring broth mixture to simmer over medium heat, then reduce heat to medium-low. Cover skillet and simmer gently until pork registers 135 to 140 degrees, 4 to 6 minutes. Transfer pork medallions to serving platter browned side up as they finish cooking, leaving sauce in skillet.
5. Finish the sauce: Increase heat to medium-high and simmer until sauce is thickened and bubbling throughout, 2 to 4 minutes. Off heat, whisk in 3 tbsp minced parsley, 1 tbsp grated lemon zest, and remaining 1 tbsp oil. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Spoon sauce over pork. Serve. Serving Suggestions - Roasted vegetables (broccoli, asparagus, or green beans) - Steamed rice or couscous - Side salad with light vinaigrette

## Notes

- For kids: Transfer plain seared medallions to kids' plates before making the pan sauce
- You will need a 12-inch skillet with a tight-fitting lid
- Don't overcook: 135-140 degrees F is the target internal temperature
- Uses 2 pork tenderloins - great for using freezer inventory
