# Beef and Broccoli Stir-Fry

Source: America's Test Kitchen (by Matthew Fairman)
Time: 55 minutes
Yield: 4 servings

## Ingredients

- 1 (1 1/2-pound) flank steak, trimmed
- 1/4 cup oyster sauce, divided
- 1 1/2 tablespoons cornstarch, divided
- 1 tablespoon water
- 1/4 teaspoon baking soda
- 3 scallions, white parts sliced thin, green parts cut into 1-inch pieces
- 3 tablespoons vegetable oil, divided
- 6 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 teaspoons grated fresh ginger
- 1/4 cup chicken broth
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1 pound broccoli florets, cut into 1 1/2-inch pieces

## Before You Begin

- Recommended oyster sauce: Lee Kum Kee Premium Oyster Flavored Sauce.
- Serve with rice.

## Instructions

1. Cut steak into thirds with the grain, then slice each third thin against the grain. Whisk 1 tablespoon oyster sauce, 1 tablespoon cornstarch, water, and baking soda in a medium bowl. Add beef, stir to coat, and let sit at room temperature 15 to 30 minutes.
2. Combine scallion whites, 2 tablespoons oil, garlic, and ginger in a second bowl. Whisk broth, soy sauce, sugar, remaining 3 tablespoons oyster sauce, and remaining 1 1/2 teaspoons cornstarch in a third bowl.
3. Heat remaining 1 tablespoon oil in a 12-inch nonstick skillet over high heat until just smoking. Add beef and break up clumps with tongs. Cook without stirring until browned on bottom, about 2 minutes. Stir and continue to cook until spotty brown and no longer pink, about 2 minutes more.
4. Stir in scallion mixture and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute. Whisk broth mixture to recombine, then add to skillet along with broccoli. Cover and cook until broccoli is crisp-tender, about 3 minutes, shaking skillet occasionally. Off heat, stir in scallion greens. Serve.
