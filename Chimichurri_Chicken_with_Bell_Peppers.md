# Chimichurri Chicken with Bell Peppers

## Notes

Claude-original flavor combo (David's protein + sauce + veg formula), not yet cooked by the family. Protein-flexible: chicken, flank steak, or shrimp -- using flank steak counts toward the weekly red-meat slot. Chimichurri itself isn't spicy; the optional red pepper flakes are the only heat, easy to leave off for the kids. Add feedback here after the first try.

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs protein of choice (chicken breast or thighs, flank steak, or shrimp)
- 1 tbsp olive oil (for cooking)
- to taste kosher salt and black pepper
- 1 cup fresh parsley, finely chopped
- 3 tbsp olive oil (for chimichurri)
- 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 2 bell peppers (any color), sliced

**Optional / For Serving:**

- red pepper flakes

## Instructions

1. Combine parsley, olive oil, red wine vinegar, garlic, and oregano in a bowl; season with salt and pepper. Set aside -- this is the chimichurri.
2. Season protein with salt and pepper. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook protein until browned and cooked through -- chicken about 3-4 minutes per side (165°F), flank steak about 4-5 minutes per side for medium (rest 5 minutes before slicing), shrimp about 1-2 minutes per side until pink. Transfer to a plate.
3. Add bell peppers to the skillet; saute 4-5 minutes until crisp-tender.
4. Slice or portion the protein and pull out plain servings for the kids before saucing.
5. Toss adult portions with chimichurri and the bell peppers; add red pepper flakes if desired. Serve kids' plain protein and peppers on the side.
