# Korean Fried Chicken (Dakgangjeong)

**Adapted from**: [Milk Street](https://www.wttw.com/playlist/2024/07/02/korean-fried-chicken-milk-street)

## Notes

- Double-frying creates superior crispness that holds after glazing.
- Maintain oil at 350°F between batches.
- Toss with glaze while chicken is hot.

## Ingredients

**Marinade:**
- 1/2 cup soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon grated ginger
- Ground black pepper
- 2 lbs boneless chicken thighs, cut into 2-3 inch pieces

**Coating:**
- 1 cup cornstarch
- 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/4 cup reserved marinade
- 1 1/4 cups water

**Glaze:**
- 1/4 cup plus 2 tablespoons gochujang
- 3 tablespoons honey
- 2 tablespoons white sugar
- 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
- 2 garlic cloves, grated
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce

**For frying and serving:**
- 2 quarts peanut or canola oil
- 4 scallions, sliced
- Toasted sesame seeds

## Instructions

1. Marinate: Combine soy sauce, ginger, and pepper in a bowl. Add chicken and toss to coat. Set aside 15-30 minutes.
2. Make glaze: Whisk together gochujang, honey, sugar, vinegar, garlic, and soy sauce in a large bowl. Set aside.
3. Prep: Transfer chicken to paper towels, reserving 1/4 cup marinade. Pat chicken dry.
4. Coating: Whisk reserved marinade, cornstarch, flour, and water until smooth. Heat oil to 350°F in a large pot.
5. First fry: Working in thirds, dip chicken in slurry and fry until light golden, about 2 minutes. Transfer to a rack. Repeat with remaining chicken.
6. Second fry: Return oil to 350°F. Fry chicken in batches until crispy and deep golden, 2-3 minutes. Repeat with remaining.
7. Finish: Toss hot chicken in glaze. Top with scallions and sesame seeds. Serve immediately.
