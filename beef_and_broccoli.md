# Beef and Broccoli Stir-Fry

Time: 55 minutes
Yield: 4 servings

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 lbs flank steak, trimmed
- 1/4 cup oyster sauce, divided
- 1 1/2 tbsp cornstarch, divided
- 1 tbsp water
- 1/4 tsp baking soda
- 3 scallions, white parts sliced thin, green parts cut into 1-inch pieces
- 3 tbsp vegetable oil, divided
- 6 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tsp grated fresh ginger
- 1/4 cup chicken broth
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 lb broccoli florets, cut into 1 1/2-inch pieces

## Instructions

1. Cut steak into thirds with the grain, then slice each third thin against the grain. Whisk 1 tbsp oyster sauce, 1 tbsp cornstarch, water, and baking soda together in a medium bowl. Add beef, stir to coat, and let sit at room temperature for 15-30 minutes.

2. Combine scallion whites, 2 tbsp oil, garlic, and ginger in a second bowl. Whisk broth, soy sauce, sugar, remaining 3 tbsp oyster sauce, and remaining 1 1/2 tsp cornstarch together in a third bowl.

3. Heat remaining 1 tbsp oil in a 12-inch nonstick skillet over high heat until just smoking. Add beef and break up clumps with tongs. Cook without stirring until browned on the bottom, about 2 minutes. Stir and continue cooking until spotty brown and no longer pink, about 2 minutes more.

4. Stir in scallion mixture and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute. Whisk broth mixture to recombine, then add to skillet along with broccoli. Cover and cook until broccoli is crisp-tender, about 3 minutes, shaking skillet occasionally. Off heat, stir in scallion greens. Serve.
