# Beef and Broccoli Stir-Fry

**Time**: 40 minutes (including 15–30 min marinating)
**Servings**: 4

## Ingredients

- 1½ lbs flank steak, trimmed
- ¼ cup oyster sauce, divided
- 1½ tablespoons cornstarch, divided
- 1 tablespoon water
- ¼ teaspoon baking soda
- 3 scallions, white parts sliced thin, green parts cut into 1-inch pieces
- 3 tablespoons vegetable oil, divided
- 6 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 teaspoons grated fresh ginger
- ¼ cup chicken broth
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1 lb broccoli florets, cut into 1½-inch pieces

## Instructions

1. Slice steak into thirds with the grain, then slice each third thin against the grain. Whisk 1 tablespoon oyster sauce, 1 tablespoon cornstarch, the water, and baking soda in a bowl. Add beef, toss to coat, and let sit at room temperature for 15–30 minutes.
2. Mix scallion whites, 2 tablespoons oil, garlic, and ginger in a second bowl. Whisk broth, soy sauce, sugar, remaining 3 tablespoons oyster sauce, and remaining 1½ teaspoons cornstarch in a third bowl.
3. Heat remaining 1 tablespoon oil in a large nonstick skillet over high heat. Add beef and spread into a single layer. Cook without stirring until browned on the bottom, about 2 minutes, then stir and cook until no longer pink, about 2 minutes more.
4. Stir in the scallion-garlic mixture and cook until fragrant, 1 minute. Add the broth mixture and broccoli. Cover and cook until broccoli is crisp-tender, about 3 minutes, shaking the pan a few times. Stir in scallion greens and serve.

## Notes

- Serve over steamed rice.
- Lee Kum Kee Premium oyster sauce is recommended.
