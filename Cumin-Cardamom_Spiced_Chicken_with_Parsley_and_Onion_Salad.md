# Cumin-Cardamom Spiced Chicken with Parsley and Onion Salad

Source: America's Test Kitchen (by Amanda Agee)
Time: 35 minutes
Yield: 4 servings

## Ingredients

**Tahini Sauce:**
- 1/4 cup tahini
- 1/4 cup water
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 3/4 teaspoon finely grated garlic
- 1/4 teaspoon table salt

**Chicken:**
- 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil, divided
- 1 teaspoon table salt
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon ground cardamom
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 3/4 teaspoon turmeric
- 1 1/2 pounds boneless, skinless chicken thighs, trimmed

**Salad:**
- 2 cups fresh parsley leaves
- 1/2 red onion, sliced thin, rinsed
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- Pinch table salt
- Lemon wedges, for serving

## Before You Begin

- Pair with warm pita bread or rice. Sliced tomatoes and/or cucumbers also complement this dish.

## Instructions

1. Whisk tahini, water, lemon juice, garlic, and salt in a bowl until smooth and thickened.
2. Whisk 1 tablespoon oil, salt, cumin, cardamom, paprika, and turmeric in a medium bowl. Add chicken and toss to coat. Heat remaining 1 tablespoon oil in a 12-inch nonstick skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add chicken and cook until browned on both sides and registers at least 175°F, 9 to 12 minutes. Transfer to a plate and let rest 5 minutes.
3. While chicken rests, toss parsley leaves, red onion, lemon juice, and salt in a bowl.
4. Slice chicken into thin strips. Serve with salad, tahini sauce, and lemon wedges.
