# Tuscan kale and chickpea soup

## Ingredients

- Dried chickpeas 1 ¼ cup
- Italian kale (cavolo nero) to be cleaned 0.9 lb
- Sweet potatoes (or yams) 0.7 lb
- Onions 3.5 oz
- Celery 1 cup
- Carrots ¾ cup
- Garlic 1 clove
- Vegetable broth 2 ½ cups
- Bay leaves 1 leaf
- Rosemary 1 sprig
- Extra virgin olive oil to taste
- Fine salt to taste
- Black pepper to taste
- Rye bread 4 slices
- Curry 1 spoonful
- Thyme to taste
- Extra virgin olive oil to taste

## Instructions

1. To prepare the Tuscan kale and chickpea soup, first soak the dried chickpeas for 8–12 hours, then drain them and transfer them to a pot, covering them with water. Add a bay leaf and cook until tender; generally this takes about 60–90 minutes, depending on the quality and how long they were soaked. In the meantime, peel and chop the onion.
2. Do the same with the carrot and celery. Now peel the sweet potatoes.
3. Cut them into even cubes. In a large pot heat the oil, then add the chopped onion, carrot and celery together with the minced garlic and the rosemary and let them sweat over low heat for about 5 minutes, just long enough to soften the vegetables without browning them.
4. Season with salt and add the sweet potatoes to the soffritto. Let them absorb the flavors for a few minutes, then add half of the chickpeas and stir.
5. At this point cover with hot vegetable broth and cook for about 20 minutes, until the sweet potatoes are tender and begin to break down slightly. Meanwhile, prepare the Tuscan kale: remove the tougher central ribs and slice the leaves into strips.
6. When the potatoes and chickpeas are well cooked, remove the rosemary and bay leaf, then blend part of the soup with an immersion blender; you should not obtain a completely smooth consistency — chickpeas and potatoes should still be visible. Now add the Tuscan kale and the remaining chickpeas and continue cooking over low heat for another 10–15 minutes, until the kale is tender.
7. At the end of cooking adjust salt and pepper to taste and let everything come together for another minute: the soup should be thick, creamy and comforting. Meanwhile, prepare the croutons: cut the rye bread into cubes.
8. Heat a drizzle of oil in a wide skillet, then add the bread and toast it. Flavor with curry powder and fresh thyme, mixing well to coat evenly.
9. When the cubes are golden and crisp, turn off the heat. Pour the hot soup into bowls, finish with a drizzle of raw olive oil and the crispy curry-and-thyme rye croutons. Your Tuscan kale and chickpea soup is ready to be enjoyed 27 !
