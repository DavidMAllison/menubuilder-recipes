# Amarillito Mole with Chicken

*Mole Amarillito con Pollo*
**Adapted from**: [Pati Jinich](https://patijinich.com/amarillito-mole-with-chicken)

## Ingredients

- 2 ancho chiles, stemmed and seeded
- 2 guajillo chiles, stemmed and seeded
- 1 lb (about 8 to 10) tomatillos, husked and rinsed
- 1 Roma tomato
- 4 garlic cloves
- 2 whole cloves
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp oregano
- 2 tsp kosher or sea salt, or to taste
- 1/4 tsp black pepper, ground
- 3 tbsp vegetable oil
- 8 chicken pieces, with skin and bones
- 1/4 cup white onion, chopped
- 5 cups chicken broth
- 3 medium fresh hoja santa or 5 dried, optional
- Corn Masa Dumplings, optional

## Instructions

1. Set a comal or dry skillet to medium heat and allow it to warm up. Toast your chiles quickly, turning them after about 10 to 15 seconds per side, until they become pliable and release their fragrant essential oils. Move the toasted chiles into a pot and combine them with tomatillos, tomato, and garlic cloves. Cover everything with water and heat to a boil on medium-high, then reduce to a simmer and cook for 10 minutes until the ingredients are completely soft and cooked through. Transfer this mixture to a blender along with 2 whole cloves, ground cinnamon, oregano, salt, and pepper, and process until the texture is perfectly uniform and smooth. Set this puree aside.

2. Warm 3 tablespoons of oil in a large skillet on medium-high heat. Lay the chicken pieces skin side down into the pan and sear until browned, allowing 3 to 4 minutes for each side. Add the onion to the pan and let it cook alongside the chicken for 2 to 3 minutes until softened and translucent. Pour the reserved chile-tomatillo puree over the chicken, stirring in the hojas santas if you choose to use them. Let this cook for 8 to 10 minutes so the flavors meld together and the sauce becomes more concentrated and thick.

3. Add the chicken stock to the pan and bring everything to a low simmer, keeping the heat at medium for 15 to 20 minutes. Turn the heat down to medium-low and gently introduce the masa dumplings into the pan, adding them individually. Maintain the simmer for another 12 to 15 minutes, cooking until the dumplings are done and the mole has thickened enough to coat a wooden spoon when you run your finger across it.
