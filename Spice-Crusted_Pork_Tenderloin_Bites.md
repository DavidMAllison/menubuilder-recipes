# Spice-Crusted Pork Tenderloin Bites


## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon honey
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1 1/2-pound pork tenderloin, trimmed of silver skin, cut into 1- to 1 1/2-inch chunks
- 1 1/2 teaspoons ground coriander
- 1 1/2 teaspoons ground cumin
- 1 1/2 teaspoons smoked paprika
- Kosher salt and ground black pepper
- 1 tablespoon neutral oil

## Before You Begin

- Inspired by Spanish pinchos morunos; good as a tapa, appetizer, or with garlicky braised greens and crusty bread.

## Instructions

1. Mix honey and lemon juice in a small bowl; set aside.
2. Toss pork with coriander, cumin, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper.
3. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add pork and cook until browned on all sides and cooked through, about 8-10 minutes.
4. Drizzle with the honey-lemon mixture and serve.
