# Pasta with Sausage Ragu

**Time**: 1 hour 15 minutes
**Servings**: 8 (makes extra sauce)

## Ingredients

- ½ fennel bulb, stalks discarded, cored and chopped coarse
- ½ onion, chopped coarse
- 1 tablespoon fennel seeds
- 1 (28-ounce) can whole peeled tomatoes
- 2 pounds sweet Italian sausage, casings removed
- 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil, plus extra for drizzling
- ½ teaspoon salt, plus salt for pasta water
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1½ teaspoons dried oregano
- ¾ cup red wine
- 1 cup water
- Pepper
- 1 pound pappardelle or tagliatelle
- Grated Parmesan, for serving

## Instructions

1. Pulse fennel, onion, and fennel seeds in a food processor until finely chopped, about 10 pulses; transfer to a bowl. Process tomatoes until smooth, about 10 seconds; transfer to a second bowl. Pulse sausage in the now-empty processor until finely chopped, about 10 pulses.
2. Heat oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high. Add sausage and cook, breaking it up, until all the liquid evaporates and the meat starts to sizzle, 10–15 minutes. Add the fennel mixture and ½ teaspoon salt and cook until softened, about 5 minutes — the bottom will be deeply browned.
3. Stir in tomato paste, garlic, and oregano and cook 30 seconds. Add wine, scraping up the browned bits, and cook until nearly evaporated, about 1 minute. Add water and pureed tomatoes and bring to a simmer. Reduce heat and simmer gently, uncovered, until thickened, about 45 minutes. Season with salt and pepper. (Extra sauce keeps refrigerated for 3 days or frozen for 1 month.)
4. Cook pasta in a large pot of well-salted water until al dente. Reserve 1 cup cooking water, then drain. Return pasta to the pot, add 3 cups sauce and ½ cup reserved pasta water, and toss to combine. Adjust consistency with more pasta water as needed. Drizzle with oil and serve with Parmesan.
