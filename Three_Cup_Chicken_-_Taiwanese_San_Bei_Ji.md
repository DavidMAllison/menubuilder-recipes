# Three Cup Chicken - Taiwanese San Bei Ji

## Ingredients

- 650 gr chicken thigh fillet or boneless, skinless chicken breasts, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 2 cloves garlic
- 3 cm ginger
- 3 pcs chilis
- 15 gr basil leaves (Thai)
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp soy sauce (dark)
- 2 tsp palm sugar
- 2 tbsp Shaoxing wine
- 1 tsp mushroom powder (optional)

## Instructions

1. Cut the chicken thighs into bite-sized pieces, about 1–2 inches wide. Place the chicken in a bowl, then add the Shaoxing wine and soy sauce. Mix well and let it marinate for 10–15 minutes.
2. Heat some cooking oil and sesame oil in a wok. Add the minced garlic and ginger, then stir-fry until fragrant.
3. Transfer the marinated chicken to the wok and pour in the remaining marinade/sauce.
4. Add the palm sugar and mushroom powder. Stir well and cook the chicken, turning the pieces occasionally, until they change color and are lightly browned. Cover the wok with a lid and cook for 5 minutes, until the thickest pieces register 175 degrees for thighs, or 160 degrees for breasts (breasts may be done a minute or two sooner, so check early to avoid drying them out).
5. Add the chilies and Thai basil. Stir-fry for about 1 minute. Once the Thai basil has wilted and the chicken is well coated with the sauce, turn off the heat. Serve hot with steamed rice.
