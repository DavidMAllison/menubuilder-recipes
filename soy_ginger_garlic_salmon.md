# Soy-Ginger-Garlic Salmon

## Ingredients

- 4 salmon fillets (6 oz each), skin-on or skinless
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons rice vinegar
- 2 tablespoons honey or brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 2 tablespoons fresh ginger, minced or grated
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil (for cooking)
- 2 green onions, sliced (for garnish)
- 1 tablespoon sesame seeds (optional, for garnish)

## Instructions

1. Make the marinade: In a small bowl, whisk together soy sauce, rice vinegar, honey, sesame oil, ginger, and garlic. Reserve 2 tablespoons in a separate small bowl for basting.
2. Marinate salmon: Place salmon in a shallow dish or zip-top bag. Pour remaining marinade over salmon. Cover and refrigerate 30 minutes to 2 hours.
3. Cook the salmon: Heat vegetable oil in a large nonstick or cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat. Remove salmon from marinade (discard used marinade) and pat dry with paper towels. Cook 4-5 minutes per side until salmon reaches 125-130°F for medium. Brush with reserved marinade during the last minute of cooking.
4. Transfer to plates and garnish with sliced green onions and sesame seeds if desired.

## Notes

- Don't marinate longer than 2 hours or the salmon texture can become mushy from the acid.
- Pat salmon dry before cooking for better browning.
- For crispier skin (if using skin-on), start salmon skin-side down.
- Serve with steamed rice and stir-fried vegetables, or roasted broccoli and quinoa.
