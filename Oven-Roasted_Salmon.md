# Oven-Roasted Salmon

**Time**: 25 minutes
**Servings**: 4

## Ingredients

- 1 skin-on salmon fillet (1¾–2 lbs), about 1½ inches thick
- 2 teaspoons olive oil
- Kosher salt and black pepper

## Instructions

1. Place a rimmed baking sheet on the lowest oven rack and heat oven to 500°F.
2. Trim any pale belly fat from the fillet and cut into 4 equal pieces. Score the skin side of each piece with 4–5 shallow slashes to prevent curling. Pat dry, rub all over with olive oil, and season generously with salt and pepper.
3. Reduce oven to 275°F. Carefully pull out the hot baking sheet and place salmon pieces skin-side down, spaced at least 1 inch apart. Return to oven.
4. Roast until the thickest part is just slightly translucent at the center, or reads 125°F on an instant-read thermometer, 9–13 minutes depending on thickness. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- 125°F gives medium/medium-rare. Cook to 130–135°F for more well-done.
- Buy a whole center-cut fillet and portion it yourself for even cooking.
- Keep skin on during cooking for best results; remove after if preferred.
- Kids: Serve plain with lemon.
