# Oven-Roasted Salmon

Source: America's Test Kitchen
Time: 30 minutes
Yield: 4 servings

## Ingredients

- 1 skin-on salmon fillet (1 3/4 to 2 lbs), about 1 1/2 inches thick at thickest part
- 2 tsp olive oil
- Kosher salt
- Freshly ground black pepper

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven: Adjust oven rack to the lowest position. Place a rimmed baking sheet on the rack. Heat oven to 500 degrees F.
2. Prepare salmon: Using a sharp knife, remove any whitish fat from the belly of the fillet. Cut the fillet into 4 equal pieces. Using a sharp knife, make 4-5 shallow slashes about 1 inch apart along the skin side of each piece, being careful not to cut into the flesh. This helps the skin crisp and prevents curling.
3. Season: Pat salmon pieces completely dry with paper towels. Rub fillets evenly with olive oil on all sides. Season generously with salt and pepper.
4. Roast: Reduce oven temperature to 275 degrees F. Carefully remove the hot baking sheet from the oven. Place salmon pieces skin-side down on the hot sheet, spacing them at least 1 inch apart.
5. Cook: Return baking sheet to oven. Roast until the centers of the thickest part of the fillets are still slightly translucent when cut into with a paring knife, or until an instant-read thermometer inserted in the thickest part registers 125 degrees F. This takes 9-13 minutes depending on thickness.
6. Serve: Transfer salmon to plates immediately. The residual heat will continue cooking the fish slightly. Serve with your choice of sauce or relish. Why This Works This hybrid roasting method starts with a preheated hot pan and high oven temperature, then immediately drops to low heat. The initial blast of high heat firms the exterior and renders excess fat, while the gentle low-temperature cooking keeps the interior moist and silky. Serving Suggestions - Roasted broccoli or asparagus - Quinoa or rice - Lemon wedges - Fresh dill or herb sauce - Cucumber-dill yogurt sauce

## Notes

- For kids: Serve plain with lemon, or offer an alternative protein
- Doneness: 125 degrees F gives medium/medium-rare salmon. Cook to 130-135 degrees F for more well-done.
- Buying tip: For uniform cooking, buy a whole center-cut fillet and cut into 4 pieces yourself
- Skin: Keep skin on during cooking for best results; remove after if preferred
