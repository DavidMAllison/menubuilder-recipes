# Baked Chermoula Chicken Thighs

## Ingredients

**Chermoula Paste:**
- 1 cup cilantro, packed
- 1 cup parsley, packed
- 1 tablespoon ground cumin
- 1 tablespoon paprika
- 1 teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
- 1/4 to 1/2 teaspoon cayenne
- 4 large garlic cloves
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 tablespoon white wine vinegar
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice

**Chicken:**
- 1 tablespoon avocado or olive oil
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 3 pounds bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (about 6-8 pieces)

## Instructions

1. Combine all chermoula paste ingredients in a blender and process into a thick, slightly grainy paste, adjusting oil as needed.
2. Preheat oven to 400°F. Grease a 9x13-inch baking dish.
3. Season chicken thighs on both sides with salt and pepper.
4. Warm oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Place chicken skin-side down and cook about 5 minutes until golden, then flip and cook 3-4 minutes on the other side.
5. Transfer browned thighs to the baking dish and coat thoroughly with the chermoula paste.
6. Bake 25-30 minutes until internal temperature reaches 165°F.
7. Broil 1-2 minutes to crisp the skin. Garnish with fresh herbs and serve.
