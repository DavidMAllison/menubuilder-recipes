# Barley and Lamb Stew

## Ingredients

- 2 pounds boneless lamb shoulder, trimmed of fat and cut into bite-size pieces
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 1 tablespoon all-purpose flour
- 1 tablespoon olive oil, divided
- 3 medium carrots, cut into 1/4-inch rounds
- 1 cup barley grits
- 4 cups chicken broth
- 2 teaspoons freshly chopped oregano

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, combine the lamb with salt, pepper, and flour, then toss until all pieces are evenly coated.
2. Add 1/2 tablespoon of olive oil to a 4- to 5-quart Dutch oven and heat on medium-high until you see the oil shimmer across the surface. Brown half of the lamb pieces in batches, working on all sides for approximately 7 to 8 minutes, then move to a separate bowl.
3. Using the remaining 1/2 tablespoon of oil, repeat the browning procedure with the other half of the lamb and transfer this batch to join the first in the bowl.
4. Add the carrots to the empty pot and sauté with occasional stirring for 3 to 4 minutes, allowing them to start becoming tender.
5. Add the grits to the pot along with all the browned lamb and mix everything together thoroughly.
6. Add the chicken broth and bring the mixture to a boil.
7. Lower the heat to low, cover the pot with its lid, and simmer the stew gently for 30 to 45 minutes until the broth has been fully absorbed and the lamb and grits are both tender.
8. Just before serving, scatter the oregano over the stew and gently incorporate it.
9. Taste the stew and adjust the salt and pepper seasoning as needed.
10. Portion the stew into bowls and serve to your guests.
