# Lemon-Pepper Chicken

## Ingredients

- 1 medium lemon (5 ounces; 141g)
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour (2 1/4 ounces; 64g)
- 4 boneless, skin-on chicken breasts (12 ounces; 340g), patted dry
- 2 3/4 teaspoons (10g) Diamond Crystal kosher salt, divided; for table salt, use half as much by volume or same weight
- 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper, divided, plus more for garnish
- 1/4 cup (60ml) extra-virgin olive oil
- 1/3 cup (90ml) dry white wine
- 4 tablespoons unsalted butter (2 ounces; 57g), cubed
- 1 tablespoon fresh thyme leaves, plus more for garnish

## Instructions

1. Position oven rack in the middle and heat to 400°F (205ºC). Using a microplane grater, produce 2 teaspoons of lemon zest and keep it aside. Cut the lemon in half widthwise; store this separately from the zest.

2. Distribute flour evenly across the bottom of a wide, shallow bowl. Season chicken with 2 1/2 teaspoons salt and 3/4 teaspoon black pepper, distributing evenly. Take each chicken breast individually, coat it in flour, tap away any excess, place on a plate, and continue this process with the remaining breasts; set aside.

3. Pour oil into a 12-inch enameled cast iron or stainless steel skillet and warm over medium heat until it begins to shimmer. Place the reserved lemon halves cut side down in the skillet and allow them to cook without moving for approximately 5 minutes until the edges turn brown. Retrieve the lemon halves and set them aside. Turn the heat up to medium-high, add the chicken skin side down to the skillet, and cook while rotating the pan as necessary until the skin achieves a golden, crispy appearance, between 6 to 8 minutes. Using tongs, turn each chicken breast over so the skin faces upward, then return the lemon halves to the skillet with cut sides facing down. Take the skillet off the heat.

4. Transfer the skillet to the oven and roast until an instant-read thermometer inserted into the thickest section of chicken shows 160°F (71ºC), roughly 15 to 18 minutes. Allow the chicken to rest for 5 minutes; the temperature will climb to 165°F (74ºC). Move chicken breasts to a serving platter; cover loosely with foil to retain warmth while you prepare the sauce. Squeeze the caramelized lemon halves to collect 2 tablespoons of juice; reserve this juice. Leave the skillet unwashed.

5. To prepare the Sauce: As the chicken rests, place the skillet back on medium-high heat. Incorporate the remaining 1/4 teaspoon each of salt and pepper into the pan drippings and stir nonstop for 1 minute. Pour in wine and stir without pause until the volume reduces by half, approximately 2 minutes.

6. Introduce butter and whisk continuously until it melts completely, roughly 1 minute. Reduce to medium heat and continue whisking constantly to achieve a smooth, emulsified sauce, about 1 minute. (The finished sauce should coat a spoon's back, and a line drawn through it with your finger should remain visible.) Incorporate the reserved lemon juice and thyme by whisking. Pour sauce over the chicken and top with additional freshly ground black pepper, thyme, and the lemon zest you set aside initially.
