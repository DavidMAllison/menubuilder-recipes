# Filipino Chicken Adobo

## Ingredients

- 8 (5- to 7-ounce) bone-in chicken thighs, trimmed
- 1/3 cup soy sauce
- 1 (13.5-ounce) can coconut milk
- 3/4 cup cider vinegar
- 8 garlic cloves, peeled
- 4 bay leaves
- 2 teaspoons pepper
- 1 scallion, sliced thin

## Instructions

1. Combine chicken with soy sauce in a large bowl and refrigerate at least 30 minutes and up to 1 hour.
2. Remove chicken from the marinade, letting excess drip back into the bowl; reserve the soy sauce. Place chicken skin-side down in a 12-inch nonstick skillet.
3. Heat skillet over medium-high heat and brown the chicken skin until golden, 7 to 10 minutes. Meanwhile, whisk the coconut milk, vinegar, garlic, bay leaves, and pepper into the reserved soy sauce.
4. Transfer chicken to a plate and discard fat from the skillet. Return chicken skin-side down to the skillet, add the coconut milk mixture, and bring to a boil.
5. Lower heat to medium-low and simmer uncovered 20 minutes. Flip chicken skin-side up and continue cooking until the thickest part registers 175°F, about 15 minutes more. Move chicken to a platter and cover loosely with foil.
6. Remove bay leaves and skim fat from the surface of the sauce. Return skillet to medium-high heat and cook the sauce until thickened, 5 to 7 minutes.
7. Pour sauce over chicken, garnish with scallion, and serve.
