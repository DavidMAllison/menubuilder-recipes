# Smoked Pulled Pork

## Notes

- Fat cap up — the fat bastes the meat as it renders down
- The "butter test" matters more than the number — trust the feel
- Can rest in a cooler (still wrapped) for up to 2 hours if timing needs to flex
- Serve with coleslaw, pickles, and white bread or brioche buns

## Ingredients

**Pork**
- 8-10 lb bone-in pork shoulder (Boston butt)

**Rub**
- Kosher salt
- Coarse black pepper
(equal parts — smoke is the flavor)

---

## Instructions

1. Season pork shoulder generously with equal parts kosher salt and coarse black pepper on all sides.

2. Set smoker to ~270°F.

3. Place shoulder fat-cap up and smoke uncovered. Plan roughly 4-5 hours for this phase.

4. Watch the fat cap — when it splits open, wrap tightly in foil. This is your signal, not a timer.

5. Return to smoker wrapped and cook an additional 2-4 hours until internal temp reaches 200-205°F. Note: temp will continue to rise during rest, so pull at 200-205°F.

6. Check doneness with the thermometer probe test — slide it through the meat and it should go in with zero resistance. Any pull or drag means it needs more time. Trust the feel over the number.

7. Rest for at least 30 minutes (wrapped, in a cooler if possible) before pulling.

8. Pull by hand, removing any large fat pieces. Mix in any accumulated juices from the wrap.
