# Lemon-Herb Chicken with Zucchini

## Notes

Claude-original flavor combo (David's protein + sauce + veg formula), not yet cooked by the family. Mild -- no heat, whole family can eat the same sauced portion. Protein-flexible: chicken, shrimp, or salmon. Add feedback here after the first try.

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs protein of choice (chicken breast or thighs, shrimp, or salmon), cut into bite-size pieces or fillets
- 1 tbsp olive oil (for cooking)
- to taste kosher salt and black pepper
- 3 tbsp olive oil (for sauce)
- 3 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp lemon zest
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp honey
- 2 medium zucchini, sliced into half-moons

**Optional / For Serving:**

- crumbled feta
- fresh parsley, chopped

## Instructions

1. Whisk together olive oil, lemon juice, zest, garlic, oregano, and honey in a small bowl. Set aside.
2. Season protein with salt and pepper. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook protein until browned and cooked through -- chicken about 3-4 minutes per side (165°F), shrimp about 1-2 minutes per side until pink, salmon about 3-4 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate.
3. Add zucchini to the same skillet; saute 4-5 minutes until crisp-tender and lightly golden.
4. Pull out plain protein and zucchini for the kids before saucing -- this one's mild, so it's fine as-is for them too if they'd rather have the sauce.
5. Return protein and zucchini to the skillet, pour in the lemon-herb sauce, and toss to coat, 1 minute until warmed through. Serve topped with feta and parsley for the adults.
