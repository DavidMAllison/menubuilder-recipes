# Tacos De Alambre De Carne Con Chorizo Y Queso

## Notes

- Kids: pull plain beef before adding chorizo and cheese back to the pan.
- Oaxaca cheese is key — mozzarella works as a substitute.
- Cook beef in batches so it browns rather than steams.

Adapted from [Pati Jinich](https://patijinich.com/tacos-de-alambre-de-carne-con-chorizo-y-queso/)

## Ingredients

### Beef Marinade
- 1½ lbs beef top sirloin, sliced into ½" steaks, cut into 1" pieces
- 2 tablespoons lime juice
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon coarsely ground black pepper

### Filling
- 8 oz thick bacon, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 8 oz Mexican-style chorizo, casings removed, chopped
- 1 white onion, halved and slivered (about 2 cups)
- 1 red bell pepper, stemmed, seeded, cut into matchsticks
- 1 poblano chile, stemmed, seeded, cut into matchsticks
- 12 oz Oaxaca cheese, pulled apart or cut into small pieces

### To Serve
- Corn tortillas
- Salsa or guacamole

## Instructions

1. Combine lime juice, soy sauce, Worcestershire sauce, salt, and pepper in a large bowl. Add beef and marinate at least 15 minutes.
2. In a large cast iron pan, cook bacon over medium-high until browned and crispy, 4–5 minutes. Add chorizo and brown in the rendered fat. Transfer bacon and chorizo to a bowl with a slotted spoon, leaving fat in pan.
3. Cook onion, bell pepper, and poblano in the same pan until softened, 3–4 minutes. Transfer to the bacon bowl.
4. Increase heat to high. Cook beef in batches without overcrowding, about 4 minutes per batch, stirring and flipping as it browns. Transfer each batch to a bowl.
5. Return all meat, vegetables, chorizo, and bacon to the pan over low heat. Scatter cheese throughout and cover until melted.
6. Heat corn tortillas on a comal or skillet over medium heat until lightly browned. Assemble tacos and top with salsa or guacamole.
