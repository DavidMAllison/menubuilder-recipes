# Tuscan-Style Roast Pork with Garlic and Rosemary (Arista)

**Time**: 2½ hours (plus 1 hour resting before roasting)
**Servings**: 6

## Ingredients

- 1 lemon
- ⅓ cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 8 garlic cloves, minced
- ¼ teaspoon red pepper flakes
- 1 tablespoon chopped fresh rosemary
- 2 ounces pancetta, cut into ½-inch pieces
- 1 (2½-lb) boneless center-cut pork loin roast, trimmed
- Kosher salt

## Instructions

1. Grate 1 teaspoon lemon zest; halve and reserve the lemon. Combine zest, oil, garlic, and pepper flakes in a small skillet and cook over medium-low, stirring, until garlic is sizzling, about 3 minutes. Add rosemary and cook 30 seconds more. Strain through a fine-mesh strainer into a bowl, pressing on the solids to extract the oil. Reserve the oil; let the garlic-rosemary paste cool.
2. Process pancetta in a food processor until a smooth paste forms, 20–30 seconds. Add the cooled garlic-rosemary paste and process until combined, scraping down the bowl as needed.
3. Butterfly the roast: with the fat side up, make a horizontal cut one-third up from the bottom along one long side, stopping ½ inch from the edge. Open the flap. Make a second horizontal cut through the thicker portion, again stopping ½ inch from the edge. Open this flap to create an even, flat surface. Pound any thick spots flat. Season both sides with 1 tablespoon kosher salt, rubbing it in. Spread the inside evenly with the pancetta paste, leaving a ¼-inch border. Roll up tightly from a short side (keeping the fat on the outside) and tie at 1-inch intervals with kitchen twine.
4. Set a wire rack over a rimmed baking sheet, spray with oil, and place the roast fat-side up on the rack. Refrigerate for 1 hour.
5. Heat oven to 275°F. Roast until the center registers 135°F, 1½–2 hours. Rest, covered loosely, for 20 minutes.
6. Heat 1 teaspoon of the reserved oil in a skillet over high. Place the lemon halves cut-side down and cook until browned and softened, 3–4 minutes. Set aside.
7. Pat the roast dry. Heat 2 tablespoons reserved oil in the same skillet over high. Brown the roast on the fat side and all sides except the bottom, 4–6 minutes. Transfer to a carving board and remove the twine.
8. Once cool enough to handle, squeeze the grilled lemons through a strainer into the remaining oil. Whisk together. Slice the roast ¼ inch thick and serve with the lemon vinaigrette.
