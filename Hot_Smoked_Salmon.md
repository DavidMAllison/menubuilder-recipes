# Hot Smoked Salmon

**Time**: 30 hours  
**Adapted from**: [Alton Brown](https://altonbrown.com/recipes/hot-smoked-salmon-2/)  

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup kosher salt
- 1 quart water
- 2 pounds salmon filets, 4.5-6 ounces each, skin on, pin bones removed

## Instructions

1. In a 4-quart container, stir salt and water together for 1 to 2 minutes until the salt dissolves completely, then fully immerse the salmon filets in the brine. Put a lid on and place in the refrigerator for 3 hours.

2. Take the salmon out of the brine, give it a thorough rinse, and use paper towels to remove excess moisture. Position it skin-side down on a wire rack set above a half-sheet pan, then refrigerate until the skin becomes shiny and tacky to the touch, approximately 21 to 24 hours.

3. Starting on the second day, heat your smoker with dry chips set to maintain a temperature between 150 and 160ºF. Position the salmon skin-side down on the smoking racks, leaving 1/4 inch between each piece, then move it into the smoker. Tend to the temperature throughout the cooking process, smoking for 2 1/2 to 3 hours until the flesh is fully cooked, the outer surface has darkened, and the smoke flavor reaches your preference.

4. Keep leftover salmon in a sealed container in the refrigerator for as long as 1 week, alternatively you can vacuum-seal it and refrigerate it for as long as 1 month.
