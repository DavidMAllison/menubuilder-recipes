# Baked Chicken Parmesan

## Ingredients

- 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (about 1.5-2 lbs)
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 large eggs
- 2 tablespoons milk
- 1.5 cups Italian-style breadcrumbs
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon dried Italian seasoning
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- Cooking spray or olive oil
- 2 cups marinara sauce
- 1.5 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
- Fresh basil for garnish (optional)

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper or lightly grease it.
2. If chicken breasts are thick, pound to an even 1/2-inch thickness. Pat dry.
3. Set up breading station: flour in a shallow dish; eggs whisked with milk in a second dish; breadcrumbs combined with Parmesan, garlic powder, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper in a third dish.
4. Dredge each breast in flour, dip in egg, then press into breadcrumb mixture to coat both sides. Place on prepared baking sheet.
5. Lightly spray or brush tops with oil. Bake 20-25 minutes until golden brown and cooked through (165°F). For kid-friendly layered cooking, remove 1-2 plain breaded pieces now for children.
6. Remove pan from oven. Spoon marinara over remaining chicken (about 1/2 cup per breast). Top with shredded mozzarella.
7. Return to oven and bake 8-10 minutes until cheese is melted and bubbly.
8. Let rest 3-5 minutes. Garnish with fresh basil. Serve plain breaded chicken to kids; serve topped chicken parmesan to adults with pasta or salad.
