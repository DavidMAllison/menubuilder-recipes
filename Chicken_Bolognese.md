# Chicken Bolognese

## Ingredients

- 1 pound pasta (paccheri or any tubular shape)
- 3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 large onion, chopped fine
- 3 medium carrots, peeled and chopped fine
- 3 celery ribs, chopped fine
- 1 pound ground chicken (not ground chicken breast)
- 1/4 cup tomato paste
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 teaspoons fresh thyme leaves
- 2 teaspoons kosher salt, plus salt for cooking pasta
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes
- 1 cup dry red wine
- 1 (28-ounce) can crushed tomatoes
- 1 cup whole milk
- 2 ounces Parmesan cheese, grated (1 cup), plus extra for serving
- 1/3 cup chopped fresh basil leaves, plus extra for serving
- 2 teaspoons red wine vinegar (optional)

## Before You Begin

Be sure to use ground chicken, not ground chicken breast (also labeled 99 percent fat-free) for a rich-tasting dish. You can substitute 1 teaspoon of dried thyme for the fresh thyme.

## Instructions

1. Bring 3 quarts water to boil in large pot. Add pasta and 2 tablespoons kosher salt and cook, stirring frequently, until al dente. Reserve 1 cup cooking water, then drain pasta and return it to pot; cover to keep warm.
2. Meanwhile, heat olive oil in large Dutch oven over medium-low heat until shimmering. Add onion, carrots, and celery and cook, stirring occasionally, until just tender, 8 to 10 minutes.
3. Increase heat to medium and add ground chicken, breaking up meat into very small pieces with wooden spoon, until no longer pink, 5 to 7 minutes. Stir in tomato paste, garlic, thyme, 2 teaspoons kosher salt, oregano, and red pepper flakes, and cook over medium heat, until dark fond begins to form on bottom of pot, 2 to 4 minutes.
4. Add red wine and simmer until liquid has mostly evaporated, 1 to 2 minutes. Add crushed tomatoes and milk and bring to boil over medium-high heat. Reduce heat to low and simmer, uncovered, stirring occasionally, until sauce thickens slightly, 15 to 20 minutes.
5. Reduce heat to low and stir in Parmesan, pasta, and 1/2 cup reserved pasta water. Off heat, stir in basil and red wine vinegar (if using). Season with salt and pepper flakes to taste. Serve, passing additional Parmesan separately.
