# Creamy Cannellini Bean Soup with Maltagliati

## Ingredients

- Maltagliati pasta 11.5 oz
- Dried cannellini beans 2 cups
- Carrots 4 oz
- Yellow onions 2.5 oz
- Celery (1 stalk) 0.7 cup
- Vegetable broth 2 cups
- Juniper berries 1
- Fresh chili pepper to taste
- Fennel seeds 1 tsp
- Bay leaves 1 leaf
- Wild fennel to taste
- Extra virgin olive oil 1 ½ tbsp
- Fine salt to taste
- Black pepper to taste
- Celery the leaves 1 ½ tbsp
- Extra virgin olive oil 1.3 tbsp

## Instructions

1. To prepare the creamy cannellini bean soup with maltagliati, first place the dried cannellini beans in a large bowl, cover them with plenty of cold water and soak overnight. The next day, rinse them, drain and set aside. At this point prepare the Vegetable broth . Once ready, strain it if necessary and keep it warm so it b4s ready to use during cooking. Peel the carrot and cut it into 3/16-inch (about.2 in) cubes. Do the same with the onion and the celery stalks. Also set aside a handful of the celery b4s green leaves; we b4ll use them later.
2. Pour a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil into a large saucepan and warm it over low heat. Add the carrot, celery and onion. Add a bay leaf as well and gently soften the soffritto, stirring occasionally, until the vegetables are soft and fragrant without taking on color. Then add the drained cannellini beans.
3. Stir to coat them, add the salt and cover with the hot vegetable broth. Add or 2 juniper berries.
4. Add the fennel seeds as well, stir and continue cooking over very low heat for about an hour and a half, stirring occasionally and adding broth as needed. To obtain an extra-creamy soup you can extend the cooking up to 3 hours, adding more broth. Meanwhile prepare the celery oil: collect the celery b4s green leaves in the jar of a blender and add the extra virgin olive oil.
5. Blend until you obtain a smooth mixture. Strain the oil through a fine-mesh sieve and set it aside.
6. The soup will be ready when the beans have broken down, helping to make the soup naturally thick and creamy. At this point, add the maltagliati directly into the pot and let them cook in the soup liquid, stirring occasionally; it will take about 6 minutes. Add more broth if needed. Also add a pinch of chili flakes.
7. At the end of cooking, adjust the salt and pepper. Add some chopped fennel fronds and stir again.
8. Plate and finish with a few drops of the celery oil, a grind of freshly ground black pepper and, if you like, some fennel fronds. Serve the creamy cannellini bean soup with maltagliati piping hot 24 !
