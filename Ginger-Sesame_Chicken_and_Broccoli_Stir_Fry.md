# Ginger-Sesame Chicken and Broccoli Stir Fry

Time: 30 minutes
Yield: 4 to 5 servings

## Ingredients

**Sauce (mix ahead):**
- 3 tablespoons low-sodium soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
- 1 tablespoon toasted sesame oil
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 2 teaspoons cornstarch
- 1/4 cup low-sodium chicken broth or water
- 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, grated
- 4 cloves garlic, minced

**Stir Fry:**
- 1.5 lbs boneless chicken thighs, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 1 large head broccoli, cut into florets
- 2 medium carrots, sliced thin on the bias
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 1 cup snap peas
- 2 tablespoons neutral oil (avocado or vegetable)
- Brown rice, for serving
- Green onions and sesame seeds, for garnish

## Instructions

1. Whisk all sauce ingredients together in a small bowl and set aside.
2. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat. Add chicken in a single layer. Do not stir for 2-3 minutes. Flip and cook through, 4-5 minutes total. Remove and set aside.
3. Add remaining oil to the pan. Cook carrots 2 minutes. Add broccoli and bell pepper; cook 3-4 minutes until just tender but still bright. Add snap peas and cook 1 minute more.
4. Return chicken to the pan. Pour sauce over everything. Toss and cook 1-2 minutes until sauce thickens and coats everything.
5. Remove from heat. Garnish with green onions and sesame seeds. Serve over brown rice.

## Notes

- Stores well for 4 days in the fridge. Keep rice stored separately to prevent sogginess.
- Slightly undercook vegetables so they hold up after reheating.
- For lower sodium: use tamari or coconut aminos instead of soy sauce.
