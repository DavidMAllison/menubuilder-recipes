# Pulled Pork Tacos

## Ingredients

### Quick Pickled Onions (make ahead or day of)
- 1 medium red onion, thinly sliced
- 1/2 cup apple cider vinegar
- 1/2 cup water
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt

### Tacos
- 1.5 lbs smoked pulled pork (pre-cooked, thawed)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 8 corn tortillas
- 1/2 head green cabbage, shredded (or coleslaw mix)
- 1/2 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
- 2 limes, cut into wedges
- salsa or pico de gallo, for serving
- sour cream or crema, for serving (optional)

## Instructions

1. **Pickled onions**: Combine vinegar, water, sugar, and salt in a small saucepan and bring to a simmer until sugar dissolves. Pour over sliced onions in a jar or bowl. Let sit at least 20 minutes (or refrigerate overnight).

2. **Reheat pork**: Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add pulled pork and season with cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, and pepper. Toss to coat and cook until heated through and slightly crispy on the edges, 5-8 minutes. Adjust seasoning to taste.

3. **Warm tortillas**: Heat corn tortillas directly over a gas burner or in a dry skillet until lightly charred, 30-60 seconds per side. Keep warm wrapped in a towel.

4. **Assemble**: Fill each tortilla with pulled pork, cabbage, pickled onions, and cilantro. Serve with lime wedges and salsa.

## Notes

- Kids get plain warmed pulled pork in a flour tortilla — skip the pickled onions and cabbage.
- The pickled onions can be made up to a week ahead and refrigerated.
- Add hot sauce or sliced jalapeños on the side for adults who want heat.
- Cotija cheese crumbled on top is a nice addition.
