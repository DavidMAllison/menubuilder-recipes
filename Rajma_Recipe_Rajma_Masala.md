# Rajma Recipe (Rajma Masala)

## Ingredients

- 1 cup rajma  ((dry kidney beans or 2 – 15 Oz canned beans (3 cups)))
- 2 to 3 tablespoons ghee  ((or oil))
- 1 small bay leaf ((optional))
- ½ teaspoon cumin seeds ((jeera, optional))
- 1 cup (120 grams)  fine chopped onions ( (or ¾ cup grated or cube, boil &amp; puree))
- 1  green chilli (( chopped or slit, optional))
- 1½ teaspoon ginger garlic paste  ((or 1 teaspoon each minced &amp; crushed))
- 1½ cups (2 large, 300 grams)  tomatoes  ((chopped &amp; pureed, or 1 cup canned tomato puree))
- ½ to 1 teaspoon red chilli powder  ((Kashmiri or byadgi))
- ¾ teaspoon garam masala ( (¼ to ½ teaspoon for later))
- 1½ to 2 teaspoons coriander powder
- ¾ to 1 teaspoon cumin powder
- ¼ teaspoon turmeric
- ½ teaspoon salt  ((½ teaspoon more to adjust))
- ½ to 1 tablespoon kasuri methi  ((dried fenugreek leaves))
- Few Ginger juliennes ( (optional))
- 2 tablespoons coriander leaves  ((fine chopped))
- 2 to 3 tablespoons heavy cream  ((Optional))

## Instructions

1. Put the rajma into a large bowl, then rinse it three separate times. Submerge it in 4 cups of water and allow it to sit uncovered for no less than 6 hours or throughout the night. After soaking is complete, empty out all the water and give it another thorough rinse.

2. Move the beans into your pressure cooker (either stovetop or instant pot). Pour in 1½ cups water and seal with pressure until the beans become soft, which requires 4 to 5 whistles for a stovetop cooker or 12 to 16 minutes for an instant pot, varying based on how old the rajma is.

3. Your finished beans should have a soft texture while staying intact as individual beans. When you squeeze a bean between your fingers, it should crumble easily.

4. Heat the ghee or oil in a pot. Add the cumin seeds and bay leaf. When they start crackling, add the onions and green chili and stir.

5. Let everything cook at medium heat until the onions become golden in color. Next, put in the ginger-garlic paste and cook for roughly one minute to release the aromas.

6. Add the tomato puree and allow it to cook for approximately 3 minutes so the onion and tomato mixture becomes thicker.

7. Combine your spices—red chili powder, garam masala, coriander powder, cumin powder, and turmeric—into the pot. Let them cook for 2 to 3 minutes until they release their fragrance.

8. Add the cooked rajma along with its cooking liquid. Mix in extra hot water to your preference (around ½ to 1 cup) to reach a sauce-like texture. (If using canned beans instead, rinse and drain them well before incorporating.)

9. Mix everything together and let it bubble gently for 10 to 15 minutes until the sauce thickens somewhat. If you want it thicker still, crush about a tablespoon of beans and return them to the pot.

10. Taste and correct the seasoning by adding more salt and ¼ teaspoon garam masala if needed, along with crushed kasuri methi. You have the choice to stir in cream and garnish with fresh coriander and thinly cut ginger. You could also finish it dhaba-style with a tadka or make it creamy like a restaurant version.

11. Squeeze fresh lemon juice in just before eating. Serve with steamed chawal (rice).

12. Set your instant pot to Saute mode and pour ghee into the steel insert. When the ghee becomes liquid, add cumin seeds, bay leaf, onions, green chili, and salt.

13. Keep stirring for about 5 minutes to cook everything evenly until the onions turn golden and start to caramelize, being careful not to burn them.

14. Add the ginger-garlic paste and cook for 30 seconds to 1 minute.

15. Stir in all your spice powders: chili powder, garam masala, turmeric, coriander powder, and cumin powder.

16. Turn off Saute mode. Place the cooked rajma into the pot and pour 2 cups water over it. Scrape the bottom of the pot to loosen any stuck-on brown pieces.

17. Place the trivet inside and set the bowl with tomato puree on top of it. Cover the instant pot with the lid and pressure cook for 18 minutes (or 16 minutes if you prefer beans that are firmer).

18. Once the pressure has come down, open the instant pot with care. Your rajma should be extremely tender while keeping its shape. Pour in the tomato puree and cook with sautéing for 2 to 3 minutes. If you prefer thicker rajma, partially break down some of the beans. (Add ½ to ¾ cup water instead if a thinner sauce is what you want.)

19. Taste your dish and season again with more salt, ¼ teaspoon garam masala, optional cream, and crushed kasuri methi. Switch off the Saute function.

20. Scatter fresh coriander leaves and thin strips of ginger on top. Serve with steamed chawal. Finish by drizzling fresh lemon juice over everything.
