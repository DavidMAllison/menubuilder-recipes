# Sweet and Sour Mexican Style Fish (Pescado Agridulce)

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 lbs white fish fillets (cod, tilapia, or snapper)
- 4 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
- 6 oz tomatillos, husked and chopped
- 1/4 cup slivered almonds
- 1/2 cup green olives
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1/4 tsp ground allspice
- 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp table salt, divided

## Instructions

1. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a large skillet over medium. Add onion and cook until wilting. Stir in garlic and cook 1 minute. Add tomatoes, tomatillos, 1/2 tsp salt, sugar, allspice, and cinnamon. Simmer 5 minutes.

2. Add almonds and olives; cook 7-8 minutes. Turn off heat.

3. Season fish with remaining 1/2 tsp salt. Heat remaining 2 tbsp oil in a casserole or large skillet over medium-high. Add fish skin-side down and cook 1-2 minutes until browned.

4. Scrape sauce over fish, cover, and reduce heat to low. Cook 10 minutes until fish is cooked through. Serve over rice or potatoes.

## Notes

- Kid-friendly: Zero chili peppers — warmth from allspice and cinnamon only. Pull plain fish for kids before adding sauce.
