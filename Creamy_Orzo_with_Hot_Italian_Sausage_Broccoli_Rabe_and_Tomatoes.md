# Creamy Orzo with Hot Italian Sausage, Broccoli Rabe, and Tomatoes

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1 large shallot, minced
- 1 1/4 teaspoons table salt
- 1 teaspoon minced garlic
- 1 1/2 cups orzo
- 1/2 cup dry white wine or vermouth
- 3 cups water
- 2 cups chicken broth
- 8 ounces hot Italian sausage, casings removed
- 8 ounces broccoli rabe, trimmed and cut into 1-inch pieces
- 6 ounces cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 ounce Pecorino Romano cheese, grated (1/2 cup), plus extra for serving
- 3 tablespoons chopped pepperoncini

## Before You Begin

Sweet Italian sausage may be substituted. Do not dry the broccoli rabe after washing; the surface moisture will provide just enough steam for it to cook. Broccolini may be substituted for broccoli rabe.

## Instructions

1. Melt butter in medium saucepan over medium heat. Add shallot and salt; cook, stirring frequently, until shallot is softened but not browned, 3 to 5 minutes.
2. Add garlic and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Add orzo and stir until evenly coated. Add white wine and cook, stirring constantly, until fully absorbed, about 30 seconds.
3. Stir in water and chicken broth. Increase heat and bring to boil; reduce heat to maintain vigorous simmer and cook, stirring occasionally, until liquid is reduced and thickened, and orzo is al dente, about 15 minutes (if orzo starts to stick toward end of cooking time, reduce heat and stir more frequently). Season with salt to taste.
4. Meanwhile, place sausage in cold 12-inch nonstick skillet. Adjust heat to medium-high and cook, using wooden spoon to break meat into pieces no larger than 1/2 inch, until sausage is no longer pink, 6 to 7 minutes. Stir in broccoli rabe. Cover and cook, stirring occasionally, until broccoli rabe is crisp-tender, about 3 minutes. Stir in cherry tomatoes.
5. Stir Pecorino into orzo and transfer to wide serving bowl. Top with sausage mixture; sprinkle with pepperoncini; and serve, passing extra Pecorino separately.
