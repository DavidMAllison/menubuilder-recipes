# Grilled Baby Back Ribs (3-2-1 Method)

## Ingredients

- 2 racks baby back ribs (about 4-5 lbs total)
- 2 tablespoons brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon paprika
- 2 teaspoons garlic powder
- 2 teaspoons onion powder
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional)
- 1 cup BBQ sauce (your favorite brand)
- 1/4 cup apple juice or water (for foil wrap)

## Instructions

1. Prep ribs: Remove membrane from back of ribs by sliding a knife under it at one end, then gripping with a paper towel and pulling it off. Pat ribs dry.
2. Make dry rub: Combine brown sugar, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, salt, and cayenne. Rub all over both sides of ribs. Let sit at room temperature 30 minutes.
3. **3 hours — smoke/indirect heat**: Set up grill for indirect heat (225-250°F). If using charcoal, add wood chunks (hickory or apple); if gas, use a smoker box. Place ribs bone-side down over indirect heat. Cook 3 hours, maintaining temperature.
4. **2 hours — wrapped**: Remove ribs from grill. Place each rack on a large sheet of foil. Splash apple juice over the ribs, then wrap tightly. Return to grill bone-side down over indirect heat. Cook 2 hours.
5. **1 hour — sauced and uncovered**: Carefully unwrap ribs (watch for steam). Return to grill bone-side down over indirect heat. Brush generously with BBQ sauce every 20 minutes for 1 hour, until sauce is sticky and caramelized.
6. Rest: Let ribs rest 10 minutes. Cut between bones into individual ribs. Serve with extra BBQ sauce, coleslaw, and cornbread.

## Notes

- 3-2-1 method is a guideline — check for tenderness (meat should pull back from bones about 1/4 inch and bend easily when lifted from one end).
- Baby backs cook faster than spare ribs; consider 2-2-1 if they seem done early.
