# Roast Pork Tenderloins, Chinese-Style

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 teaspoons five-spice powder
- 1 teaspoon table salt, plus more as needed
- 2 pounds pork tenderloins (2 loins, about 1 pound each), trimmed of silver skin and patted dry
- 4 teaspoons vegetable oil, divided
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- 1/4 cup hoisin sauce
- 2 medium cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, minced

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 425°F with a rack in the middle position. Combine five-spice powder and salt, and coat the tenderloins thoroughly with the mixture.
2. Heat 2 teaspoons oil in a large ovenproof skillet over medium-high heat until smoking. Add tenderloins and brown on one side, 1 to 1 1/2 minutes, then rotate a quarter-turn and brown again. Repeat twice more until all sides are well browned.
3. Transfer the skillet to the oven and cook until an instant-read thermometer registers 135-140°F, about 15-18 minutes. Move the meat to a cutting board and tent with foil.
4. While the meat roasts, combine honey, soy sauce, hoisin, and 1/2 cup water in a bowl.
5. Return the empty skillet to medium-high heat with the remaining 2 teaspoons oil. Add garlic and ginger; cook about 30 seconds until fragrant.
6. Add the sauce mixture and cook 3-4 minutes, stirring frequently and scraping up browned bits, until syrupy.
7. Return the pork to the pan and coat thoroughly with the sauce. Transfer to a cutting board, slice crosswise, arrange on a platter, and drizzle with the remaining sauce.
