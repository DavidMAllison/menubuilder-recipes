# Chicken and Broccoli

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 4 cups broccoli florets
- 3 tablespoons vegetable oil, divided
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, minced
- 1/3 cup soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons oyster sauce
- 2 tablespoons brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 1/2 cup water or chicken broth
- 1 teaspoon sesame oil
- Sesame seeds for garnish (optional)

## Instructions

1. Make the sauce: In a small bowl, whisk together soy sauce, oyster sauce, brown sugar, cornstarch, and water. Set aside.
2. Blanch broccoli: Bring a pot of water to boil. Add broccoli and cook 2 minutes. Drain and set aside.
3. Cook chicken: Heat 2 tablespoons oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Add chicken and cook 5-7 minutes until golden brown and cooked through. Remove to a plate.
4. Aromatics: In the same pan, add remaining 1 tablespoon oil. Add garlic and ginger, cook 30 seconds until fragrant.
5. Combine: Return chicken to pan. Add broccoli. Pour sauce over everything and toss to coat. Cook 2-3 minutes until sauce thickens and everything is heated through.
6. Finish: Drizzle with sesame oil and toss. Garnish with sesame seeds if desired. Serve over rice.
