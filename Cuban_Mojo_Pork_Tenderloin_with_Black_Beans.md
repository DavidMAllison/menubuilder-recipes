# Cuban Mojo Pork Tenderloin with Black Beans

## Ingredients

**Pork Marinade:**
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 1/4 cup orange juice
- 2 tablespoons lime juice
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon dried Mexican oregano
- 1 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 pound pork tenderloin, trimmed of silverskin
- 2 tablespoons olive oil, for searing

**Black Beans:**
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 1 small poblano pepper (or green bell pepper), chopped
- 2 large plum tomatoes, seeded and chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon ground cumin
- 2 teaspoons dried Mexican oregano
- 1/2 cup chicken broth
- 2 (15-ounce) cans black beans, drained and rinsed
- Hot sauce, to taste
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- 1/4 cup chopped cilantro

**Optional / For Serving:**

- Lime wedges, for serving

## Instructions

1. Combine 1/4 cup olive oil, orange juice, lime juice, garlic, cumin, oregano, red pepper flakes, and salt in a zipper-top bag.
2. Add the pork tenderloin, seal, and massage to coat thoroughly. Refrigerate 8-24 hours.
3. Remove pork from the marinade. Heat 2 tablespoons olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat and sear the pork on all sides.
4. Transfer to a baking sheet and roast at 350°F until internal temperature reaches 145°F. Tent with foil and rest 5 minutes, then slice against the grain.
5. Meanwhile, make the beans: heat 2 tablespoons olive oil in a pan over medium-high heat. Add onion and poblano; cook 5-6 minutes until softened.
6. Add tomatoes and cook 3-4 minutes. Stir in garlic, cumin, and oregano; cook 15 seconds until fragrant.
7. Add chicken broth and black beans; simmer 5-6 minutes.
8. Season with hot sauce, salt, and pepper; stir in cilantro. Serve pork alongside the beans with lime wedges.
