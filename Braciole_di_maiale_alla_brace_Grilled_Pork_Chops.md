# Braciole di maiale alla brace (Grilled Pork Chops)

## Ingredients

- 1-2 bone-in pork chops (see Notes)
- A sprig or two of fresh rosemary (just the leaves)
- A few sage leaves
- 1-2 cloves of garlic
- A large pinch of salt
- Freshly ground black pepper (to taste)
- 1-2 Tb leaf lard (brought to room temperature (or olive oil))

## Instructions

1. Finely mince the garlic and rosemary together with salt and pepper. Put this mixture into a small bowl and use a wooden spoon or spatula to combine it thoroughly with the lard (or oil as an alternative), stirring until you achieve a smooth paste with visible green and black specks throughout. Check the flavor and adjust—it should taste distinctly savory, so add additional salt or pepper as needed.

2. Pat both sides of the pork chop dry completely. (Drying the meat helps the paste stick properly.) Apply the paste generously to both sides of the chop. Cover it and allow at least one hour of resting time. Alternatively, refrigerate overnight; just be sure to remove the chops from the fridge a minimum of one hour prior to grilling.

3. Heat your barbecue to approximately 200C/400F—hot enough to cook properly but not excessively hot. If using charcoal, allow it to burn until it develops an ashy white appearance.

4. Place the chops on the grill and cook until they reach an internal done state and develop a golden brown crust on each side—approximately 3 minutes per side for thin chops, or 5 minutes per side for thicker cuts.

5. Allow the cooked chops several minutes of rest before plating and serving.
