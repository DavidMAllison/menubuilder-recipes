# Glazed Pork Chops with Sesame, Mirin, and Ginger

## Notes

Family hit — keeper. Glaze runs a bit sweet; next time toss in a couple dried red peppers to balance it out.

## Ingredients

### Glaze
- 1/2 cup rice vinegar, plus 2 teaspoons reserved
- 1/3 cup light brown sugar
- 3 tablespoons orange juice
- 2 tablespoons Dijon mustard
- 3 tablespoons mirin
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- 1 teaspoon finely grated fresh ginger

### Pork
- 4 boneless, center-cut pork loin chops, 5 to 7 ounces each, 1/2 to 3/4 inch thick
- Salt and ground black pepper
- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil

### Garnish
- 1 teaspoon sesame seeds
- 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil

## Instructions

1. Toast sesame seeds in a small dry skillet over medium heat, stirring frequently, until lightly browned and fragrant, 3 to 5 minutes; set aside. Combine all glaze ingredients except the reserved 2 teaspoons rice vinegar in a medium bowl; mix thoroughly and set aside. Trim the fat cap on each chop and slash through the fat and silver skin with a sharp knife, making 2 cuts about 2 inches apart — this prevents the chops from curling as they cook. Do not cut into the meat. Pat chops dry with paper towels; season with salt and pepper.
2. Heat oil in a heavy-bottomed 12-inch skillet over medium-high heat until smoking. Add pork and cook until well browned, 4 to 6 minutes. Turn chops and cook 1 minute longer; transfer to a plate and pour off any oil in the skillet. Return chops to the skillet browned side up and add the glaze mixture; cook over medium heat until the center of the chops registers 140°F on an instant-read thermometer, 5 to 8 minutes. Remove skillet from heat; transfer chops to a clean platter, tent with foil, and let rest 5 minutes.
3. When chops have rested, add any accumulated juices to the skillet and set over medium heat. Simmer, whisking constantly, until glaze is thick and the color of dark caramel (a heatproof spatula should leave a wide trail when dragged through), 2 to 6 minutes. Add the reserved 2 teaspoons rice vinegar and return chops to the skillet; turn to coat both sides with the glaze. Transfer chops back to the platter browned side up and spread remaining glaze over them. Garnish with sesame seeds and sesame oil. Serve immediately.
