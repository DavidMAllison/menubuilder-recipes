# Pan-Seared Broccolini with Pork and Oyster Sauce

## Ingredients

- 3 tablespoons oyster sauce
- 1/4 teaspoon Chinese five-spice powder
- 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 2 tablespoons neutral oil
- 1 lb ground pork
- 1 lb broccolini, cut into 2-inch lengths
- 4 scallions, thinly sliced on the diagonal

## Before You Begin

- Use a large skillet with a tight-fitting lid.
- Optional: top with a fried egg or two; serve with steamed rice.

## Instructions

1. Stir together oyster sauce, five-spice, 1/2 teaspoon pepper, and 1/3 cup water in a small bowl.
2. In a 12-inch skillet, heat the oil until shimmering. Add the pork and cook, breaking into small pieces, until browned and crisp. Transfer to a plate; leave fat in skillet.
3. Heat fat until barely smoking. Add broccolini and cook, stirring once or twice, until well charred.
4. Add the oyster sauce mixture, cover, and reduce to low. Cook until broccolini is tender.
5. Off heat, stir in the pork and scallions. Serve.
