# Crispy Chicken Thighs with “Creamy” Jalapeño Salsa

## Ingredients

- 2 tablepoons vegetable oil
- 4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
- 3 or 4 knob onions or green onions, cut into 1/2-inch slices
- 3 garlic cloves cut into 1/2-inch slices
- 3 fresh jalapeno chiles , stems removed
- 6 whole garlic cloves
- 1 cup olive oil
- Juice x of one lime
- Salt
- A few sprigs cilantro

## Instructions

1. Warm oil in a 10-inch skillet set to medium-high heat. Position chicken thighs with skin facing down in the pan and let them sear until the skin turns golden brown, taking about 7 to 8 minutes. Reduce heat to medium and flip the chicken over. Stir in the knob onions and garlic, then season with 1 teaspoon of salt. Allow to cook for an additional 7 to 8 minutes before removing the chicken to a plate.

2. Combine 2 cups of water with the jalapeños, garlic, and salt in a small sauce pot and bring to a boil over high heat. Reduce heat and let simmer gently until the garlic softens, approximately 10 minutes. Take the pot off the heat and allow the mixture to reach room temperature. Drain the liquid from the pot and finely mince the chiles and garlic. Put the minced mixture into a blender and blend on medium speed. While blending, slowly pour in the olive oil. Add a handful of cilantro sprigs and lime juice along with about 1/2 teaspoon of salt, blending until everything is incorporated.

3. Pull the chicken meat from the bones and cut it into 1/2-inch pieces. Return the shredded chicken to the skillet with the cooked onions and garlic and stir to combine. Distribute the mixture across warm tortillas and garnish each with a liberal amount of the jalapeño salsa and fresh cilantro.
