# Cast Iron Mustard Chicken

## Ingredients

- 2 split chicken breasts (bone-in, skin-on)
- 2 tablespoons Dijon mustard
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 2 whole garlic cloves
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Preheat cast iron skillet on stovetop over medium heat.
2. Mix Dijon mustard, olive oil, minced garlic, onion powder, salt, and pepper in a small bowl.
3. Using a spoon, spread mixture under the skin of each breast, then apply a thin coat on top of the skin.
4. Tuck one whole garlic clove under the skin of each breast.
5. Place chicken skin-side down in the hot cast iron. Sear 5 minutes until browned.
6. Flip chicken and transfer skillet to oven. Cook 35-40 minutes until internal temperature reaches 160°F.
7. Rest 5-10 minutes before serving.
