# Crispy Braised Chicken Thighs with Cabbage and Bacon

## Ingredients

- 2 1/2 pounds bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (6 to 8 thighs)
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
- 8 ounces slab bacon or salt pork, cut into 1/2- by 1/4-inch lardons
- 1 medium onion, thinly sliced (about 6 ounces)
- 1 pound finely shredded cabbage (about 1 medium head)
- 2 tablespoons whole-grain mustard
- 1/2 cup apple cider vinegar
- 1 cup homemade or store-bought low-sodium chicken stock
- 3 tablespoons sugar
- 2 bay leaves
- 6 sprigs thyme
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter

## Instructions

1. Position the oven rack in the center and set temperature to 350°F. Generously coat the chicken with salt and pepper. Warm oil in a 4- to 5-quart straight-sided sauté pan on medium-high heat until it shimmers. Place chicken skin side down in the pan and leave undisturbed while cooking until the skin becomes well-browned and crisp, approximately 8 minutes, reducing heat if smoking occurs. Turn the chicken over and allow the second side to brown lightly for about 3 minutes. Remove chicken and set aside on a large plate.

2. Put bacon into the pan and stir while cooking until it reaches a light brown stage, roughly 3 minutes. Pour off rendered fat until only 1/4 cup remains in the pan. Stir in the onion and let it soften and begin to brown while stirring, taking about 4 minutes. Add the cabbage, stirring occasionally until it too becomes soft and begins browning, around 4 minutes. Incorporate mustard and vinegar, stirring continuously while loosening the browned particles stuck to the pan bottom. Pour in chicken stock along with sugar, bay leaves, and thyme sprigs, then bring the mixture to a gentle simmer.

3. Return the chicken to the pan by placing it among the vegetables with skin exposed above the liquid level while the majority of the meat is submerged. Place the pan in the oven and leave uncovered for cooking until the chicken reaches complete tenderness and the liquid volume decreases by approximately half, approximately 45 minutes.

4. Take the pan from the oven and move the chicken to a serving platter. Whisk butter into the pan's contents and adjust the seasoning with salt and pepper to your preference. Serve right away by ladling the cabbage mixture and its sauce into shallow bowls and arranging chicken pieces on top.
