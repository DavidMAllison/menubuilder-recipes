# Braised Pork Belly Tacos

## Notes

- **Make ahead:** Braise the day before. Refrigerate pork in the braising liquid — the fat solidifies and is easy to remove. Reheat gently in the liquid before serving, then broil for the crispy finish.
- **Chile sub:** If you can't find dried anchos, 1–2 chipotles in adobo work fine and add a smoky note
- **Kids:** Serve plain sliced pork in a tortilla — skip the garnishes
- **Leftover liquid:** Excellent as a soup base or reduce it down into a sauce

*House recipe — based on traditional Mexican braising technique*

## Ingredients

### Braise
- 1.5 lbs pork belly, cut into 2–3 large slabs
- Salt and black pepper
- 1 Tbsp neutral oil
- 1 medium white onion, roughly chopped
- 5 cloves garlic, smashed
- 2 dried ancho chiles, stems and seeds removed (sub: 1–2 chipotles in adobo)
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp dried oregano (Mexican oregano if you have it)
- 1/2 cup fresh orange juice (~1 large orange)
- 1 cup chicken stock
- 1 bay leaf

### For Tacos
- Corn tortillas, warmed
- Diced white onion
- Fresh cilantro
- Lime wedges
- Salsa or hot sauce (adults)

## Instructions

1. **Preheat** oven to 325°F.

2. **Prep pork:** Score fat cap in a crosshatch pattern if skin-on. Pat dry and season all over with salt and pepper.

3. **Sear:** Heat oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high. Sear pork fat-side down 5–7 minutes until deeply golden. Flip and sear remaining sides 1–2 min each. Remove and set aside. Pour off all but 1 Tbsp fat.

4. **Build braise base:** Add onion to the pot, cook 3 minutes until softened. Add garlic and dried chiles (or chipotles), cook 1 minute. Add cumin and oregano, stir 30 seconds.

5. **Deglaze:** Pour in orange juice and stock, scrape up any browned bits. Add bay leaf.

6. **Braise:** Return pork belly fat-side up — liquid should reach about halfway up the pork. Cover and braise in oven until very tender, 2.5–3 hours. Pork should yield easily to a fork.

7. **Rest:** Remove pork, rest 10 minutes. Strain and reserve braising liquid.

8. **Optional crispy finish:** Slice pork into serving pieces, place fat-side up on a baking sheet. Broil 3–5 minutes until fat is blistered and golden. Watch closely.

9. **Serve:** Slice or roughly chop pork. Spoon a little braising liquid over the meat to keep it moist. Serve in warm corn tortillas with onion, cilantro, and lime.
