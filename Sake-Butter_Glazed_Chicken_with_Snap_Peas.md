# Sake-Butter Glazed Chicken with Snap Peas

## Notes

Claude-original flavor combo (David's protein + sauce + veg formula), not yet cooked by the family. Quick Japanese-style pan sauce -- good with a mild white fish too if you want to swap it in. Protein-flexible: chicken, pork tenderloin, shrimp, or cod/tilapia. Add feedback here after the first try.

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs protein of choice, cut into bite-size pieces (chicken breast or thighs, pork tenderloin, shrimp, or a mild white fish like cod or tilapia)
- 1 tbsp peanut oil (or vegetable oil)
- to taste kosher salt and black pepper
- 1/4 cup sake
- 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 1 tbsp honey
- 2 tbsp unsalted butter
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 cup sugar snap peas, trimmed
- 2 tbsp water

**Optional / For Serving:**

- lemon wedges
- sliced scallions

## Instructions

1. Season protein with salt and pepper. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook in a single layer until browned and cooked through -- chicken or pork about 3-4 minutes per side (165°F), shrimp about 1-2 minutes per side until pink, fish about 2-3 minutes per side depending on thickness. Transfer to a plate.
2. Meanwhile, add snap peas and 2 tbsp water to a covered microwave-safe bowl; microwave 2-3 minutes until bright green and crisp-tender (or steam on the stovetop).
3. Pull out plain protein and snap peas for the kids before saucing.
4. Add sake to the skillet and simmer 1 minute, scraping up any browned bits. Stir in soy sauce and honey; simmer 1 minute. Reduce heat to low, add butter and garlic, and stir until the butter melts into a glossy sauce.
5. Return protein and snap peas to the skillet and toss to coat, 1 minute until warmed through. Serve, with lemon wedges and scallions for the adults.
