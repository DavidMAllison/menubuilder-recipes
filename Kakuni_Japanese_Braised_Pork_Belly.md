# Kakuni (Japanese Braised Pork Belly)

**Time**: 4 hours 30 minutes  
**Adapted from**: [Just One Cookbook](https://www.justonecookbook.com/braised-pork-belly-kakuni/)  

## Ingredients

- 1  Tokyo negi (long green onion) ((or several green onions/scallions))
- 1 knob ginger ((2 inches, 5 cm; unpeeled; divided, for slow cooking and braising))
- 1⅔ lb pork belly
- ½ Tbsp neutral oil ((for searing))
- ¼ cup sake
- 4  large eggs (50 g each w/o shell) ((optional; cold from the refrigerator))
- 5  snap peas ((optional, for garnish; or green beans))
- 1  dried red chili pepper ((optional))
- 2½ cups dashi (Japanese soup stock)
- ¼ cup sake
- ¼ cup mirin
- ¼ cup sugar
- ¼ cup soy sauce
- shichimi togarashi (Japanese seven spice) ((optional, to taste))

## Instructions

1. Assemble your ingredients together. I prepared a rapid dashi by using a dashi packet (requiring just 3 minutes of preparation time). Keep your eggs chilled in the refrigerator until the moment you need them.
