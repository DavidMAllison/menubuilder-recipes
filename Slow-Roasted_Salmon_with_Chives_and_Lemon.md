# Slow-Roasted Salmon with Chives and Lemon

**Time**: 90 minutes  
**Adapted from**: [America's Test Kitchen](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/recipes/11915-slow-roasted-salmon-with-chive)  

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon packed brown sugar
- 1 1/2 teaspoons kosher salt , divided
- 1/2 teaspoon pepper
- 1 (2½-pound) skinless center-cut salmon fillet about 1½ inches thick
- 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 tablespoons sliced fresh chives
- 2 teaspoons grated lemon zest plus 1½ tablespoons juice

## Instructions

1. Adjust oven rack to middle position and heat oven to 250 degrees. Combine 1 tablespoon packed brown sugar, 1 teaspoon kosher salt, and ½ teaspoon pepper in small bowl. Sprinkle 2½-pound skinless center-cut salmon fillet all over with sugar mixture.
2. Place salmon, flesh side up, in 13 by 9-inch baking dish. Roast until center is still translucent when checked with tip of paring knife and thickest part registers 125 degrees (for medium-rare), 55 to 60 minutes.
3. Meanwhile, combine ¼ cup extra-virgin olive oil, 2 tablespoons minced chives, 2 teaspoons grated lemon zest, 1½ tablespoons lemon juice, and remaining ½ teaspoon kosher salt in bowl.
4. Remove dish from oven and immediately pour oil mixture evenly over salmon. Let rest for 5 minutes. Using spatula and spoon, portion salmon and sauce onto serving platter. Stir together any juices left in dish and spoon over salmon. Serve.
