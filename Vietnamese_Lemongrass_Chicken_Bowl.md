# Vietnamese Lemongrass Chicken Bowl (Bun Ga Nuong)

Source: RecipeTin Eats
Time: 45 min active (plus 30 min - 2 hr marinate)
Yield: 4 servings

## Ingredients

- Chicken & Marinade
- 1.5 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 2 stalks lemongrass, white part only, finely minced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons fish sauce
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon neutral oil Nuoc Cham (Dipping Sauce)
- 3 tablespoons fish sauce
- 3 tablespoons lime juice (about 2 limes)
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 1/4 cup warm water
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 red chili, sliced (optional, serve on side) Bowl
- 6 oz vermicelli rice noodles
- 1 cucumber, julienned or sliced
- 2 carrots, julienned
- 1 cup bean sprouts
- Fresh mint and cilantro

## Instructions

1. Marinate chicken with all marinade ingredients for 30 min to 2 hours. Do not marinate longer -- lime juice will break down the chicken.
2. Make Nuoc Cham: combine fish sauce, lime juice, sugar, and warm water. Stir until sugar dissolves. Add garlic and chili.
3. Cook vermicelli noodles per package directions. Rinse under cold water, drain.
4. Cook chicken in a skillet or grill pan over high heat, 3-4 min per side, until charred and cooked through. Rest 3 min, then slice.
5. Assemble bowls: noodles and vegetables on the bottom, sliced chicken on top. Drizzle Nuoc Cham over and serve.

## Notes

- Kids: serve plain chicken and noodles separately, skip Nuoc Cham.
- Family loved this. Good regular rotation candidate.
