# How to Make Oyakodon (Japanese Chicken and Egg Rice Bowl)

## Ingredients

- 1 cup (240ml) homemade dashi, or the equivalent in Hondashi (see note above)
- 1 tablespoon (15ml) soy sauce, plus more to taste
- 2 tablespoons (30ml) dry sake
- 1 tablespoon (15g) sugar, plus more to taste
- 1 large onion, slivered (about 6 ounces; 170g)
- 12 ounces (340g) boneless, skinless chicken thighs, thinly sliced (see note above)
- 3 thinly sliced scallions, divided
- 2 stems mitsuba (optional; see note above)
- 3 to 4 large eggs (see note above)
- To Serve:
- 2 cups cooked white rice
- Togarashi (see note above)

## Instructions

1. Add 1 cup of dashi stock to a skillet
2. Add 1 tablespoon soy sauce, 2 tablespoons sake, and 1 tablespoon sugar to the skillet
3. Bring the liquid to a simmer
4. Add sliced onion and cook until softened, about 5 minutes
5. Add sliced boneless skinless chicken thighs and cook until the chicken is cooked through
6. Season the broth to taste with salt and more sugar
7. Reduce the heat to a bare simmer
8. Sprinkle sliced scallions or mitsuba into the broth
9. Beat 2 eggs gently with chopsticks
10. Drizzle the beaten eggs into the liquid in a thin steady stream using chopsticks
11. Cover the pan and let the egg cook until set to your preference
12. Uncover the pan and shake the mixture to ensure the egg is loose
13. Serve the mixture over a bowl of rice
14. Optional: make a well in the center of the bowl and add a raw egg yolk to stir in while eating
15. Optional: top with sliced scallions and shichimi togarashi
