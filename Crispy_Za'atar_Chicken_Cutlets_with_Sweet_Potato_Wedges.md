# Crispy Za'atar Chicken Cutlets with Sweet Potato Wedges

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup plus 3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil, divided
- 1/4 cup chopped fresh dill, parsley, and/or tarragon
- 1 shallot, minced
- 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar
- 3/4 teaspoon plus 1/8 teaspoon table salt, divided
- 1 1/2 pounds sweet potatoes, unpeeled, cut lengthwise into 1-inch-wide wedges
- 2 large eggs
- 1 cup panko bread crumbs
- 2 tablespoons za'atar
- 2 (8-ounce) boneless skinless chicken breasts, trimmed

## Instructions

1. Adjust oven rack to middle position and heat oven to 450 degrees. Whisk 2 tablespoons oil, ¼ cup chopped dill, 1 minced shallot, 2 tablespoons vinegar, and ⅛ teaspoon salt together in bowl; set vinaigrette aside.
2. Toss 1½ pounds sweet potatoes, cut lengthwise into 1-inch-wide wedges, with 1 tablespoon oil and ¼ teaspoon salt and arrange cut side down in even layer on rimmed baking sheet. Roast until potatoes are softened and bottoms are well browned, about 15 minutes.
3. Meanwhile, lightly beat 2 eggs in shallow dish. Combine 1 cup panko and 2 tablespoons za’atar in second shallow dish. Halve each of 2 boneless skinless chicken breasts horizontally and pound between 2 sheets of plastic wrap to uniform ½-inch thickness. Pat cutlets dry with paper towels and sprinkle with remaining ½ teaspoon salt. Working with 1 cutlet at a time, dip in egg, allow excess to drip off, then coat with panko mixture, pressing gently to adhere; transfer to large plate.
4. Line serving platter with double layer of paper towels. Heat remaining ½ cup oil in 12-inch skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Place 2 cutlets in skillet and cook until deep golden brown, about 2 minutes per side. Transfer cutlets to prepared platter and repeat with remaining 2 cutlets. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Discard paper towels and arrange potatoes on platter with chicken. Drizzle potatoes with vinaigrette and serve.
