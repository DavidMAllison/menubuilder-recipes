# Slow-Cooker Salsa Verde Chicken and White Bean Stew

## Ingredients

- 2 lbs boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 1 1/2 cups jarred salsa verde (mild), divided
- 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth
- 2 (15-ounce) cans white beans (cannellini or great northern), rinsed and drained
- 1 cup frozen corn kernels
- 1 small yellow onion, diced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro, for serving
- 1 lime, cut into wedges
- Hot sauce or extra salsa verde, for serving (adults)
- Cooked rice, for serving

## Instructions

1. Place onion, garlic, white beans, and 1 cup salsa verde in the bottom of the slow cooker. Stir in chicken broth, cumin, and oregano.
2. Season chicken breasts with salt and pepper and nestle into the slow cooker, spooning a little of the sauce over the top.
3. Cover and cook on low for 6 hours, or until chicken shreds easily with a fork.
4. Remove chicken, shred with two forks, then return to the slow cooker. Stir in corn and remaining 1/2 cup salsa verde. Cook 10-15 minutes more until corn is heated through.
5. For kids: set aside a portion of plain shredded chicken and beans before adding the remaining salsa verde, and serve over plain rice.
6. For adults: stir in extra salsa verde or hot sauce to taste. Top with cilantro and a squeeze of lime.
