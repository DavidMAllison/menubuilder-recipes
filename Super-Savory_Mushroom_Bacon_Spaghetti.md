# Super-Savory Mushroom Bacon Spaghetti

Source: America's Test Kitchen (by Maggie Zhu)
Time: 30 minutes
Yield: 4 servings

## Ingredients

- 12 ounces spaghetti
- Table salt for cooking pasta
- 5 slices thick-cut bacon, cut crosswise into 1/2-inch-wide strips
- 1 pound assorted mushrooms, sliced 1/2 inch thick
- 4 scallions, white and green parts separated and sliced thin
- 3 garlic cloves, sliced thin
- 5 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 3 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon instant dashi powder (such as Hondashi)

## Instructions

1. Bring 2 quarts water to boil in a large pot. Add spaghetti and 1/2 tablespoon salt; cook, stirring occasionally, until al dente. Reserve 1 cup cooking water, then drain pasta and return it to pot.
2. Meanwhile, cook bacon in a 12-inch skillet over medium heat until golden and crispy, 6 to 8 minutes. Using a slotted spoon, transfer bacon to a paper towel-lined plate.
3. Add mushrooms to fat left in skillet. Cook over medium-high heat until softened and edges are golden, about 5 minutes. Add scallion whites and garlic; cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Stir in 1/2 cup reserved pasta water, butter, soy sauce, and dashi powder. Transfer to pot with pasta along with reserved bacon; toss to combine. Adjust consistency with additional pasta water as needed.
4. Sprinkle with scallion greens and serve.
