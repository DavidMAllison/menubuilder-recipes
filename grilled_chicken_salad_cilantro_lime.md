# Grilled Chicken Salad with Cilantro-Lime Dressing

**Adapted from**: [Smitten Kitchen](https://smittenkitchen.com/2025/08/grilled-chicken-salad-with-cilantro-lime-dressing/)

## Notes

- Kids: serve plain grilled chicken and corn, skip the dressing (jalapeño). Fritos are a hit.
- Jalapeño heat is mild once seeded — reduce or omit entirely for more heat-sensitive adults.

## Ingredients

**Dressing**
- 1 bundle cilantro (2 oz, leaves and stems trimmed)
- 2 scallions, thinly sliced
- 1 clove garlic, peeled
- 1 jalapeño, seeded and roughly chopped
- Zest and juice of 2 medium/large limes
- 1/2 cup olive oil
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt, plus more to taste

**Salad**
- 1 pound boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 1 1/2 teaspoons chili powder
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 2 ears corn on the cob, shucked
- 1 (5 oz) package leafy green lettuce
- 1 firm-ripe avocado, thinly sliced
- Fritos, for serving

## Instructions

1. **Dressing:** Roughly chop cilantro and add to blender with scallions, garlic, and jalapeño. Pulse to chop. Stream in olive oil while blending until greens are pureed. Add lime zest, juice, and salt; blend again. Adjust seasoning to taste.
2. Pat chicken dry and season both sides with chili powder and salt.
3. Heat grill to medium-high and oil the grates. Grill chicken 4-5 minutes per side until cooked through. Grill corn alongside until charred all over.
4. Slice chicken. Cut corn kernels off the cob.
5. Toss lettuce with dressing in a large bowl. Arrange chicken, corn, and avocado over greens. Drizzle with additional dressing and serve remaining dressing on the side with Fritos.
