- 6 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 teaspoon or more hot sauce, such as gochujang
- Salt, to taste
- 3 to 3 1/2 pounds boneless beef short ribs, rinsed and cut into 3-inch segments

- 2 scallions, thinly sliced on the diagonal (to garnish)
