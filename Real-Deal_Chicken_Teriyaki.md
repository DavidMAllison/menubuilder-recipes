# Real-Deal Chicken Teriyaki

**Time**: 45 minutes
**Servings**: 4

## Ingredients

- 1½–2 pounds bone-in chicken thighs
- 3½ tablespoons sake, divided
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 3 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 2 tablespoons grated fresh ginger
- 2 teaspoons vegetable oil

## Instructions

1. Debone the thighs: place each skin-side down and use a paring knife to cut along the bone, then underneath it to separate. Keep the skin attached. Cut the meat into 1½-inch pieces. Combine chicken with 1½ tablespoons sake and the cornstarch and toss gently to coat.
2. Combine soy sauce, sugar, and remaining 2 tablespoons sake in a bowl. Microwave 30 seconds until sugar dissolves. Set a fine-mesh strainer over the bowl, press the grated ginger through it to extract the juice, and discard the solids.
3. Heat oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium. Add chicken skin-side down — the pan will be full. Increase to medium-high and cook without moving until the skin has a ¼–½ inch border of white on all sides, 6–8 minutes. Flip and cook until just cooked through, 1–2 minutes more. Transfer to a plate and pour off the fat. Wipe the skillet clean.
4. Return chicken to skillet, add the soy-sake mixture, and cook over medium heat, stirring frequently, until chicken is coated in a glossy sauce with the consistency of maple syrup, 1–2 minutes. Serve, drizzling extra glaze over the top.

## Notes

- Boneless skin-on thighs are the ideal cut — if you can find them, buy 1½ lbs and skip the deboning step.
- Serve with short-grain white rice and peppery greens like watercress or mizuna.
- Use inexpensive sake — small cans work fine.
