# Star Anise Soy Glazed Chicken with Broccoli

## Notes

Claude-original flavor combo (David's protein + sauce + veg formula), not yet cooked by the family. A fast weeknight take on red-braise flavors (star anise, soy, shaoxing wine) without the hours-long braise. Protein-flexible: swap in pork tenderloin or flank steak depending on what's in the freezer. Add feedback here after the first try.

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs protein of choice, cut into bite-size pieces (chicken breast or thighs, pork tenderloin, or flank steak)
- 1 tbsp peanut oil (or vegetable oil)
- to taste kosher salt and black pepper
- 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 1 tbsp shaoxing wine
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 whole star anise pod
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 tsp grated fresh ginger
- 1 lb broccoli florets
- 3 tbsp water

**Optional / For Serving:**

- sliced scallions

## Instructions

1. Whisk together soy sauce, shaoxing wine, brown sugar, garlic, and ginger in a small bowl. Set aside.
2. Season protein with salt and pepper. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook in a single layer until browned and cooked through -- chicken or pork about 3-4 minutes per side (165°F), flank steak about 2-3 minutes per side for medium. Transfer to a plate.
3. Meanwhile, add broccoli florets and 2 tbsp water to a covered microwave-safe bowl; microwave 3-4 minutes until bright green and crisp-tender (or steam on the stovetop).
4. Pull out plain protein and broccoli for the kids before saucing.
5. Add the star anise pod and remaining 1 tbsp water to the skillet along with the sauce mixture; simmer 1 minute to let the star anise perfume the sauce. Return protein and broccoli to the skillet and toss to coat, 1-2 minutes until glossy and warmed through. Remove the star anise pod before serving. Top with scallions.
