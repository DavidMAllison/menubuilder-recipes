# Miso Chicken

## Ingredients

- 5 pieces boneless, skin-on chicken thighs
- 5 Tbsp Homemade All-Purpose Miso Sauce
- 1 Tbsp neutral oil ((for cooking))
- 5 Tbsp Homemade All-Purpose Miso Sauce
- 5 Tbsp water
- 1 tsp toasted white sesame seeds
- 1  green onion/scallion ((chopped))

## Instructions

1. Please note that this recipe requires a marinating time of 24 hours. Allow 30 minutes to make my All-Purpose Miso Sauce, if you haven&#39;t already. Gather all the ingredients.
2. Prick the skin of 5 pieces boneless, skin-on chicken thighs with the tip of a knife. Pat dry and place in a resealable plastic bag. Add 5 Tbsp Homemade All-Purpose Miso Sauce to the bag, remove the air, and seal.Nami&#39;s Tip: Pricking the skin allows the fat to render more easily during cooking, making the meat more tender and flavorful.
3. Rub the chicken through the bag to coat evenly. Refrigerate for 24 hours.Nami&#39;s Tip: Do not marinate longer than 24 hours or the chicken will get too salty. To freeze uncooked after marinating, see To Store below.
4. Remove the chicken from the bag and wipe off the miso marinade with your hands. Use a paper towel to remove any remaining marinade and pat dry.
5. Add 1 Tbsp neutral oil to a cold, large frying pan and swirl to coat. Don’t turn on the heat yet. Place the chicken skin-side down, leaving space between pieces. Nami&#39;s Tip: Don&#39;t overcrowd the pan or the chicken will steam instead of sear.
6. Set the heat to medium-low. Cook skin-side down, undisturbed, for 7 minutes—pressing down occasionally with a spatula— until the skin is golden brown and the edges are opaque.Nami&#39;s Tip: Do not cover with a lid. Use a splatter guard if needed.
7. Flip the chicken and cook for 5 minutes—pressing down occasionally—until browned. Wipe away any rendered fat with a paper towel.
8. Cook until the internal temperature registers 165ºF (74ºC). Transfer to a cutting board, rest until cool enough to handle, and cut into ½-inch (1.3 cm) strips.
9. Wipe the pan clean with a paper towel. Add 5 Tbsp Homemade All-Purpose Miso Sauce and 5 Tbsp water. Simmer on low, stirring, until the glaze coats the back of a spoon. Turn off the heat.
10. Serve over steamed rice and drizzle with miso glaze. Top with 1 tsp toasted white sesame seeds and 1 green onion/scallion (chopped). Serve warm.
11. Transfer the leftovers to an airtight container and refrigerate for up to 3 days and freeze for up to 1 month. To freeze the raw marinated chicken, wipe off the marinade completely and freeze for up to 2–3 weeks.
