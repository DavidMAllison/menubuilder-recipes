# Chicken Cacciatore

## Ingredients

- Chicken whole about 3 lb (1.3 kg)
- Peeled tomatoes 14 oz
- Yellow onions 1
- Carrots 1
- Celery 1 rib
- Garlic 1 clove
- Red wine ½ cup
- Extra virgin olive oil to taste
- Rosemary 1 sprig
- Parsley chopped 1 sprig
- Fine salt to taste
- Black pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Begin by preparing the vegetables for your Chicken Cacciatore: peel and prepare the onion, followed by the carrot which should be peeled and trimmed, then remove the celery top. Dice all three vegetables to approximately 1/10 inch (2-3 mm). Next, move on to the chicken by cutting it into pieces—separate the thighs, chest, and wings. With your ingredients now ready, proceed to cooking. Add just a small amount of oil to a saucepan, keeping in mind that the chicken's skin will render significant fat. Allow the pan to heat through briefly over the flame, then place the chicken pieces in with the skin facing downward.

2. Brown the chicken for approximately ten minutes, flipping the pieces partway through. Once the chicken has achieved good color, introduce the diced celery, carrot, and onion to the pan along with the peeled garlic clove. Season with salt, pepper, and rosemary, give everything a stir, and allow the flavors to meld for 5 minutes more.

3. Pour in the red wine and permit the alcohol to cook off entirely. Extract the rosemary sprigs and garlic clove from the pan, then incorporate the chopped peeled tomato.

4. Combine all ingredients thoroughly, place the lid on top, and allow the dish to simmer at moderate heat for 30-35 minutes. Note that larger chicken pieces may require slightly extended cooking time, while smaller pieces need less; the chicken is finished when the meat easily separates from the bone.

5. Once cooking is complete, verify the seasoning and adjust salt as needed, then garnish generously with parsley. Give the dish one final stir and your Chicken Cacciatore is ready to serve. Bon appétit!
