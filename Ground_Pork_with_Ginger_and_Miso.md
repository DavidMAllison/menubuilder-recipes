# Ground Pork with Ginger and Miso

Source: Cookbook (from photo)
Time: 30 minutes
Yield: 4 servings

## Ingredients

- 3 tablespoons minced fresh ginger
- 4 scallions, thinly sliced, whites and greens reserved separately
- 3 tablespoons neutral oil
- 3 tablespoons white miso, red miso, or a combination
- 1 1/2 pounds ground pork
- 3 tablespoons mirin
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- Optional garnish: toasted sesame seeds

## Before You Begin

- Japanese nikumiso-style. Serve on steamed short-grain rice, roasted eggplant, or in lettuce leaves.

## Instructions

1. In a 12-inch nonstick skillet over medium-high heat, cook ginger and scallion whites in the oil, stirring, until fragrant.
2. Add the miso and cook, stirring constantly, until the miso darkens slightly.
3. Add the pork, mirin, soy sauce, and 1/3 cup water. Cook, stirring to break up the meat, until liquid evaporates and meat begins to crisp.
4. Remove from heat and stir in 2 tablespoons water. Serve sprinkled with scallion greens and optional toasted sesame seeds.
