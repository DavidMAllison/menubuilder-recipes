# Greek-Style Garlic-Lemon Chicken

## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh rosemary
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh thyme
- 5 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 teaspoons grated lemon zest plus 2 tablespoons juice (oven) or 2 lemons, zested then halved for grilling (grill)
- 1½ teaspoons kosher salt (Diamond Crystal)
- 1 1/2 teaspoons dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon ground coriander
- 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 3 pounds bone-in chicken pieces (2 split breasts, 2 drumsticks, 2 thighs, 2 wings)

## Before You Begin

- Use Diamond Crystal Kosher Salt — the recipe was developed with it.
- Pat chicken very dry for best browning.
- Work the marinade under the skin — it won't penetrate through the skin. Apply under first, then rub any remaining on the surface.
- Oven method: reserve lemon juice to stir into pan drippings at the end.
- Grill method: zest the lemons first for the marinade, then reserve the halved lemons to grill.

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, combine oil, rosemary, thyme, garlic, lemon zest, salt, oregano, coriander, pepper flakes, and black pepper.
2. Pat chicken dry. Lift skin on each piece and work herb-garlic mixture underneath, pressing to coat the meat directly. Rub any remaining mixture over the skin surface.

### Oven Method

3. Adjust oven rack to middle position and preheat to 425°F. Place chicken skin-side up in a 12-inch oven-safe skillet.
4. Roast until breasts register 160°F and thighs/drumsticks register 175°F, 30 to 35 minutes.
5. Remove skillet from oven (handle will be very hot). Turn on broiler. Broil until skin is lightly browned, about 3 minutes, rotating skillet as needed.
6. Let chicken rest 10 minutes. Transfer to a serving platter. Stir lemon juice into pan juices, then spoon sauce over chicken. Serve.

### Grill Method

3. Preheat grill to medium-high (~400°F) and set up for indirect cooking. Place chicken skin-side up over indirect heat, close lid, and cook 30 to 35 minutes until breasts register 160°F and thighs/drumsticks register 175°F.
4. While chicken cooks, place lemon halves cut-side down on direct heat and grill 10 to 15 minutes until caramelized and slightly charred.
5. Move chicken to direct heat skin-side down for 2 to 3 minutes to crisp the skin.
6. Rest 10 minutes, then squeeze grilled lemons over chicken. Serve.
