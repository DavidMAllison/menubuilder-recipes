# Greek-Style Garlic-Lemon Chicken

**Time**: 50 minutes
**Servings**: 4–6

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh rosemary
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh thyme
- 5 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 teaspoons grated lemon zest plus 2 tablespoons juice
- 1½ teaspoons kosher salt (Diamond Crystal)
- 1½ teaspoons dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon ground coriander
- ½ teaspoon red pepper flakes
- ½ teaspoon black pepper
- 3 pounds bone-in chicken pieces (breasts, drumsticks, thighs, wings)

## Instructions

1. Combine oil, rosemary, thyme, garlic, lemon zest, salt, oregano, coriander, pepper flakes, and black pepper in a bowl.
2. Pat chicken dry. Lift the skin on each piece and work the herb mixture underneath, pressing it directly onto the meat. Rub any remainder over the surface.

### Oven Method

3. Heat oven to 425°F. Place chicken skin-side up in an oven-safe skillet. Roast until breasts hit 160°F and thighs/drumsticks hit 175°F, 30–35 minutes.
4. Switch to broil and broil until skin is golden, about 3 minutes. Let rest 10 minutes. Stir lemon juice into the pan juices and spoon over the chicken.

### Grill Method

3. Set up grill for indirect heat at medium-high (~400°F). Place chicken skin-side up over indirect heat, close lid, and cook 30–35 minutes to temperature. Meanwhile, grill lemon halves cut-side down over direct heat until caramelized, 10–15 minutes.
4. Move chicken to direct heat skin-side down for 2–3 minutes to crisp. Rest 10 minutes, then squeeze the grilled lemons over the top.

## Notes

- Work the marinade under the skin — it won't penetrate through it.
- Use Diamond Crystal kosher salt; table salt will be too salty at this quantity.
