# Greek-Style Garlic-Lemon Chicken

## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh rosemary
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh thyme
- 5 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 teaspoons grated lemon zest plus 2 tablespoons juice
- 1 tablespoon kosher salt (Diamond Crystal)
- 1 1/2 teaspoons dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon ground coriander
- 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 3 pounds bone-in chicken pieces (2 split breasts, 2 drumsticks, 2 thighs, 2 wings)

## Before You Begin

- Use Diamond Crystal Kosher Salt — the recipe was developed with it.
- Pat chicken very dry for best browning.
- Reserve lemon juice to add at the end; don't marinate with it.

## Instructions

1. Adjust oven rack to middle position and preheat to 425°F. In a medium bowl, combine oil, rosemary, thyme, garlic, lemon zest, salt, oregano, coriander, pepper flakes, and black pepper.
2. Pat chicken dry. Place chicken skin-side up in a 12-inch oven-safe skillet. Rub herb-garlic mixture all over chicken, coating evenly.
3. Transfer skillet to oven and roast until breasts register 160°F and thighs/drumsticks register 175°F, 30 to 35 minutes.
4. Remove skillet from oven (handle will be very hot). Turn on broiler. Broil until skin is lightly browned, about 3 minutes, rotating skillet as needed.
5. Let chicken rest in skillet 10 minutes. Transfer to a serving platter. Stir lemon juice into pan juices, then spoon sauce over chicken. Serve.
