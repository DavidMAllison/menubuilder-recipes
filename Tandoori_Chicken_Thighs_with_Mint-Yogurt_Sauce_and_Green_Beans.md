# Tandoori Chicken Thighs with Mint-Yogurt Sauce and Green Beans

## Notes

Claude-original flavor combo (David's protein + sauce + veg formula), not yet cooked by the family. Add feedback here after the first try.

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- kosher salt and black pepper, to taste
- 1 lb green beans, trimmed
- 3/4 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh mint
- 1 tbsp lime juice
- 1 clove garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. Pat chicken thighs dry and season with salt and pepper. In a small bowl, mix 1 tbsp olive oil with the garam masala, cumin, coriander, smoked paprika, turmeric, and garlic powder into a paste; rub over the chicken.
2. Heat remaining 1 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Sear chicken 5-6 minutes per side until browned and cooked through (165 degrees F). Transfer to a plate and tent with foil; rest 5 minutes.
3. Meanwhile, add green beans and 1 tbsp water to a covered microwave-safe bowl; microwave 4-5 minutes until crisp-tender (or steam on the stovetop). Toss with a drizzle of olive oil and a pinch of salt.
4. Whisk together the yogurt, mint, lime juice, minced garlic, and a pinch of salt for the mint-yogurt sauce.
5. Pull out plain chicken and green beans for the kids before saucing.
6. Slice the adults' chicken and drizzle with the mint-yogurt sauce. Serve alongside the green beans.
