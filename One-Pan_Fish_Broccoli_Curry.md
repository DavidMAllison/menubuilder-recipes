# One-Pan Fish & Broccoli Curry

## Ingredients

- oil of your choice
- 1 tsp chilli oil (optional)
- handful of curry leaves
- 1 tsp black mustard seeds
- 2 red onions, roughly chopped
- 2″ fresh ginger, grated
- 4 garlic cloves, grated
- 400g chopped tomatoes
- 1 tsp honey
- 1 red pepper, roughly chopped
- vegetable stock
- 2 large handfuls of broccoli, roughly chopped
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp chilli powder
- 2 tsp curry powder
- 300g skinless cod fillet, washed

## Instructions

1. Combine 2 tbsp of oil with chilli oil in a preheated pan, then add the curry leaves and black mustard seeds. Let them crackle for 1 minute.
2. Add the onions and sauté for 7-8 minutes with occasional stirring until they achieve a light golden hue.
3. Add the ginger and garlic, stir to combine, and allow to cook for 1 minute.
4. Incorporate the chopped tomatoes and 1 tsp of honey, blend together, and simmer for 1 minute.
5. Add 100ml of boiling water, place a lid on the pan, reduce the heat, and let it simmer for 10 minutes.
6. Stir in the red pepper, combine all ingredients, cover the pan again, and continue cooking for 5 minutes.
7. In a separate step, dissolve vegetable stock in 500ml of boiling water and transfer this into the pan. Cook for an additional 5 minutes.
8. Adjust seasoning with salt, chilli powder, and curry powder, then add the broccoli. Mix thoroughly, cover, and cook for 5 minutes.
9. Divide the cod into uniform portions and place in the curry, making sure each piece is thoroughly coated with the sauce. Allow to cook for 3-4 minutes.
10. Transfer to serving dishes and enjoy with freshly prepared basmati rice or alongside toasted sourdough bread.
