# One-Pan Fish & Broccoli Curry

**Adapted from**: [Chetna Makan](https://chetnamakan.co.uk/one-pan-fish-broccoli-curry/)  

## Ingredients

- oil of your choice
- 1 tsp chilli oil (optional)
- handful of curry leaves
- 1 tsp black mustard seeds
- 2 red onions, roughly chopped
- 2″ fresh ginger, grated
- 4 garlic cloves, grated
- 400g chopped tomatoes
- 1 tsp honey
- 1 red pepper, roughly chopped
- vegetable stock
- 2 large handfuls of broccoli, roughly chopped
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp chilli powder
- 2 tsp curry powder
- 300g skinless cod fillet, washed

## Instructions

1. Add 2 tbsp of oil and chilli oil to a hot pan. Add the curry leaves and black mustard seeds, and let them sizzle for 1 minute.
2. Add the onions and cook for 7-8 minutes until lightly golden.
3. Add the ginger and garlic, stir through, and cook for 1 minute.
4. Add the chopped tomatoes and 1 tsp honey, stir through, and cook for 1 minute.
5. Add 100ml of boiling water, cover, and cook on low heat for 10 minutes.
6. Add the red pepper, stir through, cover, and cook for 5 minutes.
7. Meanwhile, add vegetable stock to 500ml of boiling water. Then, add this to the pan and cook for a further 5 minutes.
8. Add the salt, chilli powder, curry powder, and broccoli. Stir through, cover, and cook for 5 minutes.
9. Chop the cod into equal chunks and add to the curry. Make sure the fish is covered in sauce and cook for 3-4 minutes.
10. Serve with piping hot basmati rice or some toasted sourdough.
