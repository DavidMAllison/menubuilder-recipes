# Pesto Pantesco with Spaghetti

## Ingredients

- 2/3 cup slivered almonds, toasted
- 1 1/4 lbs tomatoes, cored and quartered
- 3/4 cup fresh basil leaves
- 1/2 cup fresh parsley leaves
- 3 tbsp salted capers, rinsed well
- 1 garlic clove, peeled
- 1/2 tsp table salt, plus salt for cooking pasta
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1/2 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 1 lb spaghetti

## Before You Begin

Capers packed in salt have a complex flavor central to the dish — sold at specialty or Italian markets or online. If unavailable, substitute 1/4 cup brined capers (unrinsed) plus one minced anchovy. Use ripe local tomatoes; avoid plum tomatoes, they're not juicy enough. Combine the pesto and pasta just before serving.

## Instructions

1. Bring 4 quarts water to boil in large pot. While water comes to boil, pulse almonds in food processor until most pieces are no bigger than 1/4 inch, 8-10 pulses. Add tomatoes, basil, parsley, capers, garlic, salt, and pepper flakes and pulse until a cohesive mixture forms, 8-10 pulses. Scrape down sides of bowl. With processor running, slowly add oil in a steady stream until emulsified, 15-20 seconds. Transfer half of pesto to a large serving bowl.

2. Add pasta and 1 tbsp salt to boiling water and cook, stirring often, until al dente. Reserve 1/4 cup cooking water, then drain pasta and transfer to bowl with pesto. Top with remaining pesto and toss until well combined, taking care not to leave pesto at the bottom of the bowl. Adjust consistency with reserved cooking water as needed, 1 tbsp at a time. Season with salt to taste and serve immediately.
