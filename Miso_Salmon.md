# Miso Salmon

Source: Just One Cookbook (Nami)
Time: 20 minutes active, plus 1-2 hours marinating
Yield: 2 servings (double for family)

## Ingredients

- 2 skin-on salmon fillets (~6 oz each)
- 3 tablespoons white miso
- 2 tablespoons sake
- 2 tablespoons mirin
- 1 teaspoon soy sauce
- 1 teaspoon sesame oil
- 1 teaspoon sesame seeds, for garnish
- 2 scallions, sliced, for garnish

## Instructions

1. Check salmon for scales and pin bones; remove with tweezers.
2. Mix miso, sake, mirin, soy sauce, and sesame oil in a small bowl.
3. Place salmon skin-side up in marinade. Refrigerate 1-2 hours.
4. Preheat broiler to High (550°F), rack 9 inches from element. Line a baking sheet with foil and oil it.
5. Remove excess marinade from salmon. Place skin-side down on prepared sheet.
6. Broil 10-13 minutes until salmon reaches 125-130°F. (Bake alternative: 425°F for 18-20 minutes.)
7. Top with sesame seeds and green onion. Serve with rice.

## Notes

- Kid-friendly: miso glaze is savory-sweet, not spicy. Serve plain salmon to kids before glazing.
