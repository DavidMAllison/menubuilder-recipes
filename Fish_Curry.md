# Fish Curry

**Adapted from**: [Chetna Makan](https://chetnamakan.co.uk/fish-curry/)  

## Ingredients

- oil of choice
- 1 tsp fenugreek seeds
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 10-12 fresh curry leaves (skip if unavailable)
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, grated
- 1″ fresh ginger, grated
- 4 large tomatoes, roughly chopped
- salt
- 1 large sea bream (or white fish of choice)
- 1 tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- ¼ tsp sugar

## Instructions

1. Add 2 tbsp oil to a hot pan. Add the fenugreek and cumin seeds, and let them sizzle.
2. Add the curry leaves and onion. Cook for 8-10 minutes until golden.
3. Add the garlic and ginger. Cook for 30 seconds.
4. Add the tomatoes. Cook on low heat for 10-12 minutes.
5. Meanwhile prepare the fish by cutting it into thick slices. Then, rub ¼ tsp salt and ¼ tsp turmeric powder onto the slices.
6. Add ½ cup oil to a hot pan. Shallow fry the fish for 1-2 minutes on both sides until golden. Transfer to a kitchen roll-line plate to remove any excess oil.
7. Add ½ tsp salt and ½ tsp turmeric powder to the tomatoes. Then, add the chilli powder, garam masala, and ground coriander. Mix well.
8. Add 200ml water and let it bubble for 30 seconds.
9. Add the fried fish and sugar. Mix well and cook for 2 minutes.
10. Serve with some piping hot basmati rice. Time to tuck in!
