# Fish Curry

## Ingredients

- oil of choice
- 1 tsp fenugreek seeds
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 10-12 fresh curry leaves (skip if unavailable)
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, grated
- 1″ fresh ginger, grated
- 4 large tomatoes, roughly chopped
- salt
- 1 large sea bream (or white fish of choice)
- 1 tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- ¼ tsp sugar

## Instructions

1. Place 2 tbsp oil in a hot pan and add the fenugreek and cumin seeds, letting them crackle for a short time.
2. Add the curry leaves and onion, letting them simmer for 8-10 minutes until they become golden.
3. Incorporate the garlic and ginger, cooking for 30 seconds only.
4. Add the tomatoes and lower the heat, simmering for 10-12 minutes.
5. At the same time, slice the fish into thick pieces and coat each piece with ¼ tsp salt and ¼ tsp turmeric powder rubbed directly onto it.
6. Heat ½ cup oil in a hot pan and shallow fry the fish slices for 1-2 minutes per side until golden. Transfer to a paper towel-lined plate to remove excess oil.
7. Add ½ tsp salt and ½ tsp turmeric powder to the tomato mixture, then add the chilli powder, garam masala, and ground coriander. Stir until fully combined.
8. Add 200ml water to the pan and allow it to simmer for 30 seconds.
9. Carefully add the fried fish and sugar, mixing well to combine, and cook for 2 minutes.
10. Plate with hot basmati rice.
