# Pork Chops with White Wine and Herb Pan Sauce

## Notes

Kids: Pull chops from pan before making the sauce. Slice plain and serve with a simple side.

## Ingredients

- 4 boneless pork loin chops (about 1 inch thick, 6 oz each)
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 shallots, minced (or 3 garlic cloves)
- 3/4 cup dry white wine
- 2 sprigs fresh rosemary
- 3 sprigs fresh thyme
- 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
- Fresh parsley, chopped (optional garnish)

## Instructions

1. Pat pork chops dry with paper towels. Season both sides generously with salt and pepper.
2. Heat olive oil in a cast-iron or heavy-bottomed skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add chops and cook without moving for 3-4 minutes until golden brown.
3. Flip and cook another 3-4 minutes until internal temperature reaches 145F. Transfer to a plate and tent loosely with foil.
4. Reduce heat to medium. Add shallots (or garlic) to the pan and cook 30 seconds until fragrant, scraping up any browned bits.
5. Pour in white wine. Add rosemary and thyme sprigs. Simmer 3-4 minutes, stirring occasionally, until reduced by half.
6. Remove herb sprigs. Remove pan from heat and swirl in butter until melted and sauce is glossy. Taste and adjust seasoning.
7. Return chops to pan and spoon sauce over them. Garnish with fresh parsley if desired and serve immediately.
