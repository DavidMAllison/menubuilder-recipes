# Kashmiri Rajma Curry

**Time**: 1 hour  
**Serves**: 2-4  
**Adapted from**: [Ranveer Brar](https://ranveerbrar.com/recipes/kashmiri-rajma-curry/)  

## Ingredients

- Rajma

## Instructions

1. Place cleaned rajma along with 1 bay leaf, the necessary amount of salt, and 2 to 3 cups of water into a pressure cooker. Once the first whistle sounds, lower the heat and allow it to cook for an additional 15 minutes before turning off the flame.

2. Pour off the liquid from the cooked beans and reserve this cooking liquid for future use.

3. Warm oil in a wide pan, then introduce cumin seeds and asafoetida into the hot oil.

4. Incorporate finely minced onion and cook until it develops a light brown hue. Stir in the ginger paste along with the dry ginger powder.

5. Mix in the tomato puree and allow it to simmer for approximately 5 to 8 minutes.

6. Incorporate the beaten yogurt while continuously stirring to prevent curdling. Continue cooking until the oil rises to the surface of the gravy.

7. Blend in the red kashmiri chilli powder, coriander powder, sliced green chilies, and the required salt.

8. Pour in the cooked rajma and the reserved water from step 2.

9. Partially crush some of the rajma beans to thicken and give body to the gravy.

10. Mix thoroughly and simmer over low heat for 15 to 20 minutes. Finish by adding fresh homemade garam masala and cook for an additional 5 minutes.

11. When complete, top with fresh chopped coriander leaves and serve immediately.
