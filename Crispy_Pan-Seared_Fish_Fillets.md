# Crispy Pan-Seared Fish Fillets (The Food Lab Method)

**Serves:** 4
**Adapted from**: [Serious Eats / J. Kenji Lopez-Alt](https://www.seriouseats.com/the-easiest-crispy-pan-seared-fish-food-lab-recipe)

## Notes

- Works with any thick white fish fillet. Halibut and sea bass hold up especially well.
- Kids can get plain alongside — pull their portions before breading if they prefer uncoated fish, or serve with tartar sauce on the side.
- Panko breadcrumbs available at well-stocked grocery stores and Asian markets.

## Ingredients

- 4 thick white fish fillets or steaks (halibut, striped bass, sea bass, or swordfish), 5–8 oz each
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 large egg, beaten
- 1 1/2 cups panko breadcrumbs
- 3 tablespoons vegetable, canola, or peanut oil
- Lemon wedges or tartar sauce, for serving

## Instructions

1. Adjust oven rack to center position and preheat oven to 300°F.
2. Pat fish dry with paper towels and season generously with salt and pepper.
3. Set up three shallow bowls: flour, beaten egg, and panko. Season each lightly with salt and pepper.
4. Working one piece at a time, dip fish presentation-side-down into flour, then egg, then panko — pressing firmly so a thick layer adheres. Place breaded-side-up on a clean plate. Repeat with remaining fish.
5. Heat oil in a large cast iron or nonstick skillet over medium heat until shimmering. Add fish breaded-side-down and cook, swirling and rotating around the pan, until deep golden brown, about 5 minutes.
6. Carefully flip fish and transfer skillet to oven. Cook until an instant-read thermometer registers 140°F at the thickest point, about 5 more minutes.
7. Serve immediately with lemon wedges or tartar sauce.
