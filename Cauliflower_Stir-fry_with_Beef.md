# Cauliflower Stir-fry with Beef

## Ingredients

- 12 ounces flank steak (sliced 1/4-inch or 5mm thick)
- ¼ teaspoon baking soda
- 2 tablespoons water
- 1 teaspoon cornstarch
- 2 teaspoons vegetable oil
- 2 teaspoons oyster sauce
- ¾ cup low sodium chicken stock ((warmed))
- 1½ teaspoons granulated sugar ((or brown sugar))
- 1½ tablespoons light soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon oyster sauce
- 1 teaspoon dark soy sauce ((or double black dark soy sauce))
- ½ teaspoon sesame oil
- ⅛ teaspoon white pepper
- 12 ounces cauliflower florets ((preferably Chinese/fioretto cauliflower, about 4-5 cups))
- 3 tablespoons neutral oil ((such as vegetable, canola, or avocado oil, divided))
- 2 cloves garlic ((minced))
- ¼ teaspoon ginger ((grated/minced, optional))
- 1 tablespoon Shaoxing wine
- 2 tablespoons cornstarch ((mixed with 2 tablespoons/30 ml water))

## Instructions

1. Start by making a velveting marinade in a medium bowl. Mix your sliced beef with baking soda and water, then massage the mixture into the meat with your hands until all the liquid is absorbed. Afterwards, add the cornstarch, oil, and oyster sauce and mix well. Let this rest for at least 30 minutes.

2. Make your sauce by whisking together chicken stock, sugar, light soy sauce, oyster sauce, dark soy sauce, sauce, sesame oil, and white pepper. Fill a large pot with water and bring it to a boil, then quickly blanch the cauliflower for 30 to 60 seconds. After blanching, drain it in a colander and keep it aside.

3. Heat your wok over high heat until it begins to smoke slightly. Add 2 tablespoons of oil and spread the beef out in a single layer. Let it cook without moving for 1 minute on the first side, then flip the pieces over. Continue cooking until the outside is golden brown and the meat is about 80% cooked through. Take the wok off the heat, move the beef to a plate, and put it to the side.

4. Put the wok back on medium heat and pour in 1 tablespoon of oil with the garlic and ginger if you like. Cook the aromatic ingredients for 5 seconds, then pour the Shaoxing wine around the inner edge of the wok. Add the blanched cauliflower and your prepared sauce to the wok. Using a spatula, scrape and mix the sauce around the wok's sides to release any stuck-on browned bits.

5. Let the sauce come to a light simmer. In a small bowl, mix the cornstarch and water together until smooth to create a slurry, then add half of it to the simmering sauce while stirring constantly. Keep simmering for 20 seconds while the sauce thickens.

6. Put the beef back in the wok along with any juices that collected and toss everything together over medium heat. Keep stirring until the sauce simmers and coats the meat and vegetables evenly. If the sauce seems too runny, turn up the heat to reduce it and add more cornstarch slurry if needed. If it becomes too thick, thin it out with a splash of chicken stock or water. Put everything on a serving plate and serve with plenty of steamed rice.
