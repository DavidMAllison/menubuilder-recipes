# Gochujang Sesame Glazed Chicken with Green Beans

## Notes

Claude-original flavor combo (David's protein + sauce + veg formula), not yet cooked by the family. Gochujang gives real heat -- keep kids' portions plain, sauce is adults-only. Protein-flexible: swap in pork tenderloin, shrimp, or tofu depending on what's in the freezer/fridge. Add feedback here after the first try.

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs protein of choice, cut into bite-size pieces (chicken breast or thighs, pork tenderloin, shrimp, or extra-firm tofu)
- 1 tbsp peanut oil (or vegetable oil)
- to taste kosher salt and black pepper
- 2 tbsp gochujang
- 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp honey (or brown sugar)
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 lb green beans, trimmed
- 2 tbsp water

**Optional / For Serving:**

- toasted sesame seeds
- dried red chile flakes, for extra heat

## Instructions

1. Whisk together gochujang, soy sauce, rice vinegar, honey, sesame oil, and garlic in a small bowl. Set aside.
2. Season protein with salt and pepper. Heat oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Cook in a single layer until browned and cooked through -- chicken or pork about 3-4 minutes per side (165°F), shrimp about 1-2 minutes per side until pink, tofu about 2-3 minutes per side until golden. Transfer to a plate.
3. Meanwhile, add green beans and 2 tbsp water to a covered microwave-safe bowl; microwave 3-4 minutes until bright green and crisp-tender (or steam on the stovetop).
4. Pull out plain protein and green beans for the kids before saucing -- the gochujang glaze has real heat.
5. Return protein and green beans to the skillet, pour in the gochujang glaze, and toss to coat, 1-2 minutes until glossy and warmed through. Serve, topped with sesame seeds and extra chile flakes for adults who want more heat.
