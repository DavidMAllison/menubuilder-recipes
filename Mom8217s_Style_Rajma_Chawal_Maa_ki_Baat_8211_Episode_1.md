# Mom&#8217;s Style Rajma Chawal  Maa ki Baat &#8211; Episode 1

**Time**: 50 minutes  
**Serves**: 4  
**Adapted from**: [Ranveer Brar](https://ranveerbrar.com/recipes/punjabi-rajma/)  

## Ingredients

- 2 cups Rajma, soaked overnight
- Salt to taste
- Water to boil
- 1 tbsp ghee
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 tbsp ginger garlic, coarsely ground
- 3 large onions, chopped/ grated
- Salt to taste
- 1 tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 3 large tomatoes, paste
- Coriander sprig
- Ginger julienne

## Instructions
1. Season the overnight-soaked rajma with a pinch of salt, add the necessary water, and pressure cook it for 3-4 whistles.
2. While that cooks, begin making the masala by heating ghee in a pan.
3. Toast the cumin seeds for about a minute, then add the roughly ground ginger-garlic paste and cook until aromatic.
4. Stir in the onions and cook until they achieve a brown color. Incorporate salt, turmeric powder, red chilli powder, coriander powder, and garam masala, stirring to combine thoroughly.
5. Continue cooking for 3-4 minutes, then add the tomatoes and stir until they release their oil.
6. Once the rajma finishes cooking, drain it well and set the cooking liquid aside.
7. Combine the cooked rajma with the masala mixture and stir-fry for 3-4 minutes.
8. Pour in the reserved cooking water as needed, stir to incorporate, and let it simmer gently over medium heat for 4-5 minutes.
9. Top the dish with julienned ginger and fresh coriander sprigs.
10. Plate while hot and accompany with rice.
