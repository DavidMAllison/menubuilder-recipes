# Mom&#8217;s Style Rajma Chawal  Maa ki Baat &#8211; Episode 1

**Time**: 50 minutes  
**Serves**: 4  
**Adapted from**: [Ranveer Brar](https://ranveerbrar.com/recipes/punjabi-rajma/)  

## Ingredients

- 2 cups Rajma, soaked overnight
- Salt to taste
- Water to boil
- 1 tbsp ghee
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 tbsp ginger garlic, coarsely ground
- 3 large onions, chopped/ grated
- Salt to taste
- 1 tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 3 large tomatoes, paste
- Coriander sprig
- Ginger julienne

## Instructions

1. Add a pinch of salt to the over night soaked rajma, add water and required and boil them in a pressure cooker for 3-4 whistles.
2. Meanwhile prepare the masala, heat ghee in a pan.
3. Add cumin seeds and saute for a minute. Add coarsely ground ginger garlic and saute till fragrant.
4. Add onions and saute till they turn brown. Add salt, turmeric powder, red chilli powder, coriander powder and garam masala. Mix well.
5. Saute for 3-4 minutes. Add tomatoes, mix well and saute till they start leaving their oil.
6. Meanwhile, drain the rajma and reserve the water.
7. Add the boiled rajma to the prepared masala and saute for 3-4 minutes.
8. Add the reserved water as required, mix well and simmer on medium flame for 4-5 minutes.
9. Garnish with ginger julienne and coriander sprig.
10. Serve hot with rice.
