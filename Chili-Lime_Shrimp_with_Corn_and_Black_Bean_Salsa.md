# Chili-Lime Shrimp with Corn and Black Bean Salsa

## Notes

Claude-original flavor combo (David's protein + sauce + veg formula), not yet cooked by the family. Add feedback here after the first try.

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs large shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- kosher salt, to taste
- 2 cups corn kernels (fresh, or frozen and thawed)
- 1 (15 oz) can black beans, rinsed and drained
- 1/4 cup red onion, finely diced
- 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro
- 2 tbsp lime juice, divided

**Optional / For Serving:**

- 1 jalapeno, minced

## Instructions

1. Toss shrimp with 1 tbsp olive oil, chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, and a pinch of salt. Set aside a portion of shrimp with just a pinch of salt (no chili) for the kids, if preferred.
2. Heat remaining 1 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook corn 3-4 minutes until lightly charred; transfer to a bowl to cool slightly.
3. In the same skillet, cook shrimp in a single layer, 1-2 minutes per side, until pink and opaque. Transfer to a plate.
4. Pull out plain shrimp and corn for the kids.
5. To the corn, add black beans, red onion, cilantro, 1 tbsp lime juice, and jalapeno (if using); toss to combine for the black bean salsa.
6. Squeeze the remaining lime juice over the adults' shrimp. Serve shrimp over the corn and black bean salsa.
