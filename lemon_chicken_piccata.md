# Lemon Chicken Piccata

Time: 30 minutes
Yield: 4 servings

## Ingredients

- 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 4 tablespoons butter, divided
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 cup chicken broth
- 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice (about 2 lemons)
- 3 tablespoons capers, drained
- 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped
- Lemon slices for garnish (optional)

## Instructions

1. Pound chicken breasts to even 1/2-inch thickness. Season both sides with salt and pepper.
2. Place flour in a shallow dish. Dredge chicken in flour, shaking off excess.
3. Heat 2 tablespoons butter and olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add chicken and cook 3-4 minutes per side until golden brown and cooked through. Transfer to a plate and tent with foil.
4. In the same skillet, add garlic and cook 30 seconds until fragrant.
5. Add chicken broth and lemon juice. Bring to a boil, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan. Let simmer 3-4 minutes until reduced by half.
6. Stir in capers and remaining 2 tablespoons butter until melted.
7. Return chicken to the skillet and spoon sauce over top. Simmer 2 minutes to warm through.
8. Garnish with fresh parsley and lemon slices. Serve immediately with pasta, rice, or vegetables.
