# Skillet-Roasted Chicken Breasts with Harissa-Mint Carrots

## Ingredients

- 4 (10- to 12-ounce) bone-in split chicken breasts, trimmed
- 2 1/2 teaspoons kosher salt, divided
- Vegetable oil spray
- 1 shallot, sliced thin
- 2 teaspoons harissa
- 1 1/2 pounds carrots, peeled and sliced on the bias 1/4 inch thick
- 1/2 cup water
- 2 teaspoons lemon juice
- 1 tablespoon chopped fresh mint, divided

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 325°F with a rack in the lower-middle position. Separate the skin from each chicken breast, peeling it back while keeping it attached at the top, bottom, and ribs.
2. Season the meat with 1 1/2 teaspoons salt total, then replace the skin and poke 6-8 holes in the fat deposits. Spray the skin with oil.
3. Place chicken skin-side down in a 12-inch oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat and cook, without moving frequently, until the skin browns well, 7-9 minutes.
4. Flip chicken and transfer the skillet to the oven. Roast until internal temperature reaches 160°F, about 25-30 minutes.
5. Move chicken to a plate, reserving the liquid in the skillet. Add shallot, harissa, and remaining 1 teaspoon salt; cook over medium-high heat, stirring occasionally, 2-4 minutes.
6. Add carrots and water; bring to a simmer, cover, reduce heat to medium, and cook 10-12 minutes until tender, stirring halfway through.
7. Uncover and cook, stirring frequently, until the sauce coats the carrots, 2-4 minutes. Stir in lemon juice, 1 1/2 teaspoons mint, and any accumulated chicken juices; season to taste.
8. Plate the carrots, top with chicken, and garnish with the remaining mint.
