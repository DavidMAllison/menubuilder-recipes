# Pulled Pork Ragu

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs smoked pulled pork (pre-cooked, thawed), shredded
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 medium onion, chopped fine
- 2 carrots, peeled and chopped fine
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1/2 cup dry red wine
- 1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1/2 tsp dried thyme
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- kosher salt, to taste
- 1 lb pappardelle (or tagliatelle)
- Parmesan, freshly grated, for serving
- fresh basil or parsley, torn, for serving

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a large Dutch oven or deep skillet over medium heat. Add onion and carrots and cook until softened, about 8 minutes. Add garlic and cook 1 minute more.

2. Stir in tomato paste and cook, stirring, for 2 minutes until it darkens slightly.

3. Add red wine and scrape up any browned bits. Cook until wine is mostly reduced, about 3 minutes.

4. Add crushed tomatoes, oregano, thyme, and pepper. Stir to combine, then add pulled pork and stir to incorporate.

5. Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer 30-40 minutes until sauce has thickened and pork is very tender. Season with salt to taste.

6. Meanwhile, bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook pasta until al dente. Reserve 1 cup pasta water, then drain.

7. Toss pasta with ragu, adding splashes of pasta water to loosen sauce as needed. Serve with Parmesan and fresh herbs.

## Notes

- Since the pork is already smoked and cooked, it doesn't need long to braise — 30 minutes is enough; longer deepens the flavor.
- Kids get plain pasta with minimal sauce or butter.
- A splash of balsamic vinegar at the end adds depth if the sauce tastes flat.
- Leftovers reheat well; loosen with a little water or broth.
