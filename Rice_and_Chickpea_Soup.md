# Rice and Chickpea Soup

## Ingredients

- Originario rice 1 cup
- Precooked chickpeas 1 ½ cup
- Carrots 2.8 oz
- White onions ¾ cup
- Garlic 1 clove
- Vegetable broth to taste
- Curry 1 tsp
- Rosemary 1 sprig
- Black pepper to taste
- Fine salt to taste
- Extra virgin olive oil to taste
- Champignon mushrooms 3 ½ cups

## Instructions

1. To make the rice and chickpea soup, first, take care of cleaning the mushrooms: cut the base of the stem and remove the soil residues with a damp cloth. Slice the mushrooms and set them aside. Clean and chop the onion and carrots. Heat a dash of oil in the pan.
2. Sauté the carrot, onion, and garlic over medium-low heat. Add the mushrooms and cook for a couple of minutes. Add the chickpeas and mix.
3. Cover with the hot broth. Once it reaches boiling, add the rice, stir and season with salt, pepper, and curry.
4. Fragrance with chopped rosemary. Cook the rice, adding broth if necessary, stirring often. Serve the rice and chickpea soup hot.
