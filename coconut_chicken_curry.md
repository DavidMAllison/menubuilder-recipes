# Coconut Chicken Curry

Cook Time: 35 minutes

## Notes

- Family hit — kids ate the chicken. Good rotation candidate.
- Use full-fat coconut milk for the richest, creamiest sauce.

## Ingredients

- 2 lbs boneless, skinless chicken thighs or breasts, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 1 large onion, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons fresh ginger, grated
- 2-3 tablespoons curry powder (adjust to taste)
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon ground coriander
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric
- (optional) 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper, for heat
- 1 can (14 oz) coconut milk
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- 1 cup chicken broth
- 2 tablespoons fish sauce (or soy sauce)
- 1 tablespoon brown sugar
- Salt and pepper to taste
- (optional) 2 cups baby spinach or kale
- Fresh cilantro for garnish
- Lime wedges for serving

## Instructions

1. **Prep:** Cut the chicken into 1-inch pieces and season lightly with salt and pepper. Dice the onion, mince the garlic and grate the ginger. Measure the curry powder, cumin, coriander, turmeric and cayenne into one small bowl.
2. **Saute the aromatics:** Heat the oil in a large, deep skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high. Add the onion and cook, stirring occasionally, 5-6 minutes until soft and golden at the edges. Add the garlic and ginger and cook 1 minute, stirring, until fragrant.
3. **Toast the spices:** Turn the heat to medium. Add the bowl of spices and stir constantly for 30-60 seconds until fragrant and darkened slightly. Don't let them scorch -- if the pan looks dry, add a splash of oil.
4. **Brown the chicken:** Turn the heat back to medium-high and add the chicken. Stir to coat it in the spiced onions, then spread it out and cook 5-7 minutes, stirring only every minute or two, until the outside of every piece is opaque with some golden spots. It does not need to be cooked through yet -- it finishes in the sauce.
5. **Add the liquids:** Stir in the coconut milk, diced tomatoes, tomato paste and chicken broth, scraping up the browned bits from the bottom of the pan.
6. **Season:** Stir in the fish sauce and brown sugar. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat to low.
7. **Simmer:** Simmer uncovered 15-20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the chicken is cooked through (breasts 160F, thighs 175F) and the sauce has thickened to your liking.
8. **(Optional) Add greens:** Stir in the spinach or kale during the last 2 minutes and cook until wilted.
9. **Adjust:** Taste and add salt, pepper or more curry powder as needed. If too thick, add a splash of broth; if too thin, simmer a few minutes longer.
10. **Serve:** Serve over steamed rice or with naan, garnished with cilantro, with lime wedges on the side.

## Tips

- Thighs stay more tender than breasts in the simmer.
- Adjust heat with more or less curry powder and cayenne. Leave the cayenne out to keep it kid-friendly.
- Tastes even better the next day.
- Shrimp, tofu or chickpeas can stand in for the chicken.

## Serving Suggestions

- Steamed basmati or jasmine rice, warm naan, rice noodles, or cauliflower rice for low-carb.
- Cucumber raita or mango chutney on the side.
