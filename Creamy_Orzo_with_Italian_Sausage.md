# Creamy Orzo with Italian Sausage

**Time**: 35 minutes
**Servings**: 4

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 pound hot or sweet Italian sausage, casings removed
- 1 onion, chopped fine
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- ¼ teaspoon red pepper flakes (omit if using hot sausage)
- 3 cups chicken broth
- 1 (14.5-ounce) can diced tomatoes, drained
- 1½ cups orzo
- ½ cup heavy cream
- 3 ounces baby spinach (about 3 cups)
- 2 ounces Parmesan, grated (about 1 cup)
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh basil
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Add sausage and cook, breaking it up, until browned, 5–7 minutes. Remove sausage with a slotted spoon and set aside.
2. Add onion to the fat in the skillet and cook over medium heat until softened, about 5 minutes. Add garlic and pepper flakes and cook until fragrant, 30 seconds.
3. Stir in broth, tomatoes, and orzo. Bring to a simmer and cook, stirring often, until orzo is tender and the mixture is creamy, 15–18 minutes.
4. Stir in cream, spinach, and sausage. Cook until spinach is wilted and everything is heated through, about 2 minutes. Remove from heat and stir in Parmesan and basil. Season with salt and pepper. Serve right away.

## Notes

- Kids: Pull plain sausage and set aside before finishing with cream and spinach.
- Sweet or hot sausage both work — if using hot, skip the pepper flakes.
