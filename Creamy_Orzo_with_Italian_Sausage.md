# Creamy Orzo with Italian Sausage

Source: America's Test Kitchen
Time: 50 minutes
Yield: 4 to 6 servings

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 pound hot or sweet Italian sausage, casings removed
- 1 onion, chopped fine
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes (omit if using hot sausage)
- 3 cups chicken broth
- 1 (14.5-ounce) can diced tomatoes, drained
- 1 1/2 cups orzo
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 3 ounces baby spinach (3 cups)
- 2 ounces Parmesan cheese, grated (1 cup)
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh basil
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Heat oil in a 12-inch nonstick skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add sausage and cook, breaking up meat with a wooden spoon, until browned, 5 to 7 minutes. Using a slotted spoon, transfer sausage to a bowl.
2. Add onion to fat left in skillet and cook over medium heat until softened, about 5 minutes. Stir in garlic and pepper flakes and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
3. Stir in broth, tomatoes, and orzo. Bring to a simmer and cook, stirring often, until orzo is tender and mixture is creamy, 15 to 18 minutes.
4. Stir in cream, spinach, and reserved sausage; cook until heated through and spinach is wilted, about 2 minutes. Off heat, stir in Parmesan and basil. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Serve immediately.
