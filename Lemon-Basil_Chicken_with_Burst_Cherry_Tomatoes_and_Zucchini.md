# Lemon-Basil Chicken with Burst Cherry Tomatoes and Zucchini

## Notes

Claude-original flavor combo (David's protein + sauce + veg formula), not yet cooked by the family. Add feedback here after the first try.

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs boneless skinless chicken breast, sliced into thin cutlets
- kosher salt and black pepper, to taste
- 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1 lb zucchini, sliced into half-moons
- 2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1 lemon, juiced and zested
- 1/4 cup fresh basil, torn

**Optional / For Serving:**

- pinch red pepper flakes

## Instructions

1. Season chicken cutlets with salt and pepper. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook chicken 3-4 minutes per side until golden and cooked through (165 degrees F). Transfer to a plate.
2. Add the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil to the skillet. Add zucchini and sauté 4-5 minutes until just tender; season with salt. Transfer plain zucchini to a plate for the kids.
3. Add cherry tomatoes and garlic to the skillet; cook 3-4 minutes, pressing gently, until the tomatoes burst and release their juices.
4. Stir in butter, lemon juice, and lemon zest; simmer 1-2 minutes until slightly thickened.
5. Pull out plain chicken for the kids before saucing.
6. Return chicken to the skillet, spoon the tomatoes and sauce over top, and scatter with basil. Add red pepper flakes to the adults' portion if desired. Serve with the zucchini.
