# Greek Turkey Meatballs with Tzatziki

## Ingredients

**Meatballs:**
- 1 1/2 pounds ground turkey (93% lean)
- 1/2 cup panko breadcrumbs
- 1/3 cup finely grated red onion
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 large egg, lightly beaten
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh dill
- 1 tablespoon chopped fresh oregano (or 1 teaspoon dried)
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 cup crumbled feta cheese

**Tzatziki:**
- 1 cup plain Greek yogurt (whole or 2%)
- 1/2 cup grated cucumber, excess water squeezed out
- 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
- 1 tablespoon chopped fresh dill
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1/4 teaspoon salt

## Before You Begin

- Use 93% lean ground turkey — 99% lean will make dry meatballs.
- Squeeze cucumber very well in paper towels to remove excess moisture for tzatziki.
- Don't overmix the meatball mixture or they will be tough.

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Line a rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper and lightly spray with cooking spray.
2. In a large bowl, combine turkey, panko, grated onion, garlic, egg, dill, oregano, salt, and pepper. Mix gently until just combined. Fold in feta.
3. Form into 16-18 meatballs (about 1.5 inches each) and place on prepared baking sheet.
4. Bake 18-20 minutes until golden brown and cooked through (165°F).
5. While meatballs bake, make tzatziki: Combine yogurt, cucumber, lemon juice, dill, garlic, and salt. Stir well and refrigerate until ready to serve.
6. Serve meatballs hot with tzatziki on the side. Great over rice, in pita bread, or with a Greek salad.
