# Italian Baked Chicken

## Ingredients

- 2 lbs boneless skinless chicken breast
- Salt and pepper
- 2 teaspoons dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon fresh thyme
- 1 teaspoon sweet paprika
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- Juice of 1/2 lemon
- 1 medium red onion, halved and thinly sliced
- 5 to 6 Campari tomatoes (or small Roma tomatoes), halved
- Handful fresh parsley, chopped (garnish)
- Fresh basil leaves (garnish)

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
2. Pat chicken dry. Place breast in a zip-top bag, remove air, and pound flat with a meat mallet. Repeat for all pieces.
3. Season chicken with salt and pepper. Place in a mixing bowl; add oregano, thyme, paprika, garlic, olive oil, and lemon juice. Mix thoroughly to coat evenly.
4. Spread onion slices on the bottom of a lightly oiled baking dish. Arrange seasoned chicken on top and add tomatoes.
5. Cover tightly with foil and bake 10 minutes. Uncover and bake 8-10 minutes more until internal temperature reaches 165°F.
6. Remove from oven and let rest 5-10 minutes covered. Garnish with fresh parsley and basil before serving.

## Notes

- For kids: Very mild herb seasoning — can pull plain chicken before garnishing.
- Serve with pasta, rice, or crusty bread and a green salad.
