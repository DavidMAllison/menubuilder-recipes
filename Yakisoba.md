# Yakisoba (Japanese Stir-Fried Noodles)

**Adapted from**: [Just One Cookbook](https://www.justonecookbook.com/yakisoba/)

## Notes

- Use the largest pan you have -- overcrowding kills the char
- Cook vegetables in stages so each holds its texture
- Serve immediately; noodles soften as they sit
- Pork belly must be thin slices (~1/4 inch) — NOT 1-inch pieces. Sear on high heat until edges brown, then push aside before adding vegetables.
- **Pork sub:** pork shoulder or loin works fine; slice thin against the grain (~1/4 inch)
- **Kids:** pull plain noodles and pork before saucing for milder version
- Leftovers keep 3-4 days refrigerated; reheat in pan with a splash of water

## Ingredients

### Main
- 3 servings yakisoba noodles, pre-steamed (~454g)
- 3/4 lb pork belly, cut into thin slices (~1/4 inch)
- 1/4 small head green cabbage, chopped bite-sized (~227g)
- 3 shiitake mushrooms, sliced (~40g)
- 4-inch piece carrot, julienned (~100g)
- 1/2 onion, sliced 1/4-inch thick
- 2 green onions, chopped
- 2 Tbsp neutral oil
- Freshly ground black pepper

### Yakisoba Sauce
- 1/4 cup Worcestershire sauce
- 4 tsp oyster sauce
- 4 tsp ketchup
- 2 tsp soy sauce
- 2 tsp sugar

### Optional Toppings
- Aonori (dried green seaweed)
- Beni shoga (pickled red ginger)

## Instructions

1. **Make sauce:** Whisk together Worcestershire, oyster sauce, ketchup, soy sauce, and sugar until sugar dissolves. Taste and adjust sweetness.

2. **Prep:** Cut pork belly into thin slices, about 1/4 inch thick. Slice onion, julienne carrots, chop cabbage into bite-sized pieces, slice mushrooms, chop green onions.

3. **Loosen noodles:** Heat oil in a large pan or griddle over medium heat. Add noodles, flip, and gently separate with chopsticks or tongs. Transfer to a plate.

4. **Cook pork:** Increase heat to high. Add pork, season with black pepper, stir-fry until no pink remains and edges start to brown, ~2-3 minutes. Push pork to the side of the pan (or remove to a plate).

5. **Add vegetables in stages:** Reduce heat to medium-high. Cook onion 1-2 minutes, add carrots 1-2 minutes, add cabbage and mushrooms until softened, add green onions for 1 minute. Toss pork back in with the vegetables.

6. **Combine:** Return noodles to pan, pour sauce over everything, toss until evenly coated. Serve immediately.

7. **Garnish:** Top with aonori and beni shoga if using.
