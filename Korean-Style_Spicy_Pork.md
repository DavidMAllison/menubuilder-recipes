# Korean-Style Spicy Pork

Source: Cookbook (from photo)
Time: 30 minutes
Yield: 4 servings

## Ingredients

- Gochujang (to taste)
- Soy sauce (to taste)
- White sugar (to taste)
- Neutral oil
- 1 pound boneless pork shoulder, thinly sliced
- 1 medium yellow onion, sliced
- 3-inch piece fresh ginger, OR 4 cloves garlic, OR both
- Steamed short-grain rice or lettuce leaves, for serving
- Optional garnish: thinly sliced scallions, toasted sesame oil, toasted sesame seeds

## Instructions

1. Mix the sauce ingredients (gochujang, soy sauce, sugar) in a small bowl.
2. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add pork and cook until browned.
3. Add onion and ginger (or garlic); cook until fragrant.
4. Stir in the gochujang mixture and cook until pork is tender and coated.
5. Serve with steamed rice or lettuce leaves. Garnish with scallions, sesame oil, or sesame seeds if desired.
