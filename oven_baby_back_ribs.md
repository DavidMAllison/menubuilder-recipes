# Oven Baby Back Ribs

## Ingredients

- 2 racks baby back ribs (about 4-5 lbs total)
- 2 tablespoons brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon paprika
- 2 teaspoons garlic powder
- 2 teaspoons onion powder
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional)
- 1 cup BBQ sauce (your favorite brand)
- 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
- 1/4 cup apple juice or water

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 275°F. Line a large baking sheet with heavy-duty aluminum foil.
2. Prep ribs: Remove membrane from back of ribs by sliding a knife under it at one end, then gripping with a paper towel and pulling it off. Pat ribs dry.
3. Make dry rub: Combine brown sugar, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, salt, and cayenne.
4. Season ribs: Rub spice mixture all over both sides of ribs, pressing it in. Place ribs meat-side up on prepared baking sheet.
5. Wrap and bake: Pour apple juice around (not on) the ribs. Cover entire pan tightly with another layer of foil, creating a sealed packet. Bake 2.5 hours until ribs are very tender.
6. Prepare sauce: While ribs bake, mix BBQ sauce with apple cider vinegar in a small bowl.
7. Finish (oven method): Remove ribs from oven. Carefully remove top foil (watch for steam). Increase oven to 425°F. Brush ribs generously with BBQ sauce mixture. Return uncovered for 10-15 minutes until sauce is caramelized and sticky.
8. Finish (grill method, optional): Preheat grill to medium-high. Transfer ribs to grill. Brush with BBQ sauce and grill 3-4 minutes per side, basting with more sauce, until caramelized.
9. Rest: Let ribs rest 5-10 minutes. Cut between bones into individual ribs or serve as half/full racks. Serve with extra BBQ sauce, coleslaw, and cornbread.
