# Blackened Shrimp with Blistered Tomatoes and Corn

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 teaspoons paprika
- 1 1/2 teaspoons table salt, divided
- 1/2 teaspoon pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/8 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 1 1/2 pounds extra-large shrimp (21 to 25 per pound), peeled and deveined, tails removed
- 3 tablespoons unsalted butter, divided
- 12 ounces grape tomatoes
- 2 cups fresh or frozen corn
- 1 tablespoon chopped fresh basil
- 2 tablespoons minced fresh chives, divided

## Instructions

1. Combine paprika, 1 teaspoon salt, pepper, thyme, oregano, garlic powder, and cayenne in a large bowl. Pat shrimp dry, add to the bowl, and toss to coat evenly.
2. Heat a 12-inch cast-iron skillet over medium heat for 3 minutes. Add 1 tablespoon butter and swirl to melt. Cook tomatoes, stirring occasionally, until charred in spots, about 2 minutes. Cover and let sit 2 minutes until softened, then transfer to a bowl.
3. Wipe the skillet clean. Melt 1 tablespoon butter over medium heat. Add corn and cook, stirring occasionally, until kernels soften, about 2 minutes, then cover and cook until fully softened, about 2 minutes more. Add to the tomato bowl with basil, 1 tablespoon chives, and remaining 1/2 teaspoon salt.
4. Wipe the skillet again and melt the remaining 1 tablespoon butter over medium-high heat. Add shrimp in a single layer and cook 1-2 minutes per side until opaque.
5. Transfer shrimp to a platter and top with the remaining 1 tablespoon chives. Serve alongside the corn and tomato mixture.
