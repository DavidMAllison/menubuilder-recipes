# Maple-Dijon Chicken with Asparagus

## Notes

Claude-original flavor combo (David's protein + sauce + veg formula), not yet cooked by the family. Mild and kid-friendly -- no heat, sauce is fine for the whole table. Protein-flexible: chicken or pork tenderloin. Add feedback here after the first try.

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs protein of choice (chicken breast or thighs, or pork tenderloin)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- to taste kosher salt and black pepper
- 3 tbsp maple syrup
- 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1/2 tsp dried thyme
- 1 lb asparagus, trimmed
- 2 tbsp water

## Instructions

1. Whisk together maple syrup, Dijon, apple cider vinegar, garlic, and thyme in a small bowl. Set aside.
2. Season protein with salt and pepper. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook protein until browned and cooked through -- chicken about 3-4 minutes per side (165°F), pork tenderloin medallions about 3-4 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate.
3. Add asparagus and 2 tbsp water to the skillet; cover and steam 3-4 minutes until crisp-tender.
4. Pull out plain protein and asparagus for the kids before saucing -- this one's mild enough they may want the sauce too.
5. Return protein and asparagus to the skillet, pour in the maple-dijon sauce, and toss to coat, 1-2 minutes until glossy and warmed through. Serve.
