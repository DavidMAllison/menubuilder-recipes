# Sopa de Albóndigas

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ripe tomatoes ((about 3 medium tomatoes))
- 1/4 medium white onion
- 1  garlic clove ((unpeeled))
- 1/4 cup water ((if needed))
- 1 tsp vegetable oil
- 6 cups chicken broth
- 1 cup carrot  ((peeled and in large cubes))
- 1 cup potato  ((peeled and in large cubes))
- 1 1/4  cup Mexican squash  (( or zucchini large cubed))
- 1/2 cup fresh cilantro ((chopped))
- Kosher Salt to taste
- 1 slice white sandwich bread
- 1/4 cup whole milk
- 1 pound ground beef
- 1  garlic clove  ((chopped or garlic powder))
- 6  peppercorns  ((or ground black pepper) )
- 1 Lg eggs ((or two small ones beaten))
- Salt to taste

## Instructions

1. To make the broth. Preheat a comal or large skillet over medium-high heat, then roast the tomatoes, onions, and garlic.
2. After they are roasted and cool to the touch, peel the garlic and place the vegetables in a blender. Blend until smooth. If your tomatoes aren&#39;t juicy enough and your blender is having difficulty processing them, add some water.
3. Heat the oil in a large saucepan over medium-high heat. Add the sauce and cook for about 5 minutes, then add the chicken broth. When the broth starts to boil, reduce the heat to a simmer.
4. In the meantime, make the meatballs by soaking the bread with milk in a small bowl until soft. (about one minute).
5. Then, place the ground beef into a large bowl and make a well in the center.
6. On a molcajete, pulverize the garlic and peppercorns ( or use the ground powder spices). Add them to the well in the center of the meat, along with the milk-soaked bread and beaten egg. Season with salt, and gently mix the meat and the rest of the ingredients using your hands or a wooden spoon. Do not over-mix or your meatballs will be tough.
7. To form the meatballs, get your hands wet with cold water and form golf ball-sized meatballs without forcing or putting too much pressure on the meat. As you form them, carefully drop them into the simmering tomato broth.
8. Add the carrots to the saucepan, and continue forming the meatballs, about 12 in total.
9. Once the meatballs are in the broth, add the potatoes. Continue cooking for 5 more minutes and stir in the squash, then simmer for another 10 to 12 minutes. About 5 minutes before everything is done cooking, add the chopped cilantro and season with salt if needed.
10. Serve in medium bowls, along with the rice (if using) or beans, and warm tortillas.
