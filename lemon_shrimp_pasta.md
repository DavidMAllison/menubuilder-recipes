# Lemon Shrimp Pasta  Cook Time: 20 minutes 

## Ingredients

- For the Pasta:
- 1 lb linguine or spaghetti
- Salt for pasta water For the Shrimp and Sauce:
- 1 1/2 lbs large shrimp (16-20 count), peeled and deveined
- 4 tablespoons butter, divided
- 3 tablespoons olive oil, divided
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)
- 1/2 cup dry white wine (or chicken broth) Juice of 2 lemons (about 1/3 cup) Zest of 1 lemon
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped
- 2 tablespoons fresh basil, chopped (optional)
- Salt and black pepper to taste Extra lemon wedges for serving

## Instructions

1. Cook Pasta: Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook pasta according to package directions until al dente. Reserve 1 cup pasta water, then drain pasta and set aside.
2. Prepare Shrimp: Pat shrimp dry with paper towels. Season with salt and pepper.
3. Cook Shrimp: Heat 2 tablespoons butter and 2 tablespoons olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add shrimp in a single layer and to a plate and set aside.
4. Make Sauce Base: In the same skillet, add remaining 1 tablespoon olive oil. Add garlic and red pepper flakes. Saute for 30-60 seconds until fragrant (don't let garlic burn).
5. Deglaze Pan: Pour in white wine and lemon juice. Bring to a simmer and cook the pan. Let liquid reduce by half.
6. Add Cream: Reduce heat to medium-low. Stir in heavy cream and lemon zest.
7. Add Cheese: Stir in Parmesan cheese until melted and smooth. Add remaining 2 tablespoons butter and stir until incorporated.
8. Combine: Add cooked pasta to the sauce. Toss to coat evenly. If sauce is too thick, add reserved pasta water, 1/4 cup at a time, until desired consistency is reached.
9. Finish: Return shrimp to the pan and toss gently. Stir in fresh parsley and basil. Taste and adjust seasoning with salt and pepper. 10. Serve: Serve immediately, garnished with extra Parmesan, fresh herbs, and lemon wedges on the side. Tips - Don't overcook the shrimp - they cook quickly and can get rubbery - Reserve pasta water - it's essential for adjusting sauce consistency - Use freshly grated Parmesan for best results (not pre-shredded) - Fresh lemon juice is key - bottled juice won't have the same flavor - For extra richness, add more butter at the end - Can add cherry tomatoes or asparagus for extra vegetables Serving Suggestions Serve with crusty garlic bread and a simple arugula salad with lemon vinaigrette. A crisp white wine like Pinot Grigio or Sauvignon Blanc pairs beautifully with this dish.
