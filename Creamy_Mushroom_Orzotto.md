# Creamy Mushroom Orzotto

Source: America's Test Kitchen (Vallery Lomas)
Time: 1 hour 30 minutes
Yield: 4 to 6 servings

## Ingredients

- 2 ounces dried porcini mushrooms, rinsed
- 4 cups boiling water
- 3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil, divided
- 1 pound mixed fresh mushrooms (cremini, shiitake, oyster), sliced thin, divided
- 1 1/2 teaspoons table salt, divided
- 1/2 teaspoon pepper
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1 shallot, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 1/2 cups orzo
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1/2 cup dry white wine
- 2 cups chicken broth
- 3 cups reserved mushroom soaking liquid
- 1 1/2 ounces Parmesan cheese, grated (3/4 cup)
- 3 tablespoons sour cream
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh chives

## Before You Begin

You can use any mix of fresh mushrooms you prefer, but we especially like a combination of cremini, shiitake, and oyster mushrooms. Dried porcinis are essential for bringing a deep earthy savoriness to the dish. Do not discard the porcini soaking liquid -- it is essential to the dish's depth. Pour carefully to leave any grit behind. The orzotto thickens as it sits; add a splash of broth to loosen if needed before serving.

## Instructions

1. Place rinsed dried porcini mushrooms in large bowl; cover with 4 cups boiling water and soak for 20 minutes. Drain mushrooms, reserving liquid. Using your hands, squeeze mushrooms over reserved liquid to release any remaining liquid and pat dry using paper towels. Set mushrooms and reserved liquid aside.
2. Heat 2 tablespoons olive oil in large Dutch oven over medium heat until shimmering. Add half the fresh mushrooms, half the porcini mushrooms, 1/4 teaspoon salt, and 1/4 teaspoon pepper and cook until mushrooms start to brown, 6 to 8 minutes. Transfer mushrooms to large plate. Add remaining 1 tablespoon olive oil and repeat with remaining fresh mushrooms, remaining porcini mushrooms, 1/4 teaspoon salt, and remaining 1/4 teaspoon pepper. Transfer to plate.
3. Melt butter in now-empty Dutch oven. Add shallot and garlic and cook until fragrant, 1 to 2 minutes. Add orzo and dried thyme and cook 1 minute longer. Stir in white wine, scraping up any browned bits. Cook until wine is absorbed, about 30 seconds. Add chicken broth and 3 cups of reserved mushroom soaking liquid and bring to boil, then reduce to simmer. Simmer uncovered for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until liquid is absorbed.
4. Off heat, stir in Parmesan, sour cream, remaining 1 teaspoon salt, and browned mushrooms. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Sprinkle with chopped chives. Serve.
