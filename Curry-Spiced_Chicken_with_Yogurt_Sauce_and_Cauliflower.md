# Curry-Spiced Chicken with Yogurt Sauce and Cauliflower

## Notes

Claude-original flavor combo (David's protein + sauce + veg formula), not yet cooked by the family. Mild -- no heat, the cool yogurt sauce works for kids as-is. Protein-flexible: chicken, shrimp, or paneer for a vegetarian night. Add feedback here after the first try.

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs protein of choice (chicken breast or thighs, shrimp, or paneer), cut into bite-size pieces
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp garam masala
- to taste kosher salt and black pepper
- 1 cup plain yogurt
- 2 tbsp lime juice
- 1 clove garlic
- 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated
- 2 tbsp fresh cilantro, chopped
- 1 lb cauliflower florets

## Instructions

1. Stir together yogurt, lime juice, garlic, ginger, and cilantro in a small bowl; season with a pinch of salt. Set aside -- this is the sauce, served cool, not heated.
2. Toss protein with turmeric, cumin, garam masala, salt, and pepper.
3. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook protein until browned and cooked through -- chicken about 3-4 minutes per side (165°F), shrimp about 1-2 minutes per side until pink, paneer about 2-3 minutes per side until golden. Transfer to a plate.
4. Meanwhile, add cauliflower florets and 2 tbsp water to a covered microwave-safe bowl; microwave 4-5 minutes until crisp-tender (or steam on the stovetop).
5. Serve protein and cauliflower with the yogurt sauce spooned on top or on the side -- mild enough that kids can have the sauce too, no need to hold it back.
