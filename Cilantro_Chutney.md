# Cilantro Chutney

**Time**: 10 minutes  
**Yield**: ~6 servings  
**Adapted from**: [Indian Healthy Recipes](https://www.indianhealthyrecipes.com/cilantro-chutney/)

## Ingredients

- 3 cups cilantro with tender stalks (75g)
- 1–2 green chilies
- 1½ teaspoons roasted cumin powder (or 2 teaspoons roasted cumin seeds)
- 2–3 medium garlic cloves
- ½ teaspoon salt (or ¼ tsp salt + ⅛ tsp kala namak)
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 2–4 tablespoons cold water
- 3 tablespoons roasted peanuts (optional)

## Instructions

1. Rinse cilantro thoroughly and shake dry. Trim roots and mature stalks; roughly chop leaves and tender stalks.
2. Peel garlic. Remove chili stalks; deseed for less heat.
3. Add peanuts, green chilies, salt, cumin, and garlic to a blender.
4. Add cilantro, lemon juice, and 2 tablespoons water.
5. Blend until smooth, scraping sides every 30–40 seconds. Avoid over-blending to prevent bitterness.
6. Add more water as needed for a thick, spreadable consistency.
7. Taste and adjust seasoning. Refrigerate for up to 4 days.

## Notes

- Peanuts add body and richness — sub roasted chana dal or desiccated coconut, or skip entirely.
- Good with chicken tikka, samosas, and sandwiches.
