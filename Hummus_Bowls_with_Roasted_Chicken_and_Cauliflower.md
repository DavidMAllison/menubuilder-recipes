# Hummus Bowls with Roasted Chicken and Cauliflower

**Time**: 45 minutes  
**Yield**: 4 servings  
**Adapted from**: [America's Test Kitchen](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/recipes/16801-hummus-bowls-with-roasted-chicken-and-cauliflower)

## Notes

Baharat is a warm, savory spice blend used throughout the Middle East. If you can't find it, substitute 1½ tsp paprika, ¾ tsp pepper, ½ tsp ground cumin, and ¼ tsp ground cloves. Serve with warm pita wedges.

Kids' portions: pull plain chicken pieces before topping with the slaw and tomato mixture.

## Ingredients

- 1½ lbs boneless, skinless chicken thighs, trimmed
- 6 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, divided, plus extra for drizzling
- 1 tbsp baharat
- 2 tsp table salt, divided
- 1¼ lbs cauliflower florets, cut into 2-inch pieces
- 2 cups thinly sliced red cabbage
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- ½ English cucumber, cut into ½-inch dice
- 2 plum tomatoes, cut into ½-inch dice
- 2 cups hummus (store-bought)
- ¼ cup chopped fresh mint

## Instructions

1. Adjust oven rack 6 inches from broiler element and heat broiler. Line rimmed baking sheet with foil.
2. Combine chicken, 2 tbsp oil, baharat, and 1 tsp salt in a large bowl. Arrange chicken in a single layer on half of the prepared sheet and broil for 5 minutes.
3. Combine cauliflower, 2 tbsp oil, and ½ tsp salt in a clean large bowl. Spread cauliflower on the empty half of the sheet. Broil until cauliflower is well browned and tender and chicken registers at least 175°F, 10–12 minutes. Let rest for 5 minutes.
4. Meanwhile, combine cabbage, lemon juice, ¼ tsp salt, and remaining 2 tbsp oil in the now-empty bowl. Combine cucumber, tomatoes, and remaining ¼ tsp salt in a second bowl.
5. Cut chicken into bite-size pieces. Spread hummus over the bottoms of individual serving bowls. Top with piles of chicken, cauliflower, cabbage, and tomato mixture. Drizzle with extra oil and sprinkle with mint. Serve.
