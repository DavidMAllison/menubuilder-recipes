# Thai Red Curry Noodles

## Ingredients

- 16 oz brown rice noodles
- 1 (13.5 oz) can full-fat coconut milk
- 3-4 tbsp Thai red curry paste
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1-2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce or tamari
- 4 large garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp maple syrup or honey
- 1 lime, juiced (1-2 tbsp)
- 1 tsp cornstarch (or tapioca/arrowroot)
- Salt and pepper, to taste

**Optional / For Serving:**

- Cilantro, scallions, and red pepper flakes, for garnish

## Instructions

1. Boil the noodles in a large pot of salted water, pulling them a minute or so before the package time -- they finish cooking in the sauce. Set aside a portion of plain noodles for the kids before saucing.
2. While the noodles cook, warm the olive oil in a small pot or skillet with the garlic and ginger and cook until the garlic just turns golden.
3. Whisk in the coconut milk, curry paste, tomato paste, soy sauce, maple syrup, and lime juice until smooth.
4. Bring the sauce to a boil, whisk in the cornstarch, then drop the heat to low and simmer 2-3 minutes.
5. Add the drained noodles to the sauce and let them simmer gently over low heat until the sauce thickens and the noodles are tender.
6. The noodles soak up sauce as they sit, so it will look loose at first. For a drier result, start with about half the sauce and add more to taste.
7. Top with seared chicken or shrimp and a sauteed or steamed vegetable. Garnish with cilantro, scallions, and red pepper flakes.
