# San Francisco-Style Vietnamese American Garlic Noodles

Source: Kenji Lopez-Alt (NYT Cooking)
Time: 25 minutes
Yield: 4 servings

## Ingredients

- 4 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 20 medium garlic cloves, minced or smashed in a mortar and pestle
- 4 teaspoons oyster sauce
- 2 teaspoons light soy sauce or shoyu
- 2 teaspoons fish sauce
- 1 pound dry spaghetti
- 1 ounce grated Parmesan or Pecorino Romano (heaping 1/4 cup)
- Small handful thinly sliced scallions (optional)

## Instructions

1. Melt butter in a wok or saucepan over medium heat. Add the garlic and cook, stirring, until fragrant but not browned, about 2 minutes. Add the oyster sauce, soy sauce, and fish sauce; stir to combine. Remove from heat.
2. Bring 1.5 inches of water to a boil in a 12-inch skillet or sauté pan over high heat. Add pasta, stir a few times to prevent clumping, and cook stirring occasionally until just shy of al dente (about 2 minutes short of package time).
3. Using tongs, transfer pasta to garlic sauce along with whatever water clings to it (reserve pasta water in the skillet). Increase heat to high, add cheese to the wok, and toss vigorously until the sauce is creamy and emulsified, about 30 seconds. If too watery, let it reduce; if greasy, splash in pasta water and re-emulsify. Stir in scallions and serve immediately.

## Notes

- Kids: pull out plain buttered noodles before adding the garlic sauce.
