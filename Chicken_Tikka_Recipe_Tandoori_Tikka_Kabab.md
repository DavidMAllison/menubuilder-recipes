# Chicken Tikka Recipe (Tandoori Tikka Kabab)

**Time**: 35 minutes  
**Serves**: 4  
**Adapted from**: [Indian Healthy Recipes](https://www.indianhealthyrecipes.com/chicken-tikka-in-oven)  

## Notes

- **Serving**: Serve with [Cilantro Chutney](https://davidmallison.github.io/menubuilder-recipes/Cilantro_Chutney), [Mint-Cilantro Green Chutney](https://davidmallison.github.io/menubuilder-recipes/Mint-Cilantro_Green_Chutney), or raita. Also works well stuffed in naan or chapati with chutney and salad.
- **Chicken thighs**: Only need 20 minutes of resting time — good for weeknights.
- **Chicken breasts**: Need 3–4 hours minimum resting (8+ hours ideal) to stay juicy at high heat. Well-rested breast delivers better texture and flavor than thighs.

## Ingredients

- 1 pound bone-less chicken ( (450 to 500 grams))
- 1 teaspoon salt ( (adjust to taste))
- ½ to 2 teaspoons Kashmiri Red chilli powder  ((for less spicy version, use ½ teaspoon))
- ¼ teaspoon turmeric
- ¾ teaspoon roasted cumin powder ( (roasted ground cumin))
- 2 teaspoons coriander powder  ((ground coriander))
- 1 to 1 ¼ teaspoon garam masala
- 1 teaspoon chaat masala  ((skip if you don’t have or 1/3 teaspoon amchur, dried mango powder))
- ½ teaspoon black pepper ( crushed or powder (optional))
- 1 tablespoon kasuri methi ( (dried fenugreek leaves))
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice  ((skip if your yogurt is sour))
- 1 ½ tablespoon Ginger garlic paste (or 1 tbsp each fresh ginger and garlic, minced and crushed)
- 1 tablespoon oil ( (mustard oil if you have))
- ½ cup Greek yogurt ( (hung curd))
- 1 to 2 tablespoons butter (for brushing)
- 200 grams bell peppers ((1½ to 2 cups, diced))
- 1 medium onion ((diced and layers separated))

## Instructions

1. Cut the chicken into 1½-inch cubes and place them in a bowl. Pat the chicken dry with tissues to remove any excess moisture from the surface.

2. Mix together the chilli powder, coriander, cumin powder, garam masala, chaat masala, salt, kasuri methi, and lemon juice with the chicken. Toss everything to ensure even coating and allow it to rest for 10 to 15 minutes so the spices can penetrate the meat. (This initial resting period is optional.)

3. Add the ginger-garlic paste, oil, and yogurt to the chicken and mix until all ingredients are fully incorporated. Cover the bowl and place it in the refrigerator for no less than 8 hours, preferably extending to 48 hours for optimal flavor infusion. For those short on time, a 20-minute marinade will suffice.

4. If you are using bamboo skewers, soak them in water for 30 minutes and then pat them dry before skewering.

5. To prepare your oven, line a deep baking tray with foil and heat the oven to 240°C (460°F), making sure to allow at least 15 minutes for it to fully preheat.

6. (Optional) If you wish to use bell peppers and onions, add them to the marinade along with the chicken and toss until coated.

7. Thread the chicken pieces, bell peppers, and onions (if desired) onto the skewers, alternating between each ingredient.

8. Place the skewers on the foil-lined tray leaving space between them and bake for 9 minutes. Using tongs, turn the skewers and bake for an additional 9 to 10 minutes until the chicken is cooked completely. (Keep in mind that oven variations may affect cooking duration.)

9. Adjust the oven to broil and broil for 2 minutes to develop a light char on the surface. If broil is not available on your oven, place the skewers on the top rack as an alternative.

10. Take the tray out of the oven and check if the chicken is done by slicing one piece in half—there should be no pink inside. Return to the oven if any pink coloring remains.

11. Brush the skewers with melted butter and serve with cilantro chutney, mint chutney, or raita, optionally dusting with chaat masala.

12. For air frying, position the skewers in the air fryer basket (or in a compatible container if the basket is too small).

13. Set the air fryer to 200°C (400°F) and cook for 6 minutes, then flip and cook for another 6 minutes. Brush the skewers with butter, leftover marinade, or mustard oil and air fry for 2 more minutes.

14. For added richness, apply an additional coat of butter right before serving.

15. Heat a skillet with oil or butter on medium heat and place the skewers on it. Cook for 4 to 5 minutes, flip, and cook for another 4 to 5 minutes until the internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C).

16. To create a charred crust, transfer the skewers to metal ones and hold them directly over an open flame for 1 to 2 minutes.

17. For a tandoori flavor, place the hot grilled skewers on a tray and position a small steel bowl in the middle.

18. Using tongs, heat a piece of charcoal in an open flame until it glows red, then place it into the steel bowl. Drizzle 1 teaspoon of ghee onto the hot coal—smoke will immediately rise. Seal the tray by placing another tray on top or covering with foil to trap the smoke inside.

19. Let the chicken absorb the smoke for 3 to 5 minutes according to your preferred smokiness. The tandoori chicken tikka is now ready to serve!
