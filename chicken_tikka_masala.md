# Chicken Tikka Masala

Time: 60-65 minutes
Yield: 6 servings

## Ingredients

**Sauce**
- 2 tablespoons ghee (clarified butter)
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon salt, or to taste
- 1 teaspoon ground ginger
- 1 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon ground turmeric
- 1 (14 oz) can tomato sauce
- 1 cup heavy whipping cream
- 1 tablespoon white sugar, or to taste
- 2 teaspoons paprika

**Chicken**
- 4 boneless, skinless chicken breast halves, cut into bite-size pieces
- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
- 1/2 teaspoon curry powder

## Instructions

1. Heat ghee in a large skillet over medium heat. Add onion and cook until translucent, about 5 minutes.
2. Stir in garlic and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute. Add cumin, salt, ginger, cayenne, cinnamon, and turmeric; fry until fragrant, about 2 minutes.
3. Stir in tomato sauce, bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low and simmer 10 minutes.
4. Mix in cream, sugar, and paprika. Return to a simmer and cook, stirring often, until sauce thickens, 10-15 minutes.
5. Heat vegetable oil in a separate skillet over medium heat. Add chicken and curry powder. Sear until lightly browned but still pink inside, about 3 minutes.
6. Transfer chicken and pan juices into sauce. Simmer until chicken is cooked through, about 30 minutes. Adjust sugar and salt to taste.
7. Serve over rice.

## Notes

- Reduce cayenne to 1/4 tsp or omit for kids; pull plain seared chicken before adding to sauce.
