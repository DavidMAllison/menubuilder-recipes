# Braised Chicken and Mushrooms over Polenta

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs boneless, skinless chicken thighs, trimmed
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1.75 teaspoons table salt, divided
- 6 slices bacon, cut into 1/2-inch pieces
- 8 oz cremini mushrooms, trimmed and quartered
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 (14.5 oz) can fire-roasted diced tomatoes
- 4 cups chicken broth, divided
- 2 teaspoons smoked paprika
- 1.5 cups milk
- 1 cup instant polenta
- Chopped fresh parsley for garnish (optional)

## Instructions

1. Pat chicken dry; sprinkle with pepper and 1 teaspoon salt. Cook bacon in a Dutch oven over medium-high heat until crispy, 5-7 minutes. Transfer bacon to a plate. Add chicken to fat in pot; cook until lightly browned, 3-5 minutes per side. Transfer to plate.
2. Add mushrooms, onion, and 1/4 teaspoon salt to pot; cook, stirring frequently, until browned, about 5 minutes. Add tomatoes, 1/2 cup broth, and paprika; bring to a boil, scraping up browned bits. Return chicken to pot; reduce heat to medium; cover; cook, stirring occasionally, until chicken registers 195°F, 10-12 minutes.
3. Transfer chicken to a cutting board and shred into bite-size pieces. Simmer sauce until slightly thickened, about 5 minutes. Stir chicken and bacon back into sauce.
4. Meanwhile, bring milk and remaining 3.5 cups broth to boil in a large saucepan. Whisk in polenta and remaining 1/2 teaspoon salt. Reduce heat to medium-low; cook until thickened, about 3 minutes.
5. Serve braised chicken and mushroom sauce over polenta. Garnish with parsley if desired.

## Notes

- Kids: Pull plain chicken before adding to sauce. Serve with plain polenta.
- Smoked paprika is key — do not substitute regular paprika.
