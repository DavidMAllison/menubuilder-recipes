# Herb-Marinated Lamb Rib Chops with Mint Sauce

## Ingredients

**For the Chops:**
- 8 lamb rib chops (about 1 inch thick, frenched)
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tablespoon fresh rosemary, finely chopped
- 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves
- 1 teaspoon fresh oregano, chopped
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Zest of 1 lemon
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

**For the Mint Sauce:**
- 1/2 cup fresh mint leaves, finely chopped
- 2 tablespoons white wine vinegar
- 1 teaspoon honey
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 tablespoon fresh dill, chopped (optional but recommended)

## Notes

- Buy double-cut frenched rib chops from Whole Foods for best results.
- Lamb is best served pink (medium-rare to medium).
- Kids: Season a few chops with just salt and pepper before applying the herb rub. Pull those out before plating -- serve adults with the mint sauce.

## Instructions

1. Combine garlic, rosemary, thyme, oregano, olive oil, and lemon zest in a small bowl. Rub all over lamb chops. Season generously with salt and pepper. Let sit at room temperature 30 minutes (or refrigerate up to 4 hours and bring to room temp before cooking).
2. Make mint sauce: Combine mint, vinegar, honey, salt, and olive oil in a small bowl. Stir well. Add dill if using. Taste and adjust seasoning. Set aside.
3. Heat a cast-iron or heavy-bottomed skillet over high heat for 3 minutes until very hot. Add a thin film of oil.
4. Sear chops in batches -- do not crowd the pan. Cook 2-3 minutes per side for medium-rare (130°F internal), or 3-4 minutes per side for medium (140°F).
5. Transfer chops to a plate and rest 5 minutes before serving. Serve with mint sauce on the side.
