# Tilapia with Lemon Cream Sauce

Source: Cooking con Claudia
Time: 25 minutes
Yield: 4 servings

## Ingredients

**Fish:**
- 4 skinless tilapia fillets
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 tablespoon butter
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh parsley for garnish

**Lemon Cream Sauce:**
- 2 tablespoons butter, divided
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 cup low-sodium chicken broth or dry white wine
- 1/4 cup heavy cream
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Set a skillet over medium heat; heat 1 tablespoon olive oil and 1 tablespoon butter.
2. Season tilapia fillets with salt and pepper on both sides. Add to skillet and cook 3-4 minutes per side until golden and opaque. Remove fish and set aside on a plate.
3. Add 1 tablespoon butter to the same skillet and melt over medium-high heat. Stir in garlic and cook 15 seconds until fragrant.
4. Add chicken broth (or white wine), increase heat, and let reduce by about 1/3, scraping up any browned bits.
5. Reduce heat to medium-low. Stir in heavy cream and lemon juice. Season with salt and pepper; simmer 2-3 minutes until slightly thickened.
6. Return tilapia to skillet and spoon sauce over the fish. Cook 1 minute to warm through.
7. Garnish with fresh parsley and serve immediately with extra sauce spooned over the top.

## Notes

- Use the same pan for the sauce to pick up all the flavorful bits from cooking the fish.
- If sauce is too thin, let it simmer longer. If too thick, add a splash more broth.
- Great served over rice, pasta, or with crusty bread.
