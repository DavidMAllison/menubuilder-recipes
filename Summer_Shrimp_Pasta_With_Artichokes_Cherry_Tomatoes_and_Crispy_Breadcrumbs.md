# Summer Shrimp Pasta With Artichokes, Cherry Tomatoes, and Crispy Breadcrumbs

**Time**: 45 minutes  
**Adapted from**: [Serious Eats](https://www.seriouseats.com/shrimp-pasta-with-artichokes-tomatoes-and-golden-breadcrumbs-recipe-12003835)  

## Ingredients

- 2 slices stale country-style bread (6 ounces, 170 g), preferably sourdough, torn into 1-inch pieces (see notes
- 2 tablespoons (28 g) unsalted butter
- 1/2 medium shallot (20 g), minced (about 2 tablespoons)
- 1/4 teaspoon Diamond Crystal kosher salt; for table salt, use half as much by volume
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 8 ounces (225 g) medium shrimp (41/50 count), peeled and deveined
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- 3 tablespoons (42 g) unsalted butter
- 1 tablespoon (15 ml) extra-virgin olive oil
- 10 1/2 ounces (300 g) fresh wide pasta (such as pappardelle or fettuccine) or 200 g dried pasta (see notes)
- 1 medium shallot (40 g), minced (about 1/4 cup)
- 2 medium cloves garlic, minced
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) dry white wine
- 6 ounces (170 g) cherry tomatoes, halved (about 1 cup)
- 2 ounces arugula (60 g; about 2 packed cups), roughly chopped
- 1 (14-ounce; 400 g) can artichoke hearts, drained, quartered, tough outer leaves removed is necessary
- 2 tablespoons finely chopped fresh soft herbs such as parsley or chives
- Fresh lemon juice, to taste

## Instructions

1. For the Golden Breadcrumbs : If using fresh or lightly stale bread, adjust oven rack to middle position, and preheat oven to 325°F (165°C). (If using fully stale and dried bread, skip baking step and move to step 2.) Arrange bread in single layer on rimmed baking sheet, and bake until completely dried, 20 to 30 minutes. Remove from oven, and allow to cool to room temperature, about 5 minutes. Transfer bread to food processor bowl (set aside but don&#39;t clean rimmed baking sheet), and pulse until reduced to small crumbs, taking care not to over-process into a fine powder, 8 to 10 pulses.
2. In a small oven-safe skillet, melt butter over medium heat. When butter begins to foam, add shallot and cook, stirring frequently until softened and fragrant. Add breadcrumbs and salt and pepper and cook, stirring occasionally, until lightly golden and crisp on the outside, 4 to 5 minutes.
3. For the pasta and sauce : Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Season generously with salt.
4. While water is coming to a boil, pat shrimp dry with paper towels and season lightly with salt and pepper on both sides.
5. In a large skillet, add butter and oil and melt over medium-high heat until butter begins to foam. Swirl to coat the pan, then, just before the butter begins to brown, add shrimp in a single layer. Cook undisturbed until lightly golden on the first side, about 1 to 2 minutes. Flip and cook until just opaque and barely cooked through, about 1 minute. Transfer to a plate and set aside.
6. Reduce heat to medium. Add shallots to remaining butter in skillet and cook, stirring often, until softened, about 2 minutes. Add garlic and cook until fragrant but not browned, about 30 seconds.
7. Add cherry tomatoes, tomato vines (if using), and artichokes, and cook until tomatoes begin to soften, about 2 minutes. Add white wine, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of skillet with a wooden spoon. Simmer until reduced by about half. Remove and discard the tomato vines, if using.
8. Meanwhile, cook pasta in pot of salted boiling water until al dente. Reserve about 1/2 cup (120 ml) of pasta cooking water, then drain the pasta.
9. Add the pasta to skillet with tomatoes and toss to coat, adding a splash of reserved pasta water as needed to loosen the sauce. Simmer, stirring and tossing often, until warmed through and sauce comes together, about 1 minute.
10. Return shrimp and any accumulated juices to pasta in skillet. Add arugula and toss until just wilted and the shrimp are warmed through, about 1 minute. Season with salt and pepper to taste, then finish with chopped fresh herbs and lemon juice to taste.
11. Divide the pasta between two bowls and top each with about 2 tablespoons of prepared golden breadcrumbs. Serve immediately.
