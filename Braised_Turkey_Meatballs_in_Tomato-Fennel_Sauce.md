# Braised Turkey Meatballs in Tomato-Fennel Sauce

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 lbs lean ground turkey (93% lean)
- 1/2 cup whole-wheat breadcrumbs
- 1 large egg
- 1/4 cup grated Parmesan, plus more for serving
- 2 garlic cloves, minced (1 for meatballs, 1 for sauce)
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 fennel bulb, cored and thinly sliced (fronds reserved)
- 1 small yellow onion, diced
- 1 (28-ounce) can crushed tomatoes
- 1/2 cup low-sodium chicken or vegetable broth
- 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes, plus more for serving (adults)
- Whole-wheat orzo or polenta, for serving

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, gently combine ground turkey, breadcrumbs, egg, Parmesan, 1 minced garlic clove, parsley, salt, and pepper. Form into 16-18 meatballs; do not overmix.
2. Heat olive oil in a large oven-safe Dutch oven over medium heat. Brown meatballs in batches, 2-3 minutes per side, and set aside (they don't need to cook through yet).
3. In the same pot, add sliced fennel and onion; cook until softened, about 6 minutes. Stir in remaining garlic and cook 1 minute.
4. Add crushed tomatoes, broth, oregano, and red pepper flakes (omit or use sparingly if serving straight to kids). Bring to a simmer.
5. Nestle meatballs back into the sauce. Cover and braise in a 300F oven for 2 1/2 hours, or transfer everything to a slow cooker and cook on low for 5-6 hours, until meatballs are very tender.
6. For kids: pull out a few plain meatballs and toss with buttered pasta, no sauce. For adults: serve meatballs and sauce over whole-wheat orzo or polenta, topped with extra red pepper flakes, Parmesan, and chopped fennel fronds.
